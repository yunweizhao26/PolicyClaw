Privacy Policy
IGNIS AMERICA,INC. fully understands the importance of protecting personal information, and safeguards the personal information based on this Privacy Policy.
1. Definition
For the purpose of this Privacy Policy, “personal information” means information about a living individual which can identify the specific individual by name, address, phone number, e-mail address or other description contained in such information (including such information as will allow easy reference to other information and will thereby enable the identification of the specific individual), as described in the personal information protection law.
2. Collection of personal information
We will collect the users’ personal information through legal and fair methods. When we collect personal information, we will notify or announce the purpose in advance.
3. Use of “cookies”
We may collect information on execution, history or other personal information from cookies or IP addresses. Users may reject our use of cookies by disabling cookies in the browser settings. However, when cookies are disabled, a part or whole of the service may be unavailable.
4. Purpose of use of personal information
Personal information we collect may be used for the following purposes: ? Provision of information on our operation of website, applications, services and systems, improvement of convenience, and other announcements ? After-sales service and response to complaints ? Development of new services and functions ? Provision of interest-based advertising in a way that cannot identify the user ? Research, statistics and analysis of marketing data ? Maintenance of systems and failure handling ? Identification, calculation/charging of fees and prevention of unfair use ? Response to inquiries
5. Provision of information
In accordance with the personal information protection law, we will not provide the collected personal information to any third party without the person’s prior consent. However, this may not apply in any of the following cases: ? The information acquired through aggregation and analysis is disclosed or provided to a third party such as a business partner, in a way that the user cannot be identified. ? The whole or a part of our operations is outsourced to a third party to achieve the purpose of use of personal information. ? The disclosure is permitted by law. ? The information is disclosed based on a court warrant, judicial decision or order, or laws and regulations. ? The information disclosure is necessary for us to exercise our right. ? The prosecutor, police or regulatory agency inquire about personal information in a lawful and orderly manner. ? It is necessary for the protection of the safety or property of our users or the public, and it is difficult to obtain the consent of the individual. ? The information is disclosed to an insurance company for insurance claim. ? The business related to the service is assigned or transferred due to corporate separation or other reason and all rights and obligations are legally taken over to the assignee.
6. Handling of personal information on the linked website
A link to external website may be provided in our website or e-mail newsletter. We do not control the personal information registered in such external website and we assume no responsibility for such information. When you register your personal information in an external website, please read and understand the privacy policy of the website.
7. Management system
In order to protect all personal information from illegal access, theft, loss, destruction, falsification, damage and leakage, we take all preventive and corrective measures and manage the information properly. Moreover, when we outsource the handling of personal information to a third party, we sign a contract of personal information protection and supervise the third party for proper information management.
8. Maintenance of accuracy
We make efforts to maintain the users’ personal information in accurate and latest condition, to the extent necessary for achievement of our purpose of use of the information.
9. Reference, etc.
When we are requested by a user for reference, correction, addition, deletion, suspension of use or deletion of the user’s personal information, we promptly respond to such request after confirming the identity of the requesting person.
10. Revision
The contents of this Privacy Policy may be revised without notice. When any changes are made, we notify the users of the changes by posting them on our website. The changes will become effective when the changed Privacy Policy is posted on the website.

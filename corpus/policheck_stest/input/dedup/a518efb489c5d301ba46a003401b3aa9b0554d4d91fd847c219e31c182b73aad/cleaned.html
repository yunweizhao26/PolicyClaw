Privacy and Data Policy For The Guides, The Guides Compendium, and The Guides Axiom

This privacy policy governs your use of the software application The Guides and/or The Guides Compendium and/or The Guides Axiom ("Application") for mobile devices that was created by RosiMosi LLC and Luke Lisi.

What information does the Application obtain and how is it used?

User Provided Information

The Application obtains the information you provide voluntarily to us via email or social networks. This information may include (a) your name; (b) email address; (c) information you provide us when you contact us for help.

Automatically Collected Information

The Application may collect anonymous usage information to help improve the quality of our services. This data is anonymous and does not contain any information which may be used to identify a specific user.

The Application is built with Corona SDK. Corona SDK may collect anonymous usage information that is fully GDPR compliant. This means it does not collect any Personal Data which can be used to identify a specific user. View the full Privacy Policy for Corona SDK here: https://coronalabs.com/privacy-policy-for-app-users/

Advertising

The Application offers optional advertisements in some regions using Appodeal. EU/GDPR users do not have the Appodeal SDK loaded and are not shown any advertising. View the full Privacy Policy for Appodeal here: https://www.appodeal.com/privacy-policy

Does the Application collect location information of the device?

This Application does not collect the location of your mobile device.

We may disclose User Provided and Automatically Collected Information:

· as required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena, or similar legal process;

· when we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, investigate fraud, or respond to a government request;

What are my opt-out rights?

You can stop all collection of information by the Application easily by uninstalling the Application. You may use the standard uninstall processes as may be available as part of your mobile device or via the mobile application marketplace or network.

Data Retention Policy, Managing Your Information

We will retain User Provided data for as long as you use the Application and for a reasonable time thereafter. We will retain Automatically Collected information for up to 24 months and thereafter may store it in aggregate. If you'd like us to delete User Provided Data that you have provided via the Application, please contact us at info@theguidesapp.com and we will respond in a reasonable time. Please note that some or all of the User Provided Data may be required in order for the Application to function properly.

Children

We do not use the Application to knowingly solicit data from or market to children under the age of 13. If a parent or guardian becomes aware that his or her child has provided us with information without their consent, he or she should contact us at info@theguidesapp.com. We will delete such information from our files within a reasonable time.

Security

We are concerned about safeguarding the confidentiality of your information. We provide physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards to protect information we process and maintain. For example, we limit access to this information to authorized employees and contractors who need to know that information in order to operate, develop or improve our Application. Please be aware that, although we endeavor provide reasonable security for information we process and maintain, no security system can prevent all potential security breaches.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time for any reason. We will notify you of any changes to our Privacy Policy by posting the new Privacy Policy . You are advised to consult this Privacy Policy regularly for any changes, as continued use is deemed approval of all changes.

Contact us

If you have any questions regarding privacy while using the Application, or have questions about our practices, please contact us via email at info@theguidesapp.com.

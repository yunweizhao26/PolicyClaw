Your privacy at a glance

We take privacy seriously. Please read our policy below and let us know if you have any questions or feedback.

Personal Information

We do not collect any personal information about the users of our apps.

We do not knowingly contact or collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us so we can promptly remove the information.

We do collect anonymous usage information about our website to help us improve our services and applications. Some anonymous usage information is collected in our Mobile Apps as well.

Some apps contain the option to contact us or register via mail. By sending us your contact info you agree to be contacted back with our response. In addition, we may rarely use your mail to send you updates and news of our products.

Links Within Apps

Some apps have external links such as to our website or to the App Store you purchased from to allow you to find out more about our company, our products and services. These links are only available from the title screen and/or in the “Parents”/”About” sections (which require a 3 second hold to get into in most apps).

Some of our free apps may contain in-app purchases. Apps with In-App purchases are stated in the description.

Data consent policy

All our apps are safe for use by both Kids and Parents.

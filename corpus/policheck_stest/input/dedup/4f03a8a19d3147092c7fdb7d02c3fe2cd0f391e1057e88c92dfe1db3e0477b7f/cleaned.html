What information do we collect and how do we use it?

1. What Information is Collected?

We collect information that is voluntarily provided by you through any of the methods listed in this Privacy Notice. We may collect information through your participation in a sweepstakes or other giveaway sponsored by us, to locate a salon, to provide “check-in” information electronically through the Salon Locator section of the Site or App, to take advantage of Site or App functionality, or to otherwise participate in activities or services generated on the Site or in the App.

Please note that Great Clips franchisees are responsible for all hiring and personnel matters at their individual salons and may collect additional job application and employment-related information not addressed in this Notice.

Information may also be collected when you download our App, respond to push notifications that were enabled by you, use our "check in" function to add your name to the waitlist at a local salon, and to opt-in to receive a text message or App push notification reminder. Such information may include your geolocation—to help you locate the nearest salon—your first and last name and phone number to be added to the salon waitlist (although such information is not required to access the services in the salon).

The Site may also collect information about technology interface preferences (such as the type of browser or device used, or the files requested) to improve the Site and better meet your needs. Great Clips works with third-party businesses to ensure that such information is complete and accurate.

The information we may collect includes, but may not be limited to:

  • Descriptions of how you like your hair cut, descriptions of your hair or other details relevant to your visit that are captured for use at a future visit (known as Clip Notes®);
  • A customer’s identity or contact information, such as their name, address, phone number, e-mail address;
  • A customer’s transaction history with Great Clips for products or services they have obtained from a salon;
  • Computer or mobile data or files, often called “cookies” or “SDKs,” which may be sent by a customer’s web browser or mobile app and stored on an individual device to allow our server to “recognize” the customer through the navigation of the Site or App;
  • A customer’s IP address to measure our website traffic and to help provide more personalized salon information and ensure a high level of site performance;
  • Geolocation information to help you locate a salon, navigational information (including browser type and version, IP address, information about your browsing activity online), and device information (including hardware mode, operating system and version, identification numbers assigned to your mobile device, mobile network, and website or app usage behavior);
  • Information regarding a customer’s unique application cookie to distinguish one application installation from another;
  • A customer’s mobile phone number to participate in promotional activities through the Site or at a local salon;
  • Information regarding the type of device used by customers to access our App, such as whether it is through an iPhone®, Android®, or online browser.
  • Personal, professional, and financial information and history collected in a franchise application;
  • We may use third-party analytics services to analyze Site metrics and performance, analyze our visitors’ preferences, address any technical issues, optimize our online services for a better user experience, and may serve personalized content to you through the use of some or all of the technologies described above, such as cookies, SDKs, and other similar technologies.
  • Our Site and App may include social media features which may collect information about you such as your IP address and which page you are visiting on our site. They may set a cookie or other tracking technologies to collect this information.  These features and widgets may be hosted by a third party, and your interactions with the features are governed by the privacy policies of the companies that provide them.

Franchisees may be collecting additional details and information not covered under this Privacy Notice, but as may be separately disclosed by the independently owned and operated salon. This could include things like video captured by security cameras or other marketing tools not currently in use by Great Clips, Inc.

Our Site and App are not designed for children, and we do not collect information online from any person we know is under the age of 18.

We may also collect information that is related to you but that does not personally identify you (“Non-personal Information”). Non-personal Information also includes information that could personally identify you in its original form but that we have modified (for instance, by aggregating, anonymizing, or de-identifying such information) in order to remove or hide any personally identifying information.

Great Clips, Inc. stores the information it collects on servers located in the United States.

“Sale” or “sell” means selling, renting, releasing, disclosing, disseminating, making available, transferring, or otherwise communicating orally, in writing, or by electronic or other means, a consumer’s Personal Information by the business to a third party for monetary or other valuable consideration.

“Share,” “shared,” or “sharing” means sharing, renting, releasing, disclosing, disseminating, making available, transferring, or otherwise communicating orally, in writing, or by electronic or other means, a consumer’s personal information by the business to a third party for cross-context behavioral advertising, whether or not for monetary or other valuable consideration, including transactions between a business and a third party for cross-context behavioral advertising for the benefit of a business in which no money is exchanged.

“Cross-context behavioral advertising” means the targeting of advertising to a consumer based on the consumer’s personal information obtained from the consumer’s activity across businesses, distinctly-branded websites, applications, or services, other than the business, distinctly-branded website, application, or service with which the consumer intentionally interacts.

"Targeted advertising" means displaying advertisements to a consumer where the advertisement is selected based on personal data obtained from that consumer's activities over time and across nonaffiliated websites or online applications to predict such consumer's preferences or interests. "Targeted advertising" does not include:

  1. Advertisements based on activities within a controller's own websites or online applications;

  3. Advertisements directed to a consumer in response to the consumer's request for information or feedback; or
  4. Processing personal data processed solely for measuring or reporting advertising performance, reach, or frequency.

“Service Provider” means a sole proprietorship, partnership, limited liability company, corporation, association, or other legal entity that is organized or operated for the profit or financial benefit of its shareholders or other owners, that processes information on behalf of a business and to which the business discloses a consumer’s Personal Information for a business purpose pursuant to a written contract, provided that the contract prohibits the entity receiving the information from retaining, using, or disclosing the personal information for any purpose other than for the specific purpose of performing the services specified in the contract for the business, or as otherwise permitted by this title, including retaining, using, or disclosing the personal information for a commercial purpose other than providing the services specified in the contract with the business.

“Third Party” means a person or entity who is not a business that collects Personal Information from consumers as defined in the Privacy Laws, and to whom the business discloses a consumer’s Personal Information for a business purpose pursuant to a written contract.

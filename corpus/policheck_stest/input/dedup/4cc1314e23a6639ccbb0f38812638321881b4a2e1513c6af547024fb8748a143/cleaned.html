Automatically Collected Information and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)

Our new Privacy Policy (July 24, 2018) reflects the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) standards and applies to all apps that we will release after that date. In short, we do not collect your personal data. We only collect anonymous statistical information, however our ads suppliers may use advertising ID of your device to provide more relevant ads based on your personal preferences. You can find links to our partners' privacy policies below:

  • Unity Ads & Analytics: https://unity3d.com/legal/gdpr
  • Facebook: https://www.facebook.com/about/privacy/
  • Google AdMob: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Chartboost: https://answers.chartboost.com/en-us/articles/115001489613

All of our apps includes a link to this Privacy Policy on their respective store pages for you to review before deciding to download. By downloading and using the app you are consenting to the data processing as described in this Privacy Policy. You can always withdraw your consent and also request your data within the app itself or disable the use of advertising ID globally from your device settings:

  • On iOS devices you can opt-out by choosing “limit ad tracking” option from privacy settings.
  • On Android devices you can opt-out by choosing “Opt-out of interest based ads” in settings.

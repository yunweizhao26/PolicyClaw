Do these two statements of a privacy policy conflict?

Each item shows two statements from the same privacy policy: statement 1 was read as permitting a practice and
statement 2 as prohibiting it. The policy text around each statement follows the item.

Consider a situation in which both statements could apply: the practice and the data they share, under
circumstances in which the conditions of both hold, as far as the text allows. Read the text and decide what the
policy says happens in that situation. Label the item with exactly one of:
- allow: the text permits the practice there, for example because statement 2 covers a narrower case that excludes
  this situation, or because statement 1 states an exception to statement 2 that holds here.
- deny: the text prohibits the practice there, for example because statement 2 carves a narrower case out of
  statement 1 (children, sensitive data, a user who opted out) or states a prohibition that statement 1's permission
  does not override.
- no_overlap: according to the text, the two statements can never apply to the same situation.
- contradiction: the text plainly says that the practice happens (or may happen) and that it does not, for the same
  situation, and nothing in the text says which statement governs. Read literally, the two statements cannot both be
  true there.
- unresolved: both statements apply to the situation and the text does not say which governs, but the conflict
  arises from general or vague wording rather than from two plainly opposite statements.
- different_practice: the statements are about different practices (different data, recipient, purpose, or party),
  so no situation falls under both.
- misread: statement 1 does not permit a practice, or statement 2 does not prohibit one (for example, it describes a
  user's right, a choice, a security measure, or someone else's conduct), so the question does not arise.

Also quote the words of the text that decide the label, and give a reason of at most 40 words.

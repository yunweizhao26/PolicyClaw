1. What Information is Collected?
We collect information that is voluntarily provided by you through any of the methods listed in this Privacy Notice. Descriptions of how you like your hair cut, descriptions of your hair or other details relevant to your visit that are captured for use at a future visit (known as Clip Notes®);
A customer’s identity or contact information, such as their name, address, phone number, e-mail address;
A customer’s transaction history with Great Clips for products or services they have obtained from a salon;
Computer or mobile data or files, often called “cookies” or “SDKs,” which may be sent by a customer’s web browser or mobile app and stored on an individual device to allow our server to “recognize” the customer through the navigation of the Site or App;
A customer’s IP address to measure our website traffic and to help provide more personalized salon information and ensure a high level of site performance;
Geolocation information to help you locate a salon, navigational information (including browser type and version, IP address, information about your browsing activity online), and device information (including hardware mode, operating system and version, identification numbers assigned to your mobile device, mobile network, and website or app usage behavior);
Information regarding a customer’s unique application cookie to distinguish one application installation from another;
A customer’s mobile phone number to participate in promotional activities through the Site or at a local salon;
Information regarding the type of device used by customers to access our App, such as whether it is through an iPhone®, Android®, or online browser.
Personal, professional, and financial information and history collected in a franchise application;
We may use third-party analytics services to analyze Site metrics and performance, analyze our visitors’ preferences, address any technical issues, optimize our online services for a better user experience, and may serve personalized content to you through the use of some or all of the technologies described above, such as cookies, SDKs, and other similar technologies.

Our Site and App are not designed for children, and we do not collect information online from any person we know is under the age of 18.

Great Clips, Inc. stores the information it collects on servers located in the United States.

“Cross-context behavioral advertising” means the targeting of advertising to a consumer based on the consumer’s personal information obtained from the consumer’s activity across businesses, distinctly-branded websites, applications, or services, other than the business, distinctly-branded website, application, or service with which the consumer intentionally interacts.

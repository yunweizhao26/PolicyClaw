Privacy Policy
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, secure, and/or disclose end users' ("you" or "your") or otherwise process your personal information when you use our Services.
1. What Data We Collect
Data you submit to us:
Authorization information (email, Facebook/Google ID), content you upload, and communications sent to customer support.
Data we collect automatically:
Device identifiers (device ID, advertising ID, IP address), location data, usage information, and data collected via cookies or mobile SDKs.
Data from partners:
Information from social networks if you link your account, and data from analytic/advertising partners.
2. Where Your Data Can Be Transferred
Our Services are global by nature and your data can therefore be transferred anywhere in the world. We ensure adequate safeguards are in place for international transfers, including compliance with the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or under standard contractual clauses deemed to provide sufficient safeguards for personal data by the European Commission.
3. Your Rights and Options
You can opt-out of interest-based advertising on mobile applications by checking the privacy settings of your device:
- For iOS devices: Open Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking and disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track".
- For Android devices: Open Settings > Google > Ads and enable "Opt out of Ads Personalization" or "Delete advertising ID".
You also have the right to request a copy of your data or request deletion of your data, subject to certain legal exceptions.
4. Age Limits
Our Services are intended for use only by those aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.

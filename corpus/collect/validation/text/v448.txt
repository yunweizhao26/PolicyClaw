Privacy Policy
Introduction
At Devee, we are committed to protecting the privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy explains the types of information we may collect from you or that you may provide when you use the Devee website and any of its services, and our practices for collecting, using, maintaining, protecting and disclosing that information.
Information Collection and Use
Devee may collect the following types of information from its users: name, email address, and profile image. This information is obtained through OAuth authentication with providers such as Google or Discord. The information collected by Devee will be used for the purpose of providing our services and improving the user experience.
Data Storage
We will retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you services. We may also retain and use your information as necessary to comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.
Data Sharing and Disclosure
Devee does not sell, rent or lease its user lists to third parties. We may share your information with third parties only in the ways that are described in this Privacy Policy. We may provide your information to third party service providers to assist us in providing our services to you. These service providers are contractually prohibited from using your information for any purpose other than to assist us in providing our services to you.
Data Removal
After account is deleted, all the associated data will be deleted.
Changes to Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our information practices. If we make any material changes, we will notify you by email (sent to the email address specified in your account) or by means of a notice on this website prior to the change becoming effective. We encourage you to periodically review this page for the latest information on our privacy practices.

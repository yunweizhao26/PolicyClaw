Protecting your privacy is important to us. We hope the following statement will help you understand how our app deals with the personally identifiable information ( PII ) you may occasionally provide to us via the Internet (the Google Play Platform). Generally, we do not collect any PII from you when you download our Android applications. Specifically, we do not require consumers to register before downloading the application, nor do we keep track of consumers visits to our application. We do not have a server to store such PII. Information We Collect Cookies (Webview browser cookies) When you visit the Forum service, we may send one or more cookies files with a small amount of data that is commonly used as an anonymous unique identifier to your mobile device. These cookies uniquely identify your device and allow the application to help you log in faster and enhance your navigation through the Forum. You can reset your web browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent. If you choose to refuse our cookies, you may not be able to use some portions of this Service. Analytics Google Analytics is a web analytics service offered by Google that tracks and reports website/app traffic. Google uses the data collected to track and monitor the use of our Service. This data is shared with other Google services. Google may use the collected data to contextualize and personalize the ads of its own advertising network. Advertising By using our Website/Apps, you may be offered advertising content by Google AdMob. Health Kit You will also have the option to permit us to import health data into the App from third-party services such as Google Fit. Permitting us to access third-party information can help you maximize your App experience, and we will handle any such third-party information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. Log File The only situation in which we may access your PII is when you personally decide to email us your feedback or provide us with a bug log report. The PII we may get from you in that situation is strictly limited to your name, email address, device information, location information, and your survey response only. How We Use Your Information In the above situation, we guarantee that your PII will only be used for contacting you and improving our services. We will never use such information (e.g., your name and email address) for any other purposes, such as to further market our products or to disclose your personal information to a third party for commercial gains. Data Sharing We may share your personal and sensitive data with the following entities for the purposes described: - Google Fit/Health Connect: To synchronize and import health data into the app. This data sharing is done in compliance with Health Connect policies. - Google Services: Data collected by Google Analytics and Google AdMob is shared with Google as part of their standard data handling and processing procedures. We do not share your data with any third parties beyond those mentioned above unless required by law or as part of a corporate transaction such as a merger or acquisition. Data Deletion You can delete your account and associated data at any time by following these steps: - Open the app and navigate to the settings menu. - Tap on Delete Cloud Account . - Confirm the deletion. This will delete all your data from our servers. Please note that any data synced to Google Fit via Health Connect will not be deleted. If you wish to delete data from Google Fit, you must do so through the Google Fit app or your Google account settings. Data Protection We take the protection of your data seriously and implement the following measures to ensure its safety: - Encryption: All sensitive data is encrypted both at rest and in transit using industry-standard encryption methods. - Access Controls: We restrict access to your data to authorized personnel only. - Data Minimization: We only collect data that is necessary for the operation of the app. Contact Us It should be noted that whether or not to send us your feedback or bug report is a completely voluntary initiative upon your own decision. If you have concerns about your PII being misused, or if you want further information about our privacy policy and what it means, please feel free to email us at support@jetfitness.app. We will endeavor to provide clear answers to your questions in a timely manner.

Privacy Policy
Oak School of Motoring Ltd take your privacy seriously and will only use your personal data to arrange your driving lessons with our Instructors, therefore providing you with the service you have requested from us. Your information will not be passed onto third parties.
Oak School of Motoring Ltd will retain your personal data only for as long as is necessary for the purpose set out in this privacy policy. Any personal information collected from you via telephone, email, text or social media will be deleted /removed /destroyed after 3 months if no longer necessary in regards to the purposes for which they were collected.
 
Telephone Contact
When you call we will collect information such as your name, address, availability for lessons, contact number and any other information required to place you with a Driving Instructor to suit your location and availability.
 
Email
Our website contains a contact form which asks you for information such as your name, address, phone number and any additional information you wish to supply that you feel we need to take into consideration when arranging your driving lessons.
 
Facebook
Any information sent to us via social media will be deleted after 3 months. Your information will not be shared with any other organisations unless we have your consent to do so.
 
Secure Socket Layer
Our website uses SSL encryption. The reason why SSL is used is to keep sensitive information sent across the Internet encrypted so that only the intended recipient can understand it. When an SSL certificate is used the information becomes unreadable to everyone except for the server you are sending the information to.

THE IGODISPATCH APPLICATION DOES NOT COLLECT OR STORE ANY DATA.
IT DOES NOT SEND YOUR CONTACT OR ANY OTHER PERSONAL INFORMATION.
We do not have any server database to store any data.
The only time we would see your data is when you send us an email containing a bug report. These reports are meant for debugging purposes only. Your emails are deleted once a bug has been resolved or when there is no further communication from your side. Before your emails are deleted, they are stored on Netfirms.com servers.
YOUR EMAIL ADDRESSES ARE NOT SHARED WITH ANY THIRD PARTY.
Should you have further questions regarding this Privacy Policy, contact us at igodispatch@igoapp.ca.

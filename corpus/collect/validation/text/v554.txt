Optimizing User Experience: By understanding where our users are, we can tailor certain aspects of the app experience to be more relevant and intuitive, thereby making the user's journey smoother and more enjoyable. Data Collection & Privacy: While we use GPS location services to refine the app experience, it's paramount for users to know that we prioritize their privacy. Location data is used in real-time and is neither stored on our servers nor shared with third parties without explicit user consent.

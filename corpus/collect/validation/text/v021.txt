Privacy Policy for Spirit of Birth Effective Date: 9/11/2025 Spirit of Birth is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy outlines how we handle user data within the Spirit of Birth app, an Indigenous pregnancy education guide designed to support users throughout their pregnancy journey. Information We Collect Spirit of Birth does not collect any personally identifiable information such as real names, email addresses, phone numbers, or location data. The app collects the following minimal, user-defined data solely for functionality purposes: - Username: A user-defined name (not a real name) used to identify your profile. - 4-digit PIN: Used to secure access to your profile across devices. - Pregnancy Due Date: Entered by the user to personalize pregnancy tracking features. This data is stored locally and/or securely synced across devices to allow users to return to their personalized experience. How We Use Your Information The collected data is used exclusively for the following purposes: - To enable login and bookmark functionality. - To personalize pregnancy tracking based on the due date provided. We do not use this data for analytics, advertising, or profiling. Data Sharing and Third Parties Spirit of Birth does not share, sell, or disclose any user data to third parties under any circumstances. Data Linked to You The data collected is not linked to your identity and is used solely for app functionality. User Privacy Choices Users may delete their stored data at any time by accessing the app settings. No external account or opt-out mechanism is required, as no personal data is collected or shared. Security We implement reasonable safeguards to protect user-defined data, including secure storage and access controls. However, as with any digital service, we encourage users to choose non-identifiable usernames and PINs.

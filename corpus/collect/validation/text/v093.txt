Options for Supported Living is committed to protecting the privacy of its supporters and stakeholders.
Options for Supported Living complies fully with the General Data Protection Regulations (GDPR) (EU) 2016/679
Options for Supported Living is registered as a Data Controller with the Information Commissioner’s Office and will only process data under the categories for which it is registered.
How do we receive personal information?
We collect information from you when you fill out the form on the get in touch page on the website.
When submitting your information, as appropriate, you are required to enter your: name and e-mail address. Alternately you may visit the website anonymously.
What might we use your personal information for?
We are required by GDPR (EU) 2016/679 to ensure that any personal information you supply to us is only used for the purposes for which you provided it and to make sure it is kept securely.
We will dispose of your data when it is legally or appropriate to do so.
Your information will not be sold, transferred or traded with any third party organisations.
Use of Cookies
We use cookies on our website for a variety of reasons which you find out about below. The cookies we use do not store personally identifiable information nor can they harm your computer. We want our website to be informative, and as user friendly as possible and cookies help us to achieve that goal.
You will only be agreeing to cookies if you click accept on the website. We appreciate some users may like more individual control over their visit to our website and can adjust their settings accordingly. To find out more about cookies, visit www.allaboutcookies.org
Changes to this privacy policy
We may amend this policy from time to time. We will state clearly on our website any substantial changes to the way in which we use your personal information.

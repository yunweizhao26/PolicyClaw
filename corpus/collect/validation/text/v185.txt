Privacy Policy
We at American Moving Partners. appreciate the vote of confidence, and we look forward to servicing your long distance move.  This privacy policy allows the visitors to our website to understand how the collected personal information will be utilized.  Please read the entire privacy policy prior to entering any personal information.  By utilizing the American Moving Partners website, you are accepting the business practices detailed in this privacy policy.  We strongly recommend that this privacy policy be reviewed thoroughly upon each visit to the site, to ensure complete understanding on how submitted information will be utilized.  Note:  The privacy policy set forth is ONLY pertains to the domain americanmovingpartners.com.  If any links on the website direct you to a third-party site, please review the privacy policy pertaining to that site.  We at American Moving Partners hold visitors’ privacy to the highest regard.  To better protect our visitors’ information, we supply this notice, which explains in detail our online information practices and the choices available to you when it comes to how information is collected and used.
This notices applies to all information collected and/or submitted on the American Moving Partners website and network websites.  All emails, form submissions, and any correspondence online or offline is solely used by American Moving Partners and network affiliations, with the intent to provide the service(s) for which you have requested.  For example, the information collected via a form submission will be shared with the auto transporter, mover, and any other related company required to make your long distance relocation as stress-free as possible.   Information will NOT be sent to anyone/business who is not considered the party that was intended to get the information.
To prevent unauthorized access, maintain data accuracy, and ensure the correct use of information, we have put into place appropriate physical, electronic, and managerial procedures to safeguard and secure the information we collect online.
We NEVER collect or maintain information on our website form those we actually know are under the age of 16, and no segment to the website or business structure is intended to attract an audience under the age of 16.
Visitors who choose to submit personally identifiable information , have the ability to correct any errors by sending an email to info@americanmovingpartners.com .  Please make sure to detail what the error(s) were and how to correct. Show you have any questions or concerns about this privacy policy, please contact us at info@americanmovingpartners.com .

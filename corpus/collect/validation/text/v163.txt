Privacy Policy
This Privacy Policy addresses natural persons and provides information on the collection, usage, transmission and protection of your personal data and rights in accordance with the laws of the Republic of Cyprus and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) of the European Union.
Your personal data includes any information that may lead, either directly or in combination with others, to your identification as a natural person. This includes data such as name, surname, tax identification number, social security number, physical and electronic addresses, home and mobile phone numbers, e-mail addresses and any other information that allows you to identify yourself under the provisions of the General Personal Data Protection Policy and the relevant laws of the Republic of Cyprus.

When subscribing to our newsletter service.
When you visit the company’s website. We use cookies to collect information about how you use our website. In this way we can adapt to your interests and needs by recording details that will allow us to recognize you the next time you visit our site.

Our communication – i.e. e- mail, or letter, or any other form of communication.
Information about the services, of your interest.
In addition we collect information with regard to the way you use our services, such us:
The personal data of co – beneficiaries that you have provided to us.
Information with regard to the way you use our site.

We process your personal data in accordance with the GDPR and national data protection legislation for one or more of the following purposes:
To contact business transactions and offer services under the contract we have with you.
To comply with a Legal or Regulatory Obligation.
To inform you about the new services of the company and our publications (unless you have chosen not to be contacted for marketing purposes), as well as to invite you to events.

Your personal data may be transmitted to third countries [i.e. in countries outside the European Economic Area]. Any such transfer shall take place only on the basis of an adequacy decision of EU or subject to the appropriate safeguards.

Personal data security
We recognize the importance of protecting your privacy and all of your personal data and we continuously improve the technical & organizational measures, to ensure the security of the processing and to avoid any unauthorized use, access, or disclosure of, loss, accidental or unlawful destruction.
Every employee of the company, who has access to the above information, uses your personal data only to serve the above purposes.

We share the information you give us only in the ways described in this Privacy Policy .
Your Rights
You may contact us to inform you about your personal data retained by us and the way of processing, to receive a copy of your personal data. To obtain a relevant copy, you can send us appropriate instructions, signed with the authentication of your signature by a competent authority.
If you think that your personal data are incomplete or inaccurate, please contact us to proceed with the appropriate rectification.
You may request the deletion of your personal data, if you believe that there is no legitimate reason to continue processing it.
You may object to the processing of your personal data [known as the “right to object”] on grounds relating to your particular situation. If you file such a relevant request to us, we will no longer process your personal data, unless we demonstrate compelling and legitimate grounds for the processing that override your interests, rights and freedoms, or for the establishment, exercise or support of legal claims.
You also have the right to oppose to the processing of your personal data for marketing purposes or you may choose to oppose to certain ways of communication, i.e. e-mail, phone, etc.
If you don’t wish to receive e-mail for marketing purposes, you can unsubscribe from our list.
You may request a copy of your personal data in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format, and to transmit those data to another controller. [known as data portability rights]
In order to exercise any of your rights or if you have any other questions regarding the process of your personal data by us, you may contact the company or send us appropriate instructions, signed with the authentication of your signature by a competent authority to 12 Rodou, 1st floor, 1086 Nicosia, Cyprus, e-mail: info@evangelouloizou.com
The company will respond to your request within one month following its receipt. If it is not possible for us to satisfy your request within the period of one month, we will inform you of the reasons for the delay.
The company shares your concern about your personal data. For any information you can contact the company at  info@evangelouloizou.com

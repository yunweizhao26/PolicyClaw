Privacy statement
The protection of privacy and the security of personal data are very important to Cordemeyer & Slager Advocaten. We handle personal data in accordance with the provisions of the General Data Protection Regulation and the provisions of other applicable laws, regulations and rules.
Purposes
If you send Cordemeyer & Slager Advocaten an e-mail, we will use your personal data for the purpose of responding to your questions or comments. If you sign up to receive newsletters we will use your personal data to send you the newsletters requested. In the context of marketing, we may also send you newsletters or invitations on our own initiative. In addition, we may process personal data for the purpose of complying with a statutory requirement or, if applicable, in connection with the provision of our services.
Security
Cordemeyer & Slager Advocaten secures your personal data through multiple technical and organizational measures, including firewalls, secure servers and data processing agreements with third parties.
Cookies
Our website uses cookies. Cookies are text files that are stored on your computer or mobile devices when you visit the website. We use cookies that are necessary in order to communicate over the internet or in order to provide a requested service (functional cookies). We also place functional cookies for the security of the website and the user. These are session cookies, which means that they are temporary and will be removed from your computer after you close the internet browser following your visit to the website.
In addition we use Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google Inc. In this regard, our website will be analyzed in order to, for example, count the number of visitors to the site and see which pages are visited the most. The information obtained, including the IP address of your device, is stored by Google in the United States.  Click here to learn more about (the privacy statement of) Google Analytics .
On the website it is also possible to share certain pages and articles via social media buttons on various social media networks: Facebook, Twitter, Google+, Pinterest and LinkedIn. If you click on these buttons, your personal data will be processed by the relevant social media network. Read the privacy statements of Facebook, Twitter, Google+, Pinterest and LinkedIn to see how these networks process your personal data.
 
Your rights
You have the right, at any time, to object to the use of your personal data for direct marketing purposes and/or the receipt of specific or all types of marketing information. You also have the right to request Cordemeyer & Slager Advocaten to inform you of the personal data it holds relating to you and, to the extent applicable, to request that such data be corrected, supplemented, deleted or blocked. Objections and requests can be sent to Cordemeyer & Slager Advocaten N.V., Wilsonsplein 15, 2011 VG, Haarlem, of b.a.m.cordemeyer@cordemeyerslager.nl .

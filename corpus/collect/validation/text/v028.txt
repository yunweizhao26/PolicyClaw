Privacy Policy
At Sample Company, we respect our customers and understand that you are concerned about privacy so we have instituted policies intended                  to insure your personal information is handled safely and responsibly. As we continue to develop our web site and take advantage                  of new technologies to improve services for our customers, this policy will likely change.  Please refer to this page to view our current policy.
Visitors
Our web server automatically collects a visitor's domain name/IP address for internal review and then deletes the information.                  We do not collect or store E-Mail addresses of unregistered visitors to our site.
Registered Accounts
When you register/order from Sample Company, we collect your name, address, E-Mail address, ship-to information and payment information.                  This information will be automatically saved in your account set-up.
Sample Company is committed to doing its best to maintain the security of information collected on our site. In order to try to prevent                  any unauthorized access, maintain data accuracy and ensure correct use of information, we have put in place the appropriate physical,                  electronic and managerial procedures to safeguard and secure the information we collect on line. To the extent customer information is                  maintained, it is located on a secured server behind a firewall and is not directly connected to the Internet. The firewall meets                  industry standards. For added protection we subscribe to both VeriSign and McAfee Secure.
In order to serve you better, we may from time to time send out promotional catalogs, flyers and E-Mails to inform you about                  products or special offers. You may unsubscribe at anytime by clicking Contact Us and send us an E-Mail to UNSUBSCRIBE your account or call us and advise you wish to be removed from our mailing list. Telephone numbers provided online will be used for order and delivery questions only.

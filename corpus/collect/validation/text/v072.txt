Privacy Policy
This privacy policy is intended to provide information on the processing of personal data on our website. Personal data means any information that affects a user and that can be identified, directly or indirectly, by that person. By using the site, the user is requested to agree on our privacy policy when we are collecting his or her personal data.
Through our website we only collect personal data that the user sends to us and for which the user has given his or her own consent. Personal data is collected only insofar as it is necessary for the purposes of processing personal data. The purpose of processing personal data is to enable the implementation and development of our services as well as customer communication.
The collection of personal data will utilize the cookies used on our website, and the application and contact forms. The use of cookies is described in more detail in the cookie policy. Cookies that are allowed on a user’s browser and on a website can be combined with the information entered on the application and contact forms to improve service quality and communication.

Shortn Privacy Policy Last Updated: June 26, 2025 We believe it is important for you to understand what information we gather from you, how it is obtained, and the ways in which you can manage it. Our commitment is to maintain transparency in our data collection practices and to equip you with the knowledge needed to make informed decisions regarding your personal data. This commitment forms the foundation of our privacy policy. Please be aware of several key considerations when accessing our website and mobile applications. Firstly, the Shortn website and its mobile applications are accessible to the public and may be indexed by search engines, allowing global users and visitors to view posted content. Consequently, any content you choose to upload or share on the Shortn platform may be visible to third parties. Secondly, as content posted on the Shortn platform is associated with your user account, anonymity is not guaranteed. If preserving your identity is a priority, we advise using a pseudonym when interacting with the platform. While navigating or interacting with the Shortn website and applications even without logging in we may collect basic information such as the type of device you are using, your general location, and your IP address. Should you decide to create an account, you may voluntarily provide us with further personal data, as detailed in the subsequent sections. We also offer settings that allow you to control the extent of data collection and how that data is utilized. Your activity on the Shortn platform, including but not limited to posts, comments, likes, shares, and general usage of the mobile app, helps us to tailor content to your interests and improve your overall experience. This data enables us to provide more relevant material and to refine the features and functionality of our services. Certain terms used throughout this Privacy Policy are defined for clarity and legal precision. These defined terms are capitalized and their respective meanings are set forth in the Definitions section located at the end of this document. If you have any inquiries about this privacy policy, the data we collect, how it is used, or how to manage your privacy preferences, feel free to reach out to us at any time via support@shortn.video. 1. While you may use many features of the Shortn website and mobile applications without creating an account (such as browsing posts), certain services require registration. If you choose to register, you will need to supply certain personal details, including but not limited to your username, password, email address, telephone number, age, gender, and, if applicable, your contacts from third-party services (e.g., Facebook or your mobile device s address book). 1.1.2. Public Information. The majority of content on the Shortn platform is publicly accessible. Public elements include most posts, the chat functionality, likes, and comments. Such public content may be used to fulfil service requests, deliver personalized recommendations, maintain communication records, and support legal compliance and defence. 1.1.4. Private Communication Data. We may utilize personal contact details voluntarily shared with us (such as email, phone number, social media account identifiers, or contacts lists) for purposes including identity verification, communication, connecting users, facilitating invitations, managing our correspondence, and addressing legal claims. In addition, we may process private communications and related metadata to detect and mitigate harmful content, enforce our terms of service, and respond to legal obligations or law enforcement. 1.1.5. Sensitive Information. We may access and use sensitive data, including the content of messages or texts, to enable communication, connect you with others, respond to legal inquiries, and provide geographically tailored content. 1.2. We may also collect certain categories of Personal Data from you automatically while you use the Shortn website or mobile applications. This includes the following types of information: 1.2.1. Location Data. We may gather location-related information by processing your IP address and device settings. This information may be utilized to deliver location-specific services, adjust our offerings based on your geographic region, respond to legal or law enforcement requests, or display content relevant to your current location. 1.2.2. Technical Data. Technical details related to your interaction with the Shortn platform may also be collected. This could include your IP address, device identifiers, mobile application bundle ID, advertising or creative IDs, username on Shortn, phone number, pages you visit, the referring website, device model, mobile network provider, search queries, cookie identifiers, operating system, geolocation data, and your time zone. Such data may be analyzed to understand your usage patterns, detect advertising fraud or invalid impressions, maintain the security and integrity of our services, allocate technical resources appropriately, and manage system load on our servers. 1.2.3. Interaction and Usage Data. When engaging with the Shortn website or mobile applications, we may collect data regarding your usage and interactions. This may include screen recordings or logs of how you interact with the app or website. Such information assists us in enhancing our services, refining user interfaces, and remotely adjusting mobile application features to improve usability. 1.2.4. Marketing Data. Since the Shortn platform is offered as a free service, advertising serves as a key revenue source. Accordingly, we may collect information such as your IP address, app bundle ID, advertising and creative IDs, user ID on Shortn, device identifiers, location data, phone numbers, visited pages, referrer pages, device model, search terms, cookies, and unique advertising identifiers from Apple or Google. We use this data to present advertisements, assess ad performance, detect fraudulent ad traffic, and deliver ads that align with your interests. Your Personal Data may be transmitted off-device to authorized third-party advertisers to facilitate targeted advertising. 1.3. Use of Cookies. We may also obtain Personal Data through the deployment of cookies and related tracking technologies. These tools assist us in gaining insight into how you interact with the Shortn website and mobile applications, in delivering targeted advertising, and in identifying usage patterns that can inform improvements to our services and offerings. Additionally, they enable us to tailor promotional content provided by third-party advertisers to your interests. 1.4. Cookies may be either session-based or persistent. Session cookies are temporary and reside on your device only during your active session; they are automatically deleted once you close your browser. In contrast, persistent cookies remain stored on your computer or mobile device even after your browsing session has ended. These cookies can be accessed and read by the originating website upon your return. 1.5. We may utilize persistent cookies in conjunction with analytics tools such as Google Analytics or other analytics service providers, which are designed to analyze traffic sources and user behavior on the Shortn platform. Furthermore, we may periodically receive Personal Data from third parties, including analytics providers or advertising networks like Google, Facebook, or Apple. 1.6.

Information We Collect
Right Spin Health Products International, Ltd collects both personal (that which is associated with your name) and non-personal information from you.  Like most websites, Right Spin Health Products International, Ltd uses cookies, web beacons, IP address and geo-location. These other forms of information collection are explained below.
 
How Is Information Collected?
Information is collected with your permission. Right Spin Health Products International, Ltd collects customer information when we process your order or you registered an account, like your name, credit or debit card information, phone and mobile number, mailing address, e-mail address, and billing address. If you want to sign up for the Right Spin Health Products International, Ltd mailing list we will ask for your name, e-mail, mobile number, and/or mailing address. We also collect the e-mail addresses of people who e-mail us.
 
Social Media.  If you use Facebook, Twitter, or another social media site to sign up, Right Spin Health Products International, Ltd may collect the information that you submit through that site.  When you “like” Right Spin Health Products International, Ltd on Facebook or other social media sites, we will also collect your information from Facebook, such as your name and e-mail address.  Remember that if you submit information through social media sites such as Facebook, your information is governed by their privacy policies and information you share might be seen by the general public. Right Spin Health Products International, Ltd shall have no liability or responsibility for the privacy practices or other actions of any third party service or other website enabled within our service.  
 
Cookies.  Each time you visit our website, our web server automatically recognizes your domain name and what type of browser you are using, by using “cookies,” which are small text files on your computer that identify you as a unique user.  These help us measure your activity and personalize and improve your experience at Right Spin Health Products International, Ltd.  If you do not want us to use cookies, you can set your browser to delete them, or you can manually delete the cookies.  If you do this, you might have trouble accessing and using some of Right Spin Health Products International, Ltd’s web site pages. You may also manually delete these cookies after you have finished your browsing or shopping experience. We do not and are unable to use cookies to gather other information that may be stored on your hard drive. 
 
Web Beacons.  Right Spin Health Products International, Ltd also uses web beacons, which let us count and recognize users who visit our website.  This lets us see if you respond to e-mails that we sent to you by clicking on a link in the e-mail, so we can know whether the e-mails we send are effective and a valuable use of your time as well.  If you don’t want web beacons in the e-mail, you can turn off HTML display of the e-mail.
 
IP Address. When you visit our site, Right Spin Health Products International, Ltd also collects and saves the Internet Protocol address of the device you are using, like whether you are using a personal computer or a mobile device. 
 
Geo-location.  Geo-location technology refers to technology that lets us determine your location.  We may ask you to manually provide precise location information on our website, or to enable your mobile device to send us precise location information.  We may also automatically use information from your web server or computer to determine general location information, such as the city in which you live.
 
What We Do With Your Information
Right Spin Health Products International, Ltd does not rent, sell, trade or disclose your personal information to third parties without your prior consent except as specified in this Privacy Policy. When you provide us with your personal information you are automatically registered on our mailing list, and we may send you e-mails and messages announcing product information, promotional materials, specials or updates to our website we believe to be of interest to you as well as notices of change in policies.  If you don’t want to get anymore e-mails or mailings you may opt out at any time. To opt out or modify your preferences either (1) access the unsubscribe link found on an email you receive from Right Spin Health Products International, Ltd, (2) send an email to support@rightspinhealth.co.nz with the word UNSUBSCRIBE followed by your email address in the subject of the email (example: UNSUBSCRIBE me@emailaddress.com ), or (3) log into MY ACCOUNT on the Right Spin Health Products International, Ltd website and change your personal preferences.

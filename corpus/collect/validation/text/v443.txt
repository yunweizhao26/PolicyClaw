Cookies, or comparable techniques that we use
Maaskade Bevrachters B.V. uses functional, analytical and tracking cookies. A cookie is a small text file that is stored in the browser of your computer, tablet or smartphone during the first visit to this website. Maaskade Bevrachters B.V. uses cookies with a purely technical functionality. They ensure that the website works properly and that for instance your preferred settings are remembered. These cookies are also used to allow the website to operate properly and to be able to optimize it. In addition we install cookies that keep track of your surfing behavior so that we can offer custom-made content and advertisements.
During your first visit to our website we already informed you about these cookies and we asked your permission for storing them. You can be deregistered for cookies by setting your Internet browser in such a way that it no longer stores cookies. Besides you can also use the settings of your browser in such a way that it removes all information that has been stored earlier. For an explanation see:  veiliginternetten.nl
Inspecting, modifying or removing data
You are entitled to inspect, correct or remove your personal data. Besides you are entitled to withdraw any permission for data processing or to object to the processing of your personal data by Maaskade Bevrachters B.V. and you are entitled to data transferability. That means that you can submit a request to us to send the personal data that we possess from you to you or to another organization mentioned by you. 
You may send a request for inspection, correction, removal, data transfer of your personal data or a request for withdrawal of your permission or objection to the processing of your personal data to post@www.www.www.www.www.www.maaskade.nl
To be certain that the request for inspection has been made by you, we shall ask you to send a copy of your proof of identity with the request. In this copy you should black out your passport photo, MRZ (machine readable zone, the strip with numbers at the bottom of the passport), passport number and Citizen Service Number (BSN). This to protect your privacy. We shall react to your request as soon as possible but within four weeks.
Maaskade Bevrachters B.V. also wishes to point out to you that you have the possibility of submitting a complaint to the national supervisor, the Data Protection Authority. This can be done via the following link:  autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl

Privacy Policy and Terms of Use
References made to "we, us, and our" in this Privacy Policy and Terms of Use relate to Unicus Resources.
About Us
Unicus Resources are feedback, coaching and survey specialists. Established in 2006, we have a strong track-record of providing 360° and other feedback surveys to UK and international clients in the public, private and voluntary sectors. We have users in over 50 countries.
In addition to directly providing these services to our own clients, we work in partnership with other developmental consultancy organisations including ELA Development, Advance Consulting, etc. The websites either use our URL address or one specifically designed for our clients/partner organisations. Our website is hosted in the UK and complies with UK Data Protection legislation.
Information Storage
Unicus Resources take all reasonable steps to ensure that your data is collected and stored securely, in accordance with this Privacy Policy. We collect and store enough information to provide clients with 360 reports, 180 reports and Evaluation reports. We do not store information that is irrelevant to our purpose. We keep data for 6 months following cessation of our contract and this is only accessed if individuals request copies of their reports to track their progress.
360° & 180° Participant data collection is confined to:
Name
Email addresses of chosen respondents
Ratings and feedback given from Participants chosen Respondents
360° & 180° Respondents' data collection is confined to:
Name
Ratings and feedback given on a particular Participant
The feedback given is anonymous and not identified, unless it is that of a Line Manager. However, if the respondent chooses to provide details that will identify them to the Participant, Unicus Resources cannot be held responsible. If you do not want us to use your data in this way, please advise us of this at any time, by email or phone.
All information provided by you is stored on our secure servers. Where we have given you (or where you have chosen) a password that enables you to access certain parts of our site, you are responsible for keeping this password confidential. You are strongly advised not to share passwords with another person(s). Unfortunately, the transmission of information via the internet is not completely secure. Although we will do our best to protect your personal data, we cannot guarantee the security of data from you that is transmitted to our site; any transmission is at the sender's own risk. Once we have received data from any of these sources, we will use strict procedures and security features to ensure that the data is secure and safe from unauthorised access.
Security
Unicus Resources take the utmost care to ensure that our systems are secure at all times. Where appropriate, we employ firewalls, passwords, personal identification numbers and access control methods to control access to data stored on our systems.
Virus Protection
Unicus Resources take precautions to minimise the risk from software viruses, however, you should carry out your own virus checks before opening any attachments.

Unicus Resources will only contact you if there is a need to relating to an on-going survey or for research purposes. We do not pass on details to marketing companies.

The Data Protection Act 1998 gives you the right to access information held about you. Your right of access can be exercised in accordance with the Act. Any access request may be subject to a fee of GBP 10 to meet our costs in providing you with details of the information we hold about you.

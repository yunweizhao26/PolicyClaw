Keeping Your Information Private
We will never sell or disclose your personal information to anyone – guaranteed. We hate spam just as much as you, and we take extra measures to keep your information private & secure. Your personal information is stored using a secure database that uses a industry standard firewall, and SSL (Secure Socket Layer) technology.
Use of Website Cookies
Like many websites, TahoeAccommodations.com stores little files on your computers browser that are called “cookies”. Cookies are required for some of the advanced features on the website such as remembering your favorite properties, and remembering your arrival & departure dates (which makes checking property rates easier). Cookies also help us track the most popular areas of our website, and we base many of our improvements from the analytics we gather from the use of cookies.

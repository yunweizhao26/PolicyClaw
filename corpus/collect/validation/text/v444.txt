Privacy Policy
Your privacy and security is important to us. We understand you may be concerned about how we may use your personal information collected from our website.
We will not sell or disclose Personal Information such as your name, address, e-mail address and telephone number that we gather on our website to any third parties, except in cases where we have a legal obligation to do so.
We collect your Personal Information only to complete online transactions, compile our membership database, and to reply to your inquiries. Birthdates are requested only to ascertain membership types.
We do not handle or store credit card numbers for any of our transactions. All our transaction data is handled by PayPal®, you can review PayPal® policies on their website .
When you visit our website, our server logs your IP address only so that we can determine broad demographic information and to help identify any problems that may arise with our server.
Our organization may periodically provide you with additional information and updates. These emails are sent to broadcast lists which are double opt-in and We always provide you with the opportunity to opt-out of receiving further communications from us. You can unsubscribe from our mailing list at any time by clicking the UNSUBSCRIBE link at the bottom of any of our emails.
You may direct any inquiries with respect to our privacy policy by contacting the communications director .
Our privacy policy may be updated at any time so please check back often.

Privacy Policy
General Policy This overall privacy statement discloses the privacy practices for the entire Setton International Foods Inc. ("Setton") Web site. At Setton, we respect your privacy and have taken strong steps to protect your privacy online as outlined in this document.
Information Gathering
To help us serve you better Setton may ask for personal information including your name, address, e-mail address and telephone number. Setton automatically receives and records information on our server logs from your browser including your Internet Protocol (IP) address, and the page you requested, so we can better understand how you use our Web site.
Cookies
To help you get the most from your experience at Setton, we may in the future use a feature on your Internet browser called a "cookie." Cookies are small text files sent to and stored on users' computers that allow web servers to recognize repeat users, facilitate users' access to sites, and allow sites to compile aggregate data that will allow content improvements. Cookies do not damage users' computers or files. With most Internet browser programs, you have the option of setting your browser to reject cookies.
Information Disclosure
We do not disclose information about your individual visits to Setton, or personal information that you may provide such as your name, address, e-mail address, telephone number, personal profile, etc., to any outside parties, except when we believe it is required by law or to protect Setton, our users or others. But, we may record and share aggregated information with our partners.
We employ or may employ in the future other companies and individuals to perform functions on our behalf. Examples may include hosting our web servers, analyzing data, providing marketing assistance, and providing other services. These other companies may need to have access to your personal information as necessary to perform their functions, but they may not share that information with any third party.
Setton may from time to time aggregate certain information about the demographics or use of the Web site. Such information is in aggregate and not at the personal level. In addition to material on the Web site, Setton may also elect in the future to email newsletter information to users that have opted in to receive e-mail.
Security
Setton has security measures in place to protect the accidental loss from unauthorized access, use, or alteration of the information under our control. In the interest of security, we do not reveal specific methods used to secure information on our web server. All personal information is protected.
Updating the Privacy Statement
If we decide to update our privacy statement at Setton, we will post those changes here so that you will always know what information we gather, how we might use that information, and to whom we will disclose it.

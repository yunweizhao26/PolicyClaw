7. Cookies
7.1. Like many other websites, ours uses Cookies.  Cookies are small pieces of information sent by an organisation to your computer or device and stored on your computer or device to allow a website to recognise you when you visit.  They help us collect statistical data about your browsing actions and patterns but should not contain personal data (other than the cookie itself, which is defined as personal data under GDPR).
7.2. All cookies used by and on our website are using in accordance with the current cookie law.
7.3. Before cookies are placed on your computer or device you will be shown a popup requesting your consent to set such cookies as detailed below.  By giving us your consent, you are allowing us to provide the best possible experience of our website.  You may refuse consent to cookies; however certain parts of our website may then not function correctly.  You can alter your browser settings to refuse all cookies.
7.4. There are four types of cookies:
7.4.1. Strictly Necessary Cookies –These are essential to make a website work and provide features you’ve asked for.  Generally, these are used to provide shopping baskets and similar. Without these cookies the website may not work as intended.
7.4.2. Performance Cookies – These collect anonymous information about users for the purposes of tracking the performance of a website. Common uses include well known analytics tools such as Google Analytics.
7.4.3. Targeting / Advertising Cookies – Similar to performance cookies, however these are used to track users’ behaviour and that information is then used on a “per user” basis to advertise products / services on the basis of the behavioural information collected.
7.4.4. Functionality Cookies – These are used to automatically remember choices that users have made in order improve their experience on the website; for example selecting desired layout or language.

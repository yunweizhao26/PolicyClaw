Navigation data/cookies
What are cookies?
Cookies are short strings of text (letters and/or numbers) that allow the web server to store on the client (the browser, eg. Internet Explorer, Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Opera …) information that can be reused during the same visit on the website (session cookies) or later, even after several days (persistent cookies). Cookies are stored, according to user’s preferences, from the single browser on the specific device used for the navigation (computer, tablet, smartphone). A cookie should not retrieve any other data from the User’s hard disk nor transmit computer viruses or capture email addresses. Each cookie is unique to the User’s web browser. Some of the functions of cookies may be delegated to other similar technologies such as web beacons, transparent GIF and all forms of local storage introduced with HTML, that can be used to achieve information on user’s behavior and on the use of services. The word ‘cookie’ refers to cookies and all the similar technologies Based on the features and on the use of the cookies, there are different types of cookies.
Technical cookies strictly necessary. These cookies are essential for the proper functioning of a website and are used to manage different services related to the same sites (such as a login or access to private functions ).The duration of these cookies is strictly limited to the working session, or it is possible to consider a longer permanence in order to recall User’s choices. Disassembling technical cookies may jeopardize the navigation and the use of the website.
Analytics cookies and performance cookies. These cookies are used to anonymously collect and analyze the traffic and the use of a website. These cookies, without identifying the User, allow to detect when the same User returns to connect at different times. They also allow to supervise the system and improve its performance and usability. The deactivation of these cookies can be executed without any loss of functionality.
Profiling cookies (not operative on this website). These cookies are permanent cookies used to identify (anonymously and not) the preferences of the User and improve his navigation experience.
G.B.T. s.n.c. has taken note of the Order issued by the Data Protection Authority n. 229/2014 “Locating simplified way for the disclosure and acquisition of consent for the use of cookies – May 8, 2014 (Published in the Italian Official Gazette no. 126 of June 3, 2014)”, and the subsequent actions of the Authority about “cookies”.
In accordance with the above mentioned documents by the data protection Authority, G.B.T. s.n.c. wish to advice the Users of its website that only technical cookies are put in work. As explained above, these cookies are used to navigate or provide a service requested by the User. They are not used for further purposes (in particular, profiling for commercial or marketing purposes) and normally they are directly set up by the owner of the website. Without the use of these cookies, some operations may not be made or would be more difficult and less secure. In addiction, this website uses “analytics cookies”, that can be assimilated to the technical cookies since they are only used or the purpose to optimize the website, only by the owner of the site, that may collect aggregate information on the number of users and how they visit the site.

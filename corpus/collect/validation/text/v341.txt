PROTECTION All our apps are designed for maximum safety. Including young children with parent supervision. We make the kids-focused apps and games available via app stores. We take privacy very seriously and this policy is designed to tell you what information we collect and how we use it. This Privacy Policy covers all the applications developed in our studio and is intended to protect the user's right to confidentiality. 1. Collection of information We do not collect any personal information about you. We also do not collect any analytics about the users and their behavior in our apps. Our apps contain only education, fun and the joy of exploring. 2. Advertising All the apps are completely free of ads. You can find mentions of our other apps, only if it is related to the current content or they are part of the bundle. These links are behind the parental gate, sufficiently protected against the opening. For example through difficult questions that only adults can solve. 3. COPPA We comply with the Children s Online Privacy Protection Act. We do not knowingly collect personal information on children under the age of 13. We therefore will not distribute your personal data to outside parties without your consent. 4. Payments Our applications will never contain any in-app purchases. There will be never any payments in the app for the content. Payments for the applications in the store are managed directly through Apple for iOS devices, or through Google for Android devices. Only these companies manage payment data. We do not have access to them and only receive anonymized receipts. 5. Changes We may update this document from time to time. We recommend you periodically check this page to be aware of the changes. 6. Contacts You can contact us at any time on any matter through the related contacts at the foot of this page. Our principles: SAFETY Apps will never have in-app purchases and advertisements. They are for children. Purchasing is one-time. UNIVERSAL The installation is for both tablet and phone simultaneously. You always have both versions available with the purchase. AVAILABILITY We aim to keep prices under $5. For more demanding ones, months of work are often required.

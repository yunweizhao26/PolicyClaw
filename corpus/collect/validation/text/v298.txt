Our Commitment to Privacy
Your privacy is important to Maco Companies. This privacy policy applies to all information collected by or submitted to Maco Companies.
The Information We Collect
All email programs include a return address. When you contact Maco Companies via email it will automatically include your return address along with the message you are sending.
How We Use Information
We use return email addresses to answer the email we receive. Once your message has been replied to, usually within 24 hours of its receipt, your email address is deleted from our files.
Our commitment to Security
Your privacy is of great importance to Maco Companies. To ensure the appropriate use of information given to us, we have appropriate procedures (physical and managerial) in place to safeguard and secure information given to us.
Our Commitment to Children’s Privacy
Protecting the privacy of the very young is especially important and for that reason, no part of our website is structured to attract children. No information is sought or collected from anyone we know is underage.
How to Correct Your Information

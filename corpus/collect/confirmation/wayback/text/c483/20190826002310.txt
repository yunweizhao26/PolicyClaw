Protection of your privacy in the processing of personal data is important to us. When you visit our website, our web server stores the IP of your internet service provider, the website from which you visit us, the websites which you visit through us as well as the date and duration of your visit, all by default. This information is necessary for the technical transmission of the websites and for the secure operation of the server. A personalised evaluation of this data is not performed.
If you send us data through a contact form, this data is stored on our servers in the course of data backup. Your data are used by us exclusively for the processing of your enquiry. Your data are treated with strict confidentiality. There is no disclosure to third parties.
Personal data are data concerning you as an individual. This includes your name, your address and your email address. You do not have to disclose any personal data in order to visit our website. In some cases we need your name and address as well as additional information in order to offer you the desired service.
The same applies when we supply you with information material upon request or when we respond to your enquiry. In these cases we will always inform you of any saved data. In addition, we save only the data that you have transmitted to us automatically or voluntarily.
When you use one of our services, as a rule we collect only the data necessary to offer you our services. We may request additional information that, however, is of a voluntary nature. Whenever we process personal data, we do this in order to offer you our services or to pursue our commercial objectives.
When you visit our internet sites, we save certain information for administrative and technical reasons. This includes the type and version of the browser used, the date and time of access as well as the IP address.
This data is anonymised and used only for statistical purposes or to improve our internet and online services. This anonymised data is stored on secured systems – separate from personal data - and cannot be allocated to individual persons. This means that your personal data remains protected at all times.
When you visit our websites, we may store information on your computer in the form of Cookies. Cookies are small files that are transferred from an internet server to your browser and which are saved on its hard drive. The statutory basis for the use of Cookies is found in Art. 6 (1) let f DSGVO.
Only the internet protocol address is saved – no other personal data. The information saved in the Cookies allows for you to be automatically recognised upon your next visit to our internet site, which facilities your use of the site.
Naturally you can visit our website without accepting Cookies. If you do not wish that your computer is recognised upon your next visit, you can decline the use of Cookies in that you change the settings in your browser to “Reject Cookies”. You will find the respective procedures in the operating instructions of your browser. However, if you reject the use of Cookies, there may be limitations on the use of some areas of our internet sites.
We use Google Analytics, a web analysis service of Google Inc., 1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043 USA, hereinafter “Google” on our site. Google Analytics uses so-called “Cookies”, text files that are saved on your computer and which facilitate an analysis of the use of the website by you.
The information produced by these Cookies, for example the time, place and frequency of your website visit including your IP-address, are transmitted to Google in the USA and are stored there.
We use Google Analytics with the add-on "_gat._anonymizeIp" on our website. In this case, your IP-address is shortened and thereby anonymised by Google within Member States of the European Union or in other States party to the Agreement on the European Economic Area.
Google uses this information in order to analyse your use of our site in order to prepare reports on website activities for us and in order to provide additional services associated with the use of the website and of the internet. If applicable, Google will also transmit this information to third parties to the extent required by law or to the extent that third parties are engaged by Google to process this data.
According to its own information, in no event will Google connect your IP-address with other data from Google. You can prevent the installation the Cookies through a corresponding setting of your browser software; however, we inform you that in this case, you may not be able to fully use all functions of our website.
In addition, Google offers a deactivating add-on for the most common browsers that gives you more control over which data is recorded by Google on the websites accessed by you. The add-on notifies the JavaScript (ga.js) of Google Analytics that no information on the website visit should be transmitted to Google Analytics. However, the deactivation add-on for browsers of Google Analytics does not prevent that information from being transmitted to us or to another web analysis service possibly used by us. Further information on the installation of the browser add-on is found under the following Link: https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout?hl=de
If you visit our site via a mobile device (Smartphone or Tablet), you have to click this Link in order to prevent tracking by Google Analytics within this website in the future. This is also possible as an alternative to the above browser add-on. By clicking the link, an opt-out Cookie is set in your browser that is valid only for this browser and for this domain. If you delete the Cookies in this browser, the opt-out Cookie is also deleted so that you must click on the link once again.

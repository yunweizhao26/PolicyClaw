Hope Hospice is committed to protecting the privacy of its patients and families.
The personal and medical information gathered by Hope Hospice is used to monitor and manage your medical condition. Hope Hospice will not release your name, address, telephone number, emergency information or health data related to your medical history to any third party without your consent unless, 1) you expressly direct Hope Hospice to give the information to your healthcare providers or family, or 2) Hope Hospice is required to disclose such information by law.

The Hope Hospice site has security measures in place to protect the loss, misuse and alteration of the information under our control. Hope Hospice uses special software to protect your privacy. All data is encrypted and transmitted over the internet through a secured server established by an SSL certificate. The in house storage of your personal information in the Hope Hospice’ database is highly secure. Only employees authorized to access your data are able to do so based on a series of permission levels protected by passwords and software algorithms. All employees sign a confidentiality of data agreement at time of employment in which they agree to uphold the confidentiality of any such information.

For what purpose and on what basis we process personal data
Foundation for Market Information Research (SOMI) processes your personal data for the following purposes:
• Identifying and examining from a long-term perspective issues of social importance, in particular those related to market information;
• Informing and advising consumers, government bodies, policy or opinion makers, agents, advisers and experts, supervisors and market parties;
• Representing interests, taking actions and claiming compensation for the (legal) persons affiliated with or registered with it, injured parties, proxy or clients or participants;
• Providing support to other (legal) persons who in any way stand up for the person for whose benefit the foundation is researching or conducting interests, takes action;
• Entering into, elaborating and executing agreements for the benefit of the foundation.
Foundation for Market Information Research (SOMI) processes your personal data on the following principles:
• Identification data and marketing cookies are always processed on the basis of your informed and explicit consent;
• Functional and analytical data about your behavior on our website is processed on the basis of the legitimate interest of our organization;
• Data that are necessary to be able to deliver goods and services to you are collected in the context of the execution of an agreement;
• Certain sensitive personal data (for example citizen service number or copy ID) are processed insofar as this is strictly necessary for the establishment, exercise or defense of legal claims;
• Certain information about payments, invoices, agreements, statements, etc. is kept for a long time insofar as we are legally obliged to do so.
Foundation for Market Information Research (SOMI) does not make decisions based on automated processing on matters that can have (significant) consequences for people. These are decisions taken by computer programs or systems, without involving a person (for example, an employee of the Foundation for Market Information Research (SOMI)).
Foundation for Market Information Research (SOMI) does not store your personal data longer than is strictly necessary to realize the goals for which your data is collected.
We use the following maximum retention periods for your data:
• Data about your activities on our website and analytical data about your surfing behavior are stored for a maximum of 12 months;
• Identification data and data that you actively provide to us are kept for a maximum of 36 months after your last contact with us;
• Contracts, data relating to payments and invoices are kept for 10 years in order to be able to meet our legal obligations with regard to audit and (tax) control.
Foundation for Market Information Research (SOMI) shares your personal data with various third parties if this is necessary for the execution of the agreement and to comply with any legal obligation.
We conclude a processing agreement with companies that process your data on our behalf to ensure the same level of security and confidentiality of your data. Foundation for Market Information Research (SOMI) remains responsible for these processing operations.
In principle, your personal data is only processed within the EEA. If your data should leave the EEA, we will provide appropriate safeguards (eg. EU-US Privacy Shield, Adequacy Decisions, Standard Contractual Clauses, etc.) to ensure the same level of security and confidentiality of your data.
In addition, Foundation for Market Information Research (SOMI) provides your personal data to other third parties, for example if you are necessary for the establishment, exercise or defense of legal claims or to exercise your rights under the GDPR. We only do this with your express consent.
A cookie is a small text file that is stored on your computer, tablet or smartphone when you first visit this website.
When using cookies, we distinguish between functional, analytical and marketing cookies:
• The functional cookies we use are necessary for the technical operation of the website and your ease of use. They ensure that the website works properly and, for example, remember your preferred settings.
• Analytical cookies track your surfing behavior on our website and across different websites. They aim to optimize the operation of our website. We always set these analytical cookies in such a way that the impact on your privacy is as minimal as possible.
• We also use marketing cookies. The purpose of these is to keep you informed of our activities and to offer you goods and services. These cookies (eg. Facebook Pixel) are only processed after you have given us explicit permission to do so.
You can opt out of cookies by setting your internet browser so that it no longer stores cookies. In addition, you can also delete all information previously saved via the settings of your browser.

How we safeguard and value you while visiting our website.

Kinetic Insights may collect anonymous information about users who visit our site and aggregate this anonymous information for statistical analysis. For instance, we may use it to determine usage patterns so we know which parts of our site are most popular. Examples of the type of anonymous information we collect and how we collect it are:
Internet Tags: provide information about your browser (e.g., Microsoft Internet Explorer
, Google Chrome®), operating system (e.g., Windows, OSX), Internet service provider, geolocation and similar information.
IP Addresses: a number assigned to your computer by a Web server when you are on the Internet; we might use IP address information to determine the number of users who have visited a specific area of our site. Please note we could use this information to identify you if necessary to comply with legal requirements or for security issues.
Personally Identifiable Information (“Your Information”) includes information such as your name, email address, company name, telephone number and copies of communications shared through our contact forms or post comments. If you purchase through our Amazon Store, your credit card information is stored securely on that partner’s system, and we have no access to your financial data save for the information about the actual purchase (what was purchased, when it was purchased, amount spent and the shipping details).
Kinetic Insights collects Your Information only when you voluntarily provide it to us on our site during an online transaction to purchase through our Amazon Storefront (again we do not obtain your credit card data), sign up to receive Monthly Insights, complete a contact form or when you subscribe to comment on an Insights blog post. In the future we may add other instruments to our website to include event registrations, polls or surveys.
You may need to provide Your Information to participate in certain Kinetic Insights programs and activities or receive certain services or offers. Any actual purchases offered on our site involve providing Your Information to a third-party service provider on its website. In these circumstances, you will know that you are on another website because the URL will change. You should read the privacy policy of the third-party site before providing Your Information.
We use MailChimp for our email distribution. When an individual subscribes to Monthly Insights on our website, that data is transferred to the mailing list for that instrument and stored with our MailChimp account.
Kinetic Insights will use Your Information for the purpose for which you give it to us (e.g., to complete an online contact form, subscribe as a user in order to comment on an Insights blog post and receive updates, subscribe to Monthly Insights, purchase books through our Amazon Store and potentially register for events).
We may use Your Information to send you occasional postal and/or electronic mailings about Kinetic Insights and our services and programs that we think will interest you. While presently not within the scope of our activity, in the future Kinetic Insights may also use Your Information to send you occasional postal and/or electronic mailings about third parties whose information or services we think may be of interest to you.
Kinetic Insights may share Your Information with third parties that perform services for Kinetic Insights LLC, such as soliciting event attendance. Kinetic Insights also reserves the right to disclose Your Information in circumstances that the Kinetic Insights LLC deems necessary or appropriate, such as to respond to legal process or request from a governmental agency, security reasons or similar situations.
Kinetic Insights follows best practices to keep Your Information secure and safeguard it from unauthorized access by using special software tools and firewalls to protect our servers and virus/intrusion/malware detection software. However, no transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be 100% secure, so you provide Your Information at your own risk.
Our Site may include links to other Websites that may be of interest to you or are referenced within the body of an Insight post. Any links are provided only as a convenience and do not imply any endorsement of another site. Be sure to read the privacy policies on other sites other Websites.
We monitor our website for broken links, but especially with third-parties, we cannot guarantee that a link will remain valid. External links are more difficult to monitor than those linking internally on our site. If you encounter a broken link (a 404 Error – file not found or other server error), we would appreciate an email via our contact form or directly to info@kineticinsights.com with the information so we can secure some type of correction. Sometimes we can re-establish a connection with a new third-party link (sometimes other sites change without notice), and other times, we are forced to remove the link altogether if the data is no longer available. We have no control over third-party information or websites.

Automated identification of companies with your data and opt outs
For users who use the automated tool to identify companies that have their data, the App will need to securely sync with their email to import companies into the app.
In order to automatically make and process opt out requests, PrivacyHawk will also need to send and receive emails on your behalf from your email account. Unfortunately, most companies require you to verify that third parties like PrivacyHawk are authorized to make opt outs on your behalf. To do that for thousands of companies requires automation by securely connecting with your email.
To fully automate multi-step opt out process for our users, we need to be able to also read the responses from companies to be able to automatically complete the process of opting out. This is handled with secure software. For convenience, messages related to opt outs are viewable within the app so you can easily view, manage, and respond (if needed) within the app.
The goal of all this automation and the need for email access is to enable opting our users out of thousands of data harvesters and companies using their data without the end user having to do much if any manual work. It is the fastest and most effective way to reduce your digital footprint and hence risk of being hacked, spammed, or having your personal data exploited.

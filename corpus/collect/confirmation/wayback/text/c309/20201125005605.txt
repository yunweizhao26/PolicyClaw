FileExplorer is a mobile app developed by Skyjos. We take your privacy seriously. This document covers how we treat personal information that FileExplorer collects and receives when you use the app.
No identifiable or non-identifiable information is ever collected, stored or transmitted to our servers. FileExplorer never transmit your data and files without your consent.
We do not collect any contact information, location information, or files. Nothing!
As we collect no information about you, we can not use any information.
No data is collected about any children’s usage of FileExplorer in any way.
Use of Analytics Services and Third Party Services
No analytics services are taken from using this app. FileExplorer does not monitor your usage or anything about how you interact with the FileExplorer app.
We use third party services from Dropbox, Google Drive and others to provide you with various useful functions across FileExplorer. All these third parties may collect information about you and your usage of our applications in accordance with their respective privacy policies.
From time to time we may make changes to this privacy policy, which will be posted on our web sites and relevant communications channels. You are advised to check our web sites regularly to view our most recent privacy policy.
If you have any concerns or questions about this policy, please send email to support@skyjos.com

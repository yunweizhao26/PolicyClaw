The Australian Steel Institute (ASI) is committed to protecting the privacy of your personal and business information. When you visit the ASI website, we may collect and store the following information about you and your organisation: the name of the domain from which you access the Internet; the date and time you access the site.
At no time will your personal information be disclosed to another company for that company’s independent use. All business information provided is treated as commercial-in-confidence unless permission is specifically given to circulate it. Some usage information is collected for internal management purposes may include measuring the number of visitors to specific sections of the site and who referred the visitors which help us keep the site relevant and useful.
We will make every reasonable attempt at the time to inform you whenever ASI collects your information through a third party.
You will be advised of why we are collecting your information; and to whom we intend disclosing that information at that time.
At all times, the ASI will ensure that your personal and business information is accurate and guarded from unauthorised access. Only staff with a need to access your personal information to perform their job will have access to that information.
– During the application for membership of the ASI your information is collected as an essential part of this process. This information is for internal purposes only and used in a manner that allows ASI to fulfil the specific membership offering.
- Information you provide in registering for events is to ensure we offer similar events to you at the next opportunity. Your details are only disclosed outside the ASI for jointly organised events and only to the industry body concerned.
– whenever the ASI collects information or comment, any written account is provided to the source person for courtesy checks prior to publication.
– whilst the Directory is publicly available on the Internet, a password is issued to the nominated information contact with each ASI member organisation to ensure only they are able to update details as required. The ASI administration staff have access purely for grammatical and accuracy checking and the member is automatically informed via email when the ASI corrects the listing.
If you identify yourself through any of the activities mentioned above or for any other reason by sending an email or submitting a form, we collect and store your email address and other information you provide. If you give us permission to send information to you, we will tailor the correspondence we send according to your preferences. If you do not want us to contact you, please let us know by contacting the Membership Services Officer at membership@steel.org.au or log into our interactive website using your membership identification and edit your preferences to suit your interest and/or need.
Please note we cannot guarantee the security of any data or information you send to us.
If you believe that we have not dealt with your personal information in a manner that complies with the Privacy Act (1988) you should contact ASI to discuss how we may better deal with it.
This privacy policy was updated on 18 March 2011. When changes to our privacy policy occur, we will notify active members of our database via email.

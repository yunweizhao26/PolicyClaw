At Newry.ie we are committed to keeping you in the loop regarding any way in which we use your data. We understand that GDPR is in place to put you back in control of how information about you is stored or used.
Some areas of our site use Cookies, small pieces of data stored on your computer, to enable some sections of it to work, including our Shop and Photograph Gallery. You can enable or disable cookies by following the instructions of your browser on your computer, but note this will mean you would not be able to use these components.
Our site uses Google Analytics to collect anonymous statistical information such as the number of visitors to our site. Cookies added by Google Analytics are governed by the privacy policies of Google Analytics. If you want you can disable cookies from Google Analytics on your browser.
Our site also contains Google AdSense Advertisements that are targeted to site content and audience.
If you contact us via our Contact page we require your email address and name to enable us to deal with your query. We will not share any of these details unless you explicitly ask for this to be done as part of your request.
If you are Advertising on our site we will use your information to enable us carry out your request. We keep statistics on Advertising figures, ie number of clicks or hits but these are completely anonymous and they are only shared with the business that is advertising.
We do not have a mailing list but if in the future we decide to have one you will have to join it yourself and there would always be a facility to remove your details from the list on every communication.
Our websites Database is stored on a secure host and the site has a SSL certificate for maximum security.
We store our library of photographs complete with captions where appropriate securely on our computer and back up to a secure hard drive.
Newry.ie keep contact information for our customers and journalistic contacts/ contributers. We store this securely and don't share our information with others unless you give us permission.
If you have any issue with any image or editorial that appears or exists on our Website/ Facebook/ Twitter Page please contact us at news@newry.ie and we will deal with it.

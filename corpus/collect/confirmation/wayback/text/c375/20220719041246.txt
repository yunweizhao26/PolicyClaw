At Bikevirtue.com, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This blog privacy policy explains how we use and protect your personal information when you visit our blog.
We will never sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes without your explicit consent. We may use your personal information to contact you with news and updates about our blog or to ask for your feedback.
We will always keep your data securely stored and we will only process it in ways that you have consented to. If you have any questions about this blog privacy policy, please contact us at info@bikevirtue.com.
Like most websites, we collect and use the data contained in log files. The information in a log file includes your IP address, browser type, Internet service provider, referring/exit pages, operating system, date/time stamp, and clickstream data. We use this information to analyze trends, administer the site, track user’s movement around the site, and gather demographic information.
We use cookies to store information about your preferences, to personalize your experience, and to improve our website. A cookie is a small text file that is placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit our blog. Cookies allow us to recognize your browser and remember certain information. You can disable cookies if you do not want us to use them, however this may limit the functionality of our website.
We may also use web beacons on our blog from time to time. A web beacon is a transparent image that is placed on a web page or in an email. They are used to track the movement of users around a website, or to know when an email has been opened and acted upon.
Our blog may contain links to other websites that are not owned or controlled by us. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these other websites. We encourage you to be aware when you leave our site and to read the privacy policies of each website that you visit. This blog privacy policy applies only to information collected by us.
We may update our blog privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new blog privacy policy on our website. You are advised to review this blog privacy policy periodically for any changes. If you continue to visit our website after we have posted a new blog privacy policy, you hereby consent to the new blog privacy policy.

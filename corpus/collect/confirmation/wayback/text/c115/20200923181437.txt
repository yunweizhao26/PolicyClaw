Public information and commercial transaction data from real estate activity

Real estate transactions are recorded through the MLS and other sources, such as county and state tax and property data. Web beacons (also referred to as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs) are tiny graphics with a unique identifier that are used to track the online movements of web or mobile app users.

Schwarzman Scholars is the first scholarship created to respond to the geopolitical landscape of the 21st Century.
Each new cohort of Schwarzman Scholars will join a global network of the world’s most talented young leaders.
The selection process is designed to identify the most promising young leaders from around the world.

RuleDesigner is a software platform owned by RuleDesigner srl designed to implement digital innovation initiatives to support the development of new business models in consolidated manufacturing companies or startups with an approach aimed at the management, integration and automation of collaborative processes relating to innovation product and business.
Management of the product life cycle and support for the creation of a closed-loop digital thread, which goes from the concept to the entire life of the product which helps to obtain a holistic and integrated view of information assets beyond the traditional functional silo approach.
Digitalization of enterprise technical, commercial and operational processes related to the proposition of complex products customized based on the preferences and needs of the end customer. Advanced digitalization of business processes for better adaptability to business changes or new market needs.

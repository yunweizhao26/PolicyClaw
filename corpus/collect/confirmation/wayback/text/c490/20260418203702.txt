The Alive Foundation Inc., doing business as MorningCoach® ("we," "our," "us"), respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you visit our websites, use our applications — including the MorningCoach® Companion dashboard — or participate in our programs.
We collect the following categories of information depending on how you interact with MorningCoach®:
Personal details (name, email address, phone number, billing address)
Payment information processed through Stripe — we do not store your full credit card number
If you use the MorningCoach® Companion dashboard (my.morningcoach.com), we collect and store the content you create within the application, including:
Bug reports and feedback you submit through the application
The Companion dashboard allows you to upload images for your dream board and profile. Accepted file types are .jpg, .png, and .webp, with a maximum file size of 5 MB per upload.
IP address, browser type, device information, and operating system
Referral source and attribution data (how you found MorningCoach®)
Provide, maintain, and improve our services, including the Companion dashboard
Generate AI-assisted content within the Companion dashboard (see Section 4 below)
Send transactional emails (account confirmations, billing receipts, data archive notices)
Communicate updates, offers, and content relevant to your membership
Track attribution to understand how members discover MorningCoach®
Ensure platform security, enforce rate limits, and prevent fraud
The MorningCoach® Companion dashboard includes AI-assisted features — such as Daily Focus generation and AI assist buttons — powered by Anthropic's Claude language model. When you use these features:
The content you enter (such as goals, tasks, or focus prompts) is sent to Anthropic's API to generate a response
Anthropic processes this data solely to provide the AI response and does not use it to train their models
AI-generated content is clearly labeled within the dashboard
You may choose not to use AI-assisted features — they are optional and do not affect your access to other Companion functionality
For more information about how Anthropic handles data, visit
When you connect Google Calendar, MorningCoach® Companion accesses your calendar events solely to display them alongside your tasks and goals in the Companion dashboard. We do not store or share your calendar data.
We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with trusted service providers who help us operate MorningCoach®:
We may also share data with legal authorities when required by law, or with successors in the event of a business sale or transfer.
We take the security of your data seriously and employ the following measures:
Authentication is managed through secure JSON Web Tokens (JWT) with expiration controls
API endpoints are protected by rate limiting to prevent abuse
Access to member data is restricted to authorized team members on a need-to-know basis
While we use industry-standard security measures, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but are committed to protecting your information to the best of our ability.
You have the following rights regarding your personal data:
You may request a summary of the personal information we hold about you
Companion dashboard users can export their data directly from within the application
You may request corrections to inaccurate personal information
You may request deletion of your personal data by emailing
or using the delete function within the Companion dashboard
We will respond to all data requests within 30 days.
We retain your data for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide our services.
Your Companion data (goals, projects, tasks, plans, reviews, dream board, and belt progress) will be archived for
notifying you that your data will be permanently deleted in 7 days
After 90 days, archived data is permanently deleted and cannot be recovered
If you resubscribe within the 90-day archive window, your data will be fully restored
Account-level data (name, email, billing history) is retained separately in accordance with legal and financial record-keeping requirements.
We use cookies and similar technologies to improve user experience and analyze site performance. These include:
Essential cookies required for authentication and site functionality
Analytics cookies (Google Analytics) to understand how visitors use our site
Attribution tracking to identify how members discover MorningCoach®
You can disable non-essential cookies in your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies may affect site functionality.
Our websites and applications may contain links to external sites, including Amazon, Circle, and other platforms. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies independently.
MorningCoach® is not directed at individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.
We may update this policy periodically to reflect changes in our services, technology, or legal requirements. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "last updated" date. Material changes will be communicated to active members via email.

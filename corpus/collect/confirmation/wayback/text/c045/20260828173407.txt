We want to make you aware of how we use "cookies", including tags, pixels, SDKs
and other similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience when visiting FBFS Websites, email, texts, and applications. “Cookies” are small data files sent to your browser from a web server and stored on your devices. We use “cookies” and other technologies to help us track visits to our Website, applications and Other Services and to find out how visitors use our sites. We routinely gather information regarding activity using tags or pixels, or transparent GIF files to help manage our online advertising and site visitor marketing efforts. This information may be used to manage and customize our Website’s and application’s content, layout, and services in order to improve our sites and enhance our service to you. We log your geolocation which includes IP address and location so that we may tailor the content displayed on the Website to your current location.

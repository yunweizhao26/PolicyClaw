This privacy policy is designed to inform you about how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our applications. We are committed to protecting your privacy.
Our applications do not collect personal information directly from you. However, to provide you with the best possible user experience, we use Google Firebase Analytics, Google AdMob and Google Play Games Services in our applications.
This service collects anonymous, aggregated data about app usage, such as which features are used most often. This data helps us understand how our apps are used and allows us to improve them.
AdMob is an advertising platform that allows us to display personalized ads within our apps. AdMob collects anonymous, aggregated data about ad impressions and user interactions. Additionally, we use AdMob's mediation feature, which means we can display ads from other ad networks within our apps.
Some of our apps uses Google Play Games Services to allow you to save and sync your game progress across multiple devices. This service makes it easy to continue your game anytime, anywhere, without losing your progress.
What data do we collect using Google Play Games Services?
Using Google Play Games Services, we only collect data related to your game progress.
A unique identifier that distinguishes the app from other apps.
A unique identifier that distinguishes you from other players.
All data related to your progress in the game, such as level, achievements, purchases, etc.
The collected data is used exclusively for saving and syncing your game progress. According to Google Play Games Services' privacy policy, Google may collect additional information about your gameplay, but this data is not available to us.
The collected data is used solely for the purpose of improving our applications and enhancing the user experience. For example, we can use the data to identify which features need further development and which are most appreciated by users. Google AdMob uses the data it collects to select and display relevant ads to you. By accepting the UMP consent form upon first launching the app, you have agreed to receive personalized ads.
We do not share your data with third parties, except as required by law or with your explicit consent. However, Google Firebase Analytics, Google AdMob and Google Play Games Services may share anonymous, aggregated data with Google in accordance with their privacy policies. The collected data is stored on secure servers, and we implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect the data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, destruction, accidental loss or damage, and unlawful destruction or accidental loss.
Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to access your personal data and request its correction. For requests related to data erasure, please contact Google directly, as they handle your data.
We reserve the right to modify this privacy policy at any time. We will notify you of any changes through the application.

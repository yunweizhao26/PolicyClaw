This document describes how Onlinevents (OE) the data controller, collects, uses and protects your information.

To create and operate an OE Library membership you will need to enter your name, email address, create a username and password and enter payment details. These details are entered using our online signup form via a HTTPS connection.

Learning logs are stored in with the OE website database using a secure and encrypted server located within the UK. OE Library Members can only access their own learning logs. Access is secured with a username and password chosen by OE Library Members at registration.
OE operates an email mailing list which is used to list subscribers of upcoming events and latest OE news. Users subscribe through an
online process with their email address. All email addresses are held securely in a password protected platform and never passed to a third party. Subscribers can decide whether they would like daily, weekly or monthly emails by updating 'email preferences' on any email received from OE.

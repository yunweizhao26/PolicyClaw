At Skin, Bones, Hearts & Private Parts, we understand and respect your concern about the privacy of any information you supply. We believe that knowledge is the key to monitoring and protecting how your information is used on the Internet. This statement sets forth Skin, Bones, Hearts & Private Part’s Privacy Policy and describes the practice we follow with respect to all visitors to this site.
Skin, Bones, Hearts & Private Parts collects certain personal information from users of this site. Guests are required to provide certain personal information in order to access certain areas, and authorized users must provide certain contact and account information in order to access these areas. Skin, Bones, Hearts & Private Parts may use certain account information of authorized users to conduct credit inquiries. Skin, Bones, Hearts & Private Parts uses this information for internal purposes (to process your order and any invoices, to maintain or update your Skin, Bones, Hearts & Private Parts account, or to send you updates about special offers, new services, special promotions, and noteworthy news and events). Skin, Bones, Hearts & Private Parts does not sell or rent information about its customers to third parties. Skin, Bones, Hearts & Private Parts may share contact information with its partners or affiliates to enable them to provide you with special benefits, promotions, or offers from either Skin, Bones, Hearts & Private Parts or its affiliates and its partners. Skin, Bones, Hearts & Private Parts may also profile your Web site activity in order to accurately track your account and provide you with Skin, Bones, Hearts & Private Parts events that correspond to your interests. Skin, Bones, Hearts & Private Parts may also compile data in aggregate form so that we may better understand the users that are visiting our site. For example, we may produce reports on the most popular search terms by collecting general search term data based on individual searches. Aggregate data is anonymous and does not contain any personal information that identifies a user.
Please note that your information is saved on our server as you enter it. Skin, Bones, Hearts & Private Parts will keep your registration information saved after it is entered into the form.
Skin, Bones, Hearts & Private Parts offers its members the choice to not receive e-mails or updates about special benefits, promotions, or offers from Skin, Bones, Hearts & Private Parts, its affiliates, or its partners. If you no longer wish to receive these updates, or you wish to unsubscribe from Skin, Bones, Hearts & Private Parts membership, please complete our Feedback form and let us know in the comments section that you would like to be removed from the mailing list.
Skin, Bones, Hearts & Private Parts has a number of operational functions in place to protect the confidentiality of personal information. However, perfect security on the Internet does not exist, and Skin, Bones, Hearts & Private Parts does not warrant that its site is impenetrable or invulnerable to hackers.
When you visit the Skin, Bones, Hearts & Private Parts Web site, www.skinbonescme.com sends your computer a cookie, which is a small file that resides on your computers hard drive and contains non-personally identifiable information about the user. Cookies only identify the users computer. Cookies allow Skin, Bones, Hearts & Private Parts to make its web site more useful by storing information about your preferences. For example, if you select events and frequently enroll to attend, Skin, Bones, Hearts & Private Parts will send your computer a cookie that identifies you as an attendee of events. You can set your browsers options not to accept cookies, but this may prevent you from accessing all of the features of the Skin, Bones, Hearts & Private Parts web site www.skinbonescme.com. Additionally, this site uses clear GIFs, or pixel tags, provided by our ad serving company to help manage our online advertising. These clear GIFs provide our ad serving company with the ability to recognize a browser cookie when a browser visits this site. This allows us to learn which banner ads bring users to our Web site. The information we collect and share through this technology is not personally identifiable (it does not include your name, address, telephone number, or email address).
This website uses the Google Analytics service to collect demographic and interest reports that include information such as age, gender, general interests etc. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to collect this information based on past visits to the Skin, Bones, Hearts & Private Parts Piercing website. For privacy reasons, Google applies thresholds to the data to prevent site-owners from knowing the demographics of individual users. Skin, Bones, Hearts & Private Parts does not rent or sell e-mail lists. Skin, Bones, Hearts & Private Parts may send you an e-mail to notify you about your recent web form completion and notification on an upcoming sale for our services; this will be sent to the e-mail address you provide to Skin, Bones, Hearts & Private Parts.
Participation in surveys or contests on the Skin, Bones, Hearts & Private Parts web site is completely voluntary and the user therefore has a choice whether or not to disclose personal information. Information requested may include contact information (such as name, address, and phone number), and demographic information (such as age, gender, zip code). Contact information will be used to notify the winners and award prizes. Survey information will be used for the purposes of monitoring or improving the professionalisms of services offered and the user experience of the Skin, Bones, Hearts & Private Parts web site.
Please be aware that the Skin, Bones, Hearts & Private Parts website may contain links to other sites that are covered by their own privacy policies. Skin, Bones, Hearts & Private Parts is not responsible for the privacy practices of such other sites and we encourage our users to be aware when they leave our site. We recommend that our users read the privacy statements of each and every website that collects personally identifiable information.

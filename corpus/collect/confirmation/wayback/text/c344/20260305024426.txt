Mysk Inc. (“Mysk”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information related to your use of Psylo, our privacy-focused browser for iOS and iPadOS designed to create isolated web browsing sessions (“silos”). By using Psylo, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.
We collect only the minimal data necessary to operate the app and manage your subscription, specifically:
A randomly generated unique identifier used exclusively to manage your subscription.
Information provided by Apple indicating the region associated with your App Store account used for subscription purposes. This data does not reflect your actual residence or location.
Data volume metrics (in MB) uploaded and downloaded to inform you about your monthly bandwidth consumption.
Logs recording the times data is uploaded or downloaded, used strictly for monitoring service reliability and preventing abuse.

Your data is used exclusively for the following purposes:
To maintain and validate your subscription status using your unique subscription identifier.
To provide you with bandwidth usage statistics, detect potential abuse, and monitor service integrity and performance.
Mysk implements industry-standard security practices to safeguard your data:
Psylo routes all browsing traffic securely through the Mysk Private Proxy Network using Transport Layer Security (TLS) encryption.
The Mysk Private Proxy Network explicitly avoids logging IP addresses, website visits, or browsing data, and cannot decrypt the encrypted traffic between your device and visited websites.
Data collected is retained only for the active duration of your subscription.
All usage data are automatically deleted two months following subscription expiration.

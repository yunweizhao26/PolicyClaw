Personal Contact Information: First & Last Name, Email, State/Region and Date of Birth are all required when creating account and used as unique identifiers within the Service. Users may elect to add a phone number and mailing address to their profile which are not used for solicitation or marketing purposes. Adding a mailing address can help when sending out prizes and products.
Your Contacts: In the Community section on the Buddies page you may select to share contacts. The purpose of this is to allow you to see which of your contacts in your phone has an existing HuntLeague account and it gives you the option to send an invitation to your contacts inviting them to use the application. The Service matches up the email addresses of your contacts with current HuntLeague accounts to see if there is a match. Contact cards from your phone are not being collected or stored by the Service. Contacts are accessed only to help you find and connect with people you already know.
Location: GPS data is a central part of the applications functionality. Your location is only used to record events connected to your experiences in the field such as where a sighting or shot took place. We use the start location of your hunt to identify the state, country, or region that you are in for posting hunt and scout summary information on the Community page. Protecting your stored location data is a top priority and it will never be used or sold without your permission. You may choose to share hunt logs that contain GPS information only with hunting Buddies and Parties within the app. You may choose to unshare GPS information contained in hunt logs at any time. Shared Stands and Areas cannot be unshared later after sharing with a Buddy or Party.
User Content: HuntLeague collects the data you store in hunt logs which may contain pictures, audio, video, text notes, and event specific information. Prior to accessing your microphone, camera, and photo library you must grant permission within the app. You can deny these or change these permission settings on your phone at any time. That data belongs to you and you may choose to share it with hunting Buddies or Parties. The Service does not sell or share that information. We use generic markers for events connected to your account for point system calculations and for analytics on app usage. Content you post on the Community page is considered public information and HuntLeague reserves the right to reuse that content for marketing, website, social media, and within the app to highlight app usage and acknowledge individual users. Usage Data: HuntLeague receives a time stamp connected to your user account when you open the app. This is used to verify active verse inactive users on the platform. The Service does not record the number of sessions, time in the app, page clicks, or long term usage data. It only records the last time you accessed (synced data) with the mobile application.
Device Identifier: Device identity is used by the service for data integrity. When you login to the Service, your hunt logs and database syncs with the server. If the server recognizes you are attempting to login with a different device it will warn you that you may lose data. For this reason you may receive a warning that you are logged in on another device and it is recommended that you sync your data and logout of that device prior to logging in on a different device.

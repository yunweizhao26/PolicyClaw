Data Protection – University of Galway Mountaineering Club
NUI Galway Mountaineering club (here after refereed to as the club) is a registered organisation inside of the National University of Ireland – Galway, and managed by the Sport & Clubs unit. As a result data protection polices for the above two entities take precedence over any of the information below.
Member’s information is stored by these entities and falls directly under their responsibility.
By interacting with the club your data may be stored on services outside of the services maintained by the two entities named above and the following information is related to those services only.
The following are external services that may process your data by your interactions with the club:
* All email interactions with official club addresses (example@nuigmc.com)
* Specific information you give us for certain activities that may be stored on this platform.
* Any payments made on the nuigmc.com/store platform
* Making a card payment to the club
In addition to the above your information will most likely be stored in the following regards:
Club medical consent forms which are collected for all active club members.
Registered climbers database to record assessment levels of climbers.
Additional lists related to specific activities such as trips.
What does the club do with my data?
The purpose of storing the above data is to:
Maintain accurate records to allow the club to function efficiently.
Ensure the safety & enjoyment of all club members.
We will request your permission if the above purposes reasonable undergo change. This all conforms with GDPR.
(Only in the event of an emergency) Emergency organisations
Your data is not shared outside of the above.
In all cases, your data is stored in safe and secure systems. Personal data is kept for a one year period (Normally September to September), except in limited cases such as incident report forms which are kept as per external legal requirements. Emails from our alumni and GMIT members are keep until requested they are left.

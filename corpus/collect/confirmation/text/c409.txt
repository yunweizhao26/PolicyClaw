We collect and process different types of personal data when you use Rider X s services. We only process your personal data for specific purposes and where we have a legal basis under GDPR to do so[3][4]. The main purposes for which Rider X uses your data, and their corresponding legal bases, are: - Providing Our Services: We use your personal data to process and deliver your orders, provide the services you request, and manage your account or verified sessions. For example, we use your name and address to deliver goods, your contact info to communicate order updates, and your payment details to process payments. Legal Basis: Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR)[5] this processing is necessary to fulfill our contract with you or to take steps at your request before entering into a contract (such as quoting a ride or verifying your identity to use the service). - Account Management and User Identification: If you choose to create an account, we process your data to register and maintain your account. If you opt to use the service without a full account by one-time identity verification via email or phone, we process your contact data to verify your identity and prevent fraud. Legal Basis: Performance of contract (to provide the service) and our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR)[4] in ensuring the security of our platform and preventing misuse. - Communication and Customer Support: We use your email or phone number to send service-related communications such as order confirmations, receipts, delivery updates, or important service notices. We may also use your data to respond when you contact customer support. Legal Basis: Performance of contract (to keep you informed and assist you) or legitimate interests in providing you with appropriate customer service (we have a legitimate interest in ensuring customer inquiries are answered and issues resolved). - Payment Processing and Fraud Prevention: Your payment details are used to charge for our services and to conduct fraud prevention (for example, we may use security measures to detect fraudulent transactions). Legal Basis: Performance of contract (processing payments you authorize) and legitimate interests (to protect our business and customers from fraud). We may also be under a legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR) to keep certain transaction records for financial reporting and anti-fraud purposes. - Location-Based Services: If you use features that rely on location data (for instance, to automatically determine your pickup point or destination), we process your device s location. Legal Basis: Performance of contract (if the service requires your location to function) or, in some cases, consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) if you grant location access in an app. We will ask your permission before using precise GPS location where required. You can withdraw your consent for location access at any time via your device settings. - Service Improvement and Analytics: We may use order history and usage data (excluding any third-party analytics tools) to analyze service performance, troubleshoot issues, and improve our offerings. This involves looking at how users navigate our app/website, what features are used, and where improvements can be made. These analyses are done internally and without using external analytics providers. Legal Basis: Legitimate interests (to continuously improve our products and user experience). We ensure that any analytics or logging we perform do not intrude on your privacy unreasonably and are necessary for our service improvement (and, as noted, we do not share these data with external analytics platforms). - Legal Compliance: We process and retain certain data to comply with our legal obligations. For example, Austrian commercial and tax laws may require us to keep transaction records and invoices for a specified period. We may also process data when necessary to respond to lawful requests by public authorities or to meet other legal requirements. Legal Basis: Compliance with a legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR)[6]. We will not use your personal data for any new purpose that is incompatible with the above without informing you and obtaining your consent if required. We do not perform any automated decision-making, including profiling, that produces legal or similarly significant effects on you (for example, there are no algorithms solely deciding to deny you services without human involvement, and no profiling for marketing). Your privacy is important to us. Therefore, we do not sell your personal information or share it with unaffiliated third parties for their own marketing or other unrelated purposes[7]. This includes, for example, payment processing companies (to charge your credit card or facilitate payment transactions), IT hosting and infrastructure providers (to host our application and data securely), or SMS/email delivery services (to send verification codes or notifications). When we engage such service providers, they are bound by contracts (Data Processing Agreements) to process personal data only under our instructions and to provide adequate security measures, in compliance with GDPR. They do not have rights to use your data for any other purpose. - Legal and Safety Requirements: If required by law, or if necessary to establish, exercise, or defend our legal rights, we might disclose personal data to authorities, courts, or law enforcement (for example, responding to a court order or an official investigation). We will ensure any request is valid and only provide the minimum necessary data. Additionally, if it s necessary to protect your vital interests or those of another person (e.g., in an emergency situation), we may share information on the basis of vital interests (Art. 6(1)(d) GDPR). Aside from the above, Rider X does not share your personal data with any other third parties. In particular, no data is shared with third-party advertising networks, analytics providers, or social media companies since we use no external marketing or analytics tools as stated. If in the future we ever consider partnerships or data sharing beyond these categories, we will update this Privacy Policy and, if required, seek your consent. We also ensure that all data remains within jurisdictions approved under GDPR. Currently, we store and process data on servers located in Austria and/or other member states of the European Union. We do not transfer your personal data to countries outside the European Economic Area (EEA). If this ever changes (for example, using a cloud service outside the EEA), we will implement appropriate safeguards as required by GDPR (such as EU Commission adequacy decisions or Standard Contractual Clauses) and inform you accordingly in this Policy. We keep your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described above, or as required by law. This means: - Account Data: If you have a Rider X account, we retain your personal profile information for as long as your account is active. For example, if we are handling a dispute or an investigation, relevant data will be retained until the matter is closed, then removed or archived as required. Once the applicable retention period expires, or if we no longer need your personal data for the purposes we collected it, we will delete it or anonymize it so you can no longer be identified. If deletion or anonymization is not immediately possible (for example, because the data is stored in secure backups), we will ensure the data remains securely stored and isolated from any further active processing until deletion is possible. We implement role-based access and authentication controls to prevent unauthorized access by our employees or contractors. - Secure Infrastructure: We host data in secure facilities/data centers that have physical security controls and cybersecurity measures. Our servers are regularly updated with security patches. - Monitoring and Testing: We monitor our systems for possible vulnerabilities and attacks, and we carry out regular security assessments. In the event of any data breach that poses a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by GDPR (Articles 33 and 34). - Training and Policies: Our personnel are trained in data protection and are bound by confidentiality agreements. We have internal policies to ensure we approach privacy and security as a company priority. While we strive to protect your information, please note that no system can be 100% secure. However, we continuously update and improve our security practices to meet or exceed industry standards and legal requirements for data protection. As a user of Rider X s services and a data subject under GDPR, you have specific rights regarding your personal data. You can exercise these rights at any time by contacting us (see the Contact section). These rights include[10][11]: - Right of Access: You have the right to obtain confirmation whether we are processing your personal data, and if so, request access to the data. We will provide a copy of your personal data undergoing processing, along with information about how we use it[12]. (Note: For additional copies requested, we may charge a reasonable fee as permitted by law.) - Right to Rectification: If any of your personal data we hold is inaccurate or incomplete, you have the right to request that we correct or update it[13]. We encourage you to keep your information up-to-date and will honor any rectification requests promptly. - Right to Erasure: You have the right to request deletion of your personal data in certain circumstances[14]. This is also known as the right to be forgotten. We will erase your data upon request if, for example, the data is no longer needed for the purposes it was collected, or you withdraw consent and there is no other legal ground for processing, or if we are legally obliged to delete it. Please note that this right is not absolute sometimes we must retain certain data to comply with legal obligations or establish/exercise legal claims, in which case we will inform you if your deletion request cannot be fully honored (for instance, we cannot delete data we are required to keep for tax law within the mandatory retention period). - Right to Restrict Processing: You have the right to ask us to restrict (pause) the processing of your personal data in certain situations[15]. For example, if you contest the accuracy of the data or object to our processing, you can request a restriction while your request is being handled. When processing is restricted, we will store your data but not use it until the issue is resolved (unless you consent or further processing is required by law). - Right to Data Portability: You have the right to receive the personal data that you have provided to us in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format, and to have that data transmitted to another controller where technically feasible[16]. This applies to data processed by us by automated means, on the legal basis of your consent or for performance of a contract. We will, upon your request, provide you with such data or send it directly to another service provider if you prefer, provided it s feasible. - Right to Object: You have the right to object to certain processing of your personal data. (i) If we process your data for direct marketing purposes (note: currently we do not send marketing communications without your consent, but if we ever did, you can object at any time and we will stop using your data for that purpose). (ii) If we process your data based on legitimate interests, you also have the right to object to that processing on grounds relating to your particular situation[17]. If you lodge an objection, we will evaluate your request and will no longer process the data for that purpose unless we demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds for the processing that override your interests, rights and freedoms, or if continuing processing is necessary for the establishment, exercise or defense of legal claims. - Right to Withdraw Consent: In cases where we are processing your personal data based on your consent, you have the right to withdraw that consent at any time[18][19]. If you withdraw consent, we will stop the processing that was based on it. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing done before the withdrawal. (For example, if you gave consent to use your location and later withdraw it, we will stop collecting your real-time location going forward.) - Right to Lodge a Complaint: If you believe your data protection rights have been violated, you have the right to file a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Austria, the relevant supervisory authority is the Austrian Data Protection Authority (Datenschutzbeh rde)[20]. Address: Barichgasse 40-42, 1030 Vienna, Austria. Website: www.dsb.gv.at[21]. You can contact the Austrian Data Protection Authority to report concerns or file a complaint directly[20]. We would, however, appreciate the chance to address your concerns before you approach the authority, so please feel free to contact us with any issues and we will do our best to resolve them. These rights can be exercised free of charge.

MetroPacer | Running App for iPhone and Apple Watch MetroPacer respects your privacy. All workout and health data are processed and stored locally on your Apple Watch and iPhone. No data is ever transmitted to the developer or to any third parties. During your run, MetroPacer records workout data such as time, distance, pace, heart rate, cadence, power, and GPS route. This data is saved on your devices to provide post-run analysis and statistics. MetroPacer also writes workouts and metrics to the Apple Health app, where they are managed under Apple s privacy and security policies. MetroPacer does not collect analytics, tracking information, or personal identifiers of any kind. Your running data stays entirely under your control.

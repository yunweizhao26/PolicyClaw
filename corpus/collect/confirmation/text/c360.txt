Privacy Policy Last updated: May 21, 2025 Introduction This Privacy Policy describes how Luxmeter ( we , us , or our ) collects, uses, and discloses your information when you use our Luxmeter iOS application. Information We Collect Information You Provide Luxmeter does not require you to create an account. Any data you generate while using the app is stored locally on your device. Automatically Collected Information - Device Information: We collect device-specific information such as your operating system version and device model to optimize the app s performance. - Sensor Data: The app accesses your device s light sensor to provide light measurements. Data Security We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access or disclosure.

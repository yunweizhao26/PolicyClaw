Personal Data We Collect or Process and How We Use It The Services capture some information about visitors to our Services automatically utilizing background local storage and session storage technologies such as cookies and pixels (collectively, "Cookies"). Cookies are small files or other pieces of data which are downloaded or stored on your computer or other device, that can be tied to information about your use of the Services. Examples of information of this type are your IP address, the browser you are using, the operating system you are using, the pages on the website that you visit and details of your transaction activity, including if you have opened up our emails or if you completed a registration. When we use Cookies, we may use "session" Cookies that last until you close your browser or "persistent" Cookies that last until you or your browser delete them. You may change your browser setting to decline the use of Cookies. We use Cookies to store visitors preferences and to record session information. We use Google Analytics, which use Cookies to identify the frequency of use of certain areas of our website and to identify preferences. For more information regarding how Google collects, uses, and shares your information please visit www.google.com/policies/privacy/partners. Google will use this information to analyze your use of the Services, compile reports on activity for us, and perform further services associated with website use and Internet use. Google may also transmit this information to third parties where required by law or to the extent that third parties process these data on behalf of Google. While you can deactivate Google Analytics using a browser add-on https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout?hl=en if you do not wish the analysis to take place, our website will not operate unless you accept cookies. You may be able to configure your browser to accept or reject all or some cookies, or notify you when a cookie is set -- each browser is different, so check the "Help" menu of your browser to learn how to change your cookie preferences -- however, you must enable cookies from fubar in order to use the site. In order for a user to register and become a fubar member, we collect (and require) an email, password, screen name (or nickname), date of birth and gender. We use email address and password to authenticate members and to communicate with our users, including to send updates, a newsletter, or news regarding the Services. Members may choose not to receive emails of this type, or change the number and frequency of emails, by changing their "notification" setting in their profile settings. Members within a personal network communicate on fubar with each other through the fubar service, without disclosing their email addresses. We collect age information to ensure our members are 18 years of age or older. We collect gender information to assist members in finding and communicating with each other and to group our players according to gender, as game play on the Services is divided between men and women. fubar also collects other, optional profile data including but not limited to first and last name, location (city, state and country or postal code) and relationship status. Members may upload a profile picture, which may be displayed along with the member s screen name to other members in order to facilitate user interaction in the fubar social networking community. Members full names are not directly revealed to other members, although fubar users are able to search members by their screen name or email address. You may indicate in your profile settings who can view your profile and whether members can be notified when you view their profiles. From time to time, fubar or a partner, may sponsor a promotion, sweepstake or contest on fubar. Users may be asked to provide personal information including name, email address or home address or to answer questions in order to participate. Some features on the Services require the payment of a fee, such as the purchase of coins and subscriptions and to advance in games, in which case we work with a payment processor and collect your name, billing address and payment card information. You have the option of allowing fubar to save your payment information via a token (which is not complete payment card information) for your convenience in connection with future purchases. We collect game play information from our users and provide a leaderboard that shows how everyone did, so your game play will be available for viewing by other users. fubar members may store email addresses of people they know in their internal fubar address book and may choose to send invitations and other communications to those addresses via the Services automated invitation system. Recipients of invitations from fubar may contact fubar to request the removal of their information from our database. Members may also invite friends to fubar using their social media accounts.

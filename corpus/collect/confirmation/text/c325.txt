OPSIS TV PRIVACY POLICY
The OPSIS TV Privacy Policy is designed to help you understand how we collect and use the personal information you decide to share, and help you make informed decisions when using OPSIS TV, located at  https://www.opsistv.com/ and its directly associated domains and social network presence (collectively, “Opsis TV” or “Website”).
We strongly advise that you read our Privacy Policy in its entirety.
By using or accessing OPSIS TV, you are accepting the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not accept these practices, please do not subscribe to OPSIS TV, or unsubscribe if you are already a subscriber.
If you have questions or concerns regarding this statement, you should contact our privacy staff at contact@opsistv.com
The Information We Collect
When you visit OPSIS TV you provide us with two types of information: personal information you knowingly choose to disclose that is collected by us and Website use information collected by us as you interact with our Website.
When you register with OPSIS TV, you provide us with certain personal information, such as your name, your email address, your telephone number, your address, your gender, schools attended and any other personal or preference information that you provide to us.
When you enter OPSIS TV, we collect your browser type and IP address. This information is gathered for all OPSIS TV visitors. In addition, we store certain information from your browser using “cookies.” A cookie is a piece of data stored on the user’s computer tied to information about the user. We use session ID cookies to confirm that users are logged in. These cookies terminate once the user closes the browser. By default, we use a persistent cookie that stores your login ID (but not your password) to make it easier for you to log in when you come back to Opsis TV. You can remove or block this cookie using the settings in your browser if you want to disable this convenience feature.
When you use OPSIS TV, you may set up your personal profile and perform searches and queries. We collect this information so that we can provide you the service and offer personalized features. In most cases, we retain it so that, for instance, you can update your profile. When you update information, we usually keep a backup copy of the prior version for a reasonable period of time to enable reversion to the prior version of that information.
You post your own private or personal information on the Website at your own risk. Although we allow you to set privacy options that limit access to your pages, please be aware that no security measures are perfect or impenetrable. We cannot control the actions of other Users with whom you may choose to share your pages and information. Therefore, we cannot and do not guarantee that information you post on the Website will not be viewed by unauthorized persons. We are not responsible for circumvention of any privacy settings or security measures contained on the Website. You understand and acknowledge that, even after removal, copies of user information may remain viewable in cached and archived pages or if other Users have copied or stored your information.
If you choose to offer a OPSIS TV subscription to a friend, we will ask you for information needed to send the gift subscription, such as your friend’s email address. We will automatically send your friend a one-time email or instant message with information about how to create an account on the platform. OPSIS TV stores this information to send this one-time invitation and to track the success of our referral program. Your friend may contact us at  contact@opsistv.com  to request that we remove this information from our database.
OPSIS TV may also collect information about you from other sources, such as newspapers, blogs, instant messaging services, and other users of the OPSIS TV service through the operation of the service (e.g., photo tags) in order to provide you with more useful information and a more personalized experience.
By using OPSIS TV, you are consenting to have your personal data transferred to our servers and processed.
Use of Information Obtained by Opsis TV
When you register with OPSIS TV, you create your own profile. We may occasionally use your name and email address to send you notifications regarding new services offered by OPSIS TV that we think you may find valuable.
Profile information is used by OPSIS TV primarily to be presented back to and edited by you when you access the service.
OPSIS TV may send you service-related announcements from time to time through the general operation of the service.
Generally, you may opt out of such emails by clicking Unsubscribe in an e-mail, though OPSIS TV reserves the right to send you notices about your account even if you opt out of all voluntary email notifications.
OPSIS TV may use information in your profile without identifying you as an individual to third parties. We do this for purposes such as aggregating how many people in a network like a video and personalizing advertisements and promotions so that we can provide you with OPSIS TV. We believe this benefits you. You can know more about the world around you and, where there are advertisements, they’re more likely to be interesting to you.
Sharing Your Information with Third Parties
We do not provide contact information to third party marketers without your permission. We share your information with third parties only in limited circumstances where we believe such sharing is 1) reasonably necessary to offer the service, 2) legally required or, 3) permitted by you. When you use OPSIS TV, certain information you post or share with third parties (e.g., a friend or someone in your network), such as photos, videos, listings or other information, may be shared with other users in accordance with the privacy settings. All such sharing of information is done at your own risk. Please keep in mind that if you disclose personal information on social media or when posting comments, messages, photos, videos, listings or other items, this information may become publicly available.
Links
OPSIS TV may contain links to other websites or be accessed through other websites. We are of course not responsible for the privacy practices of other web sites. We encourage our users to be aware when they leave our site to read the privacy statements of each and every web site that collects personally identifiable information. This Privacy Policy applies solely to information collected by OPSIS TV.
Google Analytics
We use a tool called “Google Analytics” to collect information about use of this site. Google Analytics collects information such as how often users visit this site, what pages they visit when they do so, and what other sites they used prior to coming to this site. We use the information we get from Google Analytics only to improve this site. Google Analytics collects only the IP address assigned to you on the date you visit this site, rather than your name or other identifying information. We do not combine the information collected through the use of Google Analytics with personally identifiable information. Although Google Analytics plants a permanent cookie on your web browser to identify you as a unique user the next time you visit this site, the cookie cannot be used by anyone but Google. Google’s ability to use and share information collected by Google Analytics about your visits to this site is restricted by the  Google Analytics Terms of Use . You can prevent Google Analytics from recognizing you on return visits to this site by disabling cookies on your browser.
Changing or Removing Information
Access and control over most personal information on OPSIS TV is readily available through the profile editing tools. OPSIS TV users may modify or delete any of their profile information at any time by logging into their account. You must sign in with your personal login and personal password, and then go to  https://videos.opsistv.com/profile . Information will be updated immediately.
Individuals who wish to deactivate their OPSIS TV account may do so on their account page. Removed information may persist in backup copies for a reasonable period of time but will not be available to other subscribers to OPSIS TV. Once you have deactivated your OPSIS TV account, you will not be able to access your profile page. Should you want to make sure that your data has been removed, you can contact us at contact@opsistv.com to receive a confirmation whether it has been removed from all our files or that it is still present in backup copies.
Where you make use of the communication features of the service to share information with other individuals on OPSIS TV, however, (e.g., sending a personal message to another OPSIS TV user) you generally cannot remove such communications.
Sensitive Information Retained by our Partners
When you have activated your OPSIS TV account, you have agreed to provide your bank or credit card details to our payment service provider, Stripe. OPSIS TV neither requests nor keeps this information on file. Stripe’s Privacy Policy is available at  https://stripe.com/privacy .
To stream our videos, OPSIS TV has chosen OKAST-Flamefy a third-party provider performing subscription-based video-on-demand services. Okast and Flamefy belong to MEI Group. You can access their respective Privacy Policies at  https://okast.tv/cgu/  and  https://www.flamefy.com/privacy .
Should you have any problem in accessing or modifying your personal information held by Stripe, Okast or Flamefy, please contact us at  contact@opsistv.com so that we can assist you.
Security
OPSIS TV takes appropriate precautions to protect our users’ information. Your account information is located on a secured server behind a firewall. When you enter sensitive information (such as credit card number or your password), we encrypt that information using secure socket layer technology (SSL). (To learn more about SSL, go to http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secure_Sockets_Layer). Because email and instant messaging are not recognized as secure communications, we request that you not send private information to us by email or instant messaging services.

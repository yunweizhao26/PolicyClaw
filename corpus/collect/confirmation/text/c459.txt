As a result data protection polices for the above two entities take precedence over any of the information below.
Member’s information is stored by these entities and falls directly under their responsibility.
By interacting with the club your data may be stored on services outside of the services maintained by the two entities named above and the following information is related to those services only.

Data storage 
In all cases, your data is stored in safe and secure systems.  Personal data is kept for a one year period (Normally September to September), except in limited cases such as incident report forms which are kept as per external legal requirements. Emails from our alumni and GMIT members are keep until requested they are left.
You data is stored either in G-suite servers (Above), Blacknight hosting or Digital Ocean.
Right to be forgotten 
As per GDPR guidelines, you have a right to request the club removes all personal information we have stored on you within 14 days. You can submit this request by emailing both  committee@nuigmc.com & webmaster@nuigmc.com
Data breaches 
As per GDPR guidelines you will be informed within 48 hours if your personal data has be breached.

Disclaimer
Club members must note that our outdoor activities are of an extreme nature and can be dangerous. Participants should be aware of these risks and accept responsibility for their own action and involvement. As a result the club committee will make a final call on all decisions regarding involvement in these activities and maintain the right to prevent people participating in an event. If you have any further comments or questions please contact the committee directly at committee@nuigmc.com.

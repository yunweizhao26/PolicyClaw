Last updated: January 26, 2026 Word Invaders ( we, us, our ) is a word-game experience available on the web and as mobile apps for iOS and Android. We take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights. We designed Word Invaders to collect as little data as possible. We do not collect, store, or process any of the following: - No names, email addresses, or phone numbers - No account creation - No GPS or precise location data - No contact lists - No photos, videos, or media - No biometric or health data - No financial information - No personal profiles tied to your real-world identity Your gameplay results exist primarily on your device unless you explicitly share them or submit a score to the daily leaderboard. The game stores small pieces of data locally not sent to us including: - Daily high scores - Your past personal bests - Your chosen game mode (slow/regular/expert) - The daily seed used to generate the day s letters This data never leaves your device unless you manually delete it or uninstall the app. If you play in a mode that reports daily high scores, the game may send the following anonymous information to our server: - Score - Game mode - Date - Daily seed (to verify fairness) No personal or device-identifiable information is included. Scores cannot be linked to any individual. Word Invaders uses Apple App Store and Google Play billing systems, along with RevenueCat, to process purchases and restore entitlements. Purchase information is used only to unlock paid features and restore access on your device. We do not receive or store your payment details. This information may include: - Device type and operating system - Approximate location (city-level or region-level only; no GPS) - IP address - App usage data - Advertising identifiers (IDFA on iOS, AAID on Android) These identifiers are used only for serving contextual ads, measuring ad performance, and preventing fraud. They are not used for cross-app tracking or linked to your identity. Paid users do not receive ads, and no advertising-related data is collected from users who have purchased the ad-free version.

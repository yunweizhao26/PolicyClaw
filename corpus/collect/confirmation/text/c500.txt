CinemaCam Privacy Policy Last updated: March 19, 2026 CinemaCam ("the App") is a cinematic camera application for iPhone. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the App. Data We Do Not Collect CinemaCam does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. Specifically: - No account or login is required - No analytics or tracking tools are used - No data is sent to external servers - No advertising identifiers are accessed - No location data is collected Device Permissions The App requests the following permissions solely to provide its core functionality: - Camera required to capture photos and videos - Microphone required to record audio during video capture - Photo Library required to save captured photos and videos to your device All captured media is saved directly to your device and never uploaded anywhere. Third-Party Services CinemaCam does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics services, or advertising networks. Children's Privacy The App does not collect any data from any users, including children under the age of 13.

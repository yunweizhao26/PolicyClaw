- Right to Restrict Processing: Request restriction of processing of your personal information

If you are a California resident, you have certain rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA):

- Right to Limit Use: Limit the use and disclosure of sensitive personal information

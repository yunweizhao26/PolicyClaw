Which rule does the policy apply?

Each item shows two rules extracted from the same privacy policy: rule 1 permits a practice (ALLOW) and rule 2
prohibits it (DENY). condition_predicate is the formula of each rule's condition; "[untranslated: ...]" is text the
formula does not express, and "true" means no condition. The policy sections the rules came from follow the item.

Consider a situation in which both rules could apply: the practice and the data they share, under circumstances in
which the conditions of both rules hold, as far as the text allows. Read the sections and decide what the policy
says happens in that situation. Label the item with exactly one of:
- allow: the text permits the practice there, for example because rule 2 covers a narrower case that excludes this
  situation, or because rule 1 states an exception to rule 2 that holds here.
- deny: the text prohibits the practice there, for example because rule 2 carves a narrower case out of rule 1
  (children, sensitive data, a user who opted out) or states a prohibition that rule 1's permission does not override.
- no_overlap: according to the text, the two rules can never apply to the same situation, although their formulas
  suggest they can.
- contradiction: the text plainly says that the practice happens (or may happen) and that it does not, for the same
  situation, and nothing in the text says which statement governs. Read literally, the two statements cannot both be
  true there.
- unresolved: both statements apply to the situation and the text does not say which governs, but the conflict
  arises from general or vague wording rather than from two plainly opposite statements.
- different_practice: the rules are about different practices (different data, recipient, purpose, or party), so no
  situation falls under both.
- extraction_error: one rule is not supported by the text, so the question does not arise.

Also say which rule the text treats as the narrower one ("1", "2", or "neither"), quote the words of the text that
decide the label, and give a reason of at most 40 words.

INFORMATION WE COLLECT FROM YOU
Most of the information that we collect about you comes from you or your device when you play our Games, interact with third-party ads in our Games, or interact with our ads in apps or websites of others. Other than that, we may receive your information when you contact us directly via email.
Some data is provided directly by you, whereas other data is collected automatically.
We follow the data minimization standards when collecting information, meaning that we'll only collect information necessary for a certain purpose and not reuse it for a different, undisclosed purpose.
Information you provide directly
If you decide to contact us directly via email, we will use your data solely to respond to your inquiry. We delete all associated personal information from any inquiries received from Children promptly after replying to the inquiry. You may also be asked to participate in a survey or testing of our Games - which is all voluntary; please read more about this in the section
Participation in the testing and surveys

If you are in any way engaging with User Generated Content, note that no information is collected from you from the Game; please read more information about this in our Social Media Engagement Policy, accessible at
https://talkingtomandfriends.com/social-policy

For some of our Games we collect information about the nickname you choose or information about the name you give to your virtual pet and your rating of the Game (if any). Please do not use your real name. Please note that if you are a Child (as defined below) your nickname is only shown to you and not to any other players in order to provide an additional layer of safety.
Information collected automatically
The information we automatically collect about you relates to the type of device you are using, information that helps us identify your device, how you play our Games, how you interact with our ads, and third-party ads. This includes your approximate geolocation and online identifiers, which do not identify you personally but may uniquely identify your device, and other device data. We may also collect information from app store platforms, partners and other third parties, such as information about your interests and in-app purchases (but we never receive your payment or credit card information).
We also ask you to submit, and we may process, other non-personally identifiable information that may be personal to you - like your year of birth.

SPECIAL FEATURES IN SOME OF OUR GAMES
In some of our Games there is an option for the virtual character to repeat back what you said to it. Before doing so, you will always be asked to give permission to access the microphone. Please note that none of our Games have the option to record audio - you can learn more below.
Learn more
All of our Games that offer a feature in which the virtual character repeats back what you said will always ask in advance for your permission to access the microphone on your device. If you decide to give the permission, the virtual character will be able to repeat what you say to it. Please note that our Games do not have a function to record audio, so what you say to the virtual character is not stored on our servers, it is only used by the Game to repeat to you in real time. If you decide not to give us the permission to access the microphone on your device, the virtual character will not be able to repeat after you, but you can continue playing the Game and use other features.
In the US, some of our Games offer you the option to connect your device to an entertainment device, i.e. a talking, animatronic streamer GameBud™ Talking Tom® (the "Entertainment Device"). To read more about this, please expand this section.
Learn more
Please note that privacy practices of your Entertainment Device are not subject to this privacy policy. Please visit Huge Play's privacy policy available at
http://gamebud.com/talkingtom/privacy
to learn more about privacy practices of your Entertainment Device. All of our Games that offer such a connection feature will offer users an option to connect to the Entertainment Device in the Game Settings. To connect to your Entertainment Device, our Game will need to use the Bluetooth on your device to find your Entertainment Device. On iOS we will ask you for your permission to access the Bluetooth in advance. On Android, we will be automatically able to scan for the Entertainment Device if your Bluetooth is turned on, if not we will ask you to allow us to turn on the Bluetooth on your device. If you decide to give us permission, your Entertainment Device will be able to connect to our Game and react to your achievements and progress in the Game. If you decide not to give us permission, the game connection function of your Entertainment Device will not work.

We always pay special attention to ensure that we process your information lawfully in accordance with one of the legal bases set out under Europe data protection laws. We have appointed a data protection officer and a UK representative to help us ensure compliance with Europe data protection laws. Find out how to reach out to them in the Contact us section below.

Legal bases
In each case where we process your personal data, we do so lawfully in accordance with one of the legal bases set out under Europe data protection law.
The legal bases that we rely upon are the following:
Contractual Necessity.
This covers information that is processed by us in order to provide you with our Games you have downloaded - and that we have agreed to provide to you, that is to deliver and improve the Games, to provide customer support, to deliver special features in our Games (such as in-app chat feature, leaderboards), or to deliver content of the in-app purchase you make.
Legitimate Interests.
This covers information that is processed by us for the purposes that can be reasonably expected within the context of your use of our Games to pursue our legitimate interests in order to ensure you have the best experience when playing our Games, to make sure your information is secure and to provide you our Games free of charge or at a low cost. We pay special attention to your data protection rights, making sure that your data protection rights are not overridden by our legitimate interests. We rely on our legitimate interests for data processing for analytics, attribution and fraud prevention, push-notifications, cross-promotion, and contextual advertising purposes.
Consent.
When we ask for your consent to use your data for a particular purpose, we will make this clear at the point of collection and we will also make clear how you can withdraw your consent. We will ask for your consent before sharing any information with the advertising partners for the purposes of interest-based advertising and before conducting any questionnaires.
Legal Obligation.
This covers information that is processed by us to comply with a legal obligation.
International transfer
We will transfer your personal information only when the transfer is based on one of the following safeguards:
To a country with adequate data protection.
We will transfer your personal information outside Europe to a country that ensures an adequate level of data protection in accordance with European data protection legislation.
Based on standard contractual clauses.
We will transfer your personal information only to such companies outside Europe if we have entered into standard contractual clauses with them. Standard contractual clauses are adopted by the European Commission or the UK Information Commissioner in accordance with European data protection legislation and are deemed to offer sufficient safeguards with respect to the protection of the privacy and fundamental rights and freedoms of individuals (you can obtain a copy of the clauses by contacting us via
[email protected]
).
Based on an approved certification mechanism.
We will transfer your personal information only to such companies outside Europe if they are certified by an approved certification mechanism pursuant to Europe data protection legislation.

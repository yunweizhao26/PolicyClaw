We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to track activity on our Service and hold certain information.

Session Cookies: We use Session Cookies to operate our Service.
Preference Cookies: We use Preference Cookies to remember your preferences and various settings.
Security Cookies: We use Security Cookies for security purposes.
User Uploaded Photos
Some features of our Service allow users to upload photos in order to generate personalized images using artificial intelligence.
Photos uploaded by users may contain personal data, including images of faces. These photos are collected and processed for the purpose of providing the image generation functionality of the Service.
Uploaded photos are stored in a private cloud storage container that is not publicly accessible. Access to uploaded photos is restricted and may only be granted through tokens issued by our backend server after authorization checks.
AI Image Generation Data
When you use image generation features of the Service, we may process photos and text inputs (such as prompts) in order to generate personalized images.
This processing may involve automated systems and artificial intelligence technologies. In order to provide the image generation functionality, uploaded photos and related inputs may be transmitted to third-party image generation API providers acting on our behalf.
The data is processed only for the purpose of generating images requested by the user.

These identifiers do not directly identify you, and we do not associate them with any personal information you may provide. All data is anonymized, stored internally, and not shared with any third parties.
We use this data solely for internal analytics purposes such as:
Measure website traffic and performance;
Understand how users arrive at and navigate through the Site;
Track aggregated events such as pageviews, sessions, form submissions, downloads, and transactions.
This tracking method operates independently of cookie preferences. In case you consent to use of advanced analytical cookies, personally identifiable data may be processed in accordance with our

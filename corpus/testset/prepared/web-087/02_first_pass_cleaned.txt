2.1.1
When clients use our Hosting and Cloud Services, they provide us with third party data. We only collect this data in accordance with our client's instructions. This means that our clients are in control of the data they upload to our platforms.

How We Use Cookies
We use the following types of Cookies:
Essential Cookies
enable you to use our Website. These Cookies are essential to enable you to browse our Website and use certain features you have specifically requested. Disabling them may prevent you from using certain parts of the Website. For example, without these Cookies, services such as account creation and login functions cannot be provided. These types of Cookies allow us to:
Record that you are logged into your account;
Record when you give consent to our use of Cookies, when applicable;
Provide Services to you and manage transactions, and to remember the information you provided in forms;
Provide the necessary security the Website requires, for example, to monitor traffic flow and visits in order to let us know of potential security threats
We want you to understand these Essential Cookies, and why we use them, but your consent is not required for us to use them, as we use these Cookies only to provide you with services that you have requested.
Preference Cookies
store information such as your preferred language selection, login data, and website preferences. Without these Cookies, our Website may not be able to remember certain choices you've previously made, or personalize your browsing experience by providing you with relevant information. These Cookies can recognize your device and offer you a more personal experience so that you do not have to provide the same information more than once. Disabling these Cookies means that you will be asked to reenter this information every time you use our Website.
Performance Cookies
collect information about how you use our Website such as which pages you visit regularly. They allow us to understand:
Which device and/or website directed you to our site;
Which of our features and their pages you visit most often so that we can use this information to improve their functionality;
Page load and site response times; and
If you get error messages or unexpected behavior.
These cookies may facilitate recording of basic interactions, such as clicks, scrolling, and navigation, but do not capture form entries or directly identifying information, such as your name or email address.
Targeting Cookies
and similar technologies, such as tracking pixels, gather information about your use of our Website so we may improve your experience and provide you with more relevant content. They remember that you've visited our Website and help us to both understand your usage of the site, and to ensure that the advertisements you see across the web are more relevant to you. These Cookies are also used to limit the number of times you see an advertisement, as well as to help measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns. Some of these Cookies are from third parties that collect information about users of our Website (as described above in the "Targeted advertisements or interest-based offers" section) to provide interest-based advertising (on our site and elsewhere) based on users' online activities.

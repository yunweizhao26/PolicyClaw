Cookie Notice
Here at Frontier Airlines, we use cookies, pixels, and other similar technologies (collectively “cookies”) on our Websites and mobile app to help us provide a better experience to our passengers who choose to search for or book a flight with us on FlyFrontier.com or on our mobile app. Our cookies are used to provide us the necessary information to improve the functionality of our Website, and third-party cookies to provide our passengers advertisements about our latest offerings. We use the data collected to provide better services to our passengers.
The types of cookies we use at Frontier Airlines fall into four purpose-based categories: Strictly Necessary Cookies, Performance Cookies, Functional Cookies, and Targeting Cookies.
STRICTLY NECESSARY COOKIES
: Strictly necessary cookies are cookies that are essential for a Website to function properly and provide basic features such as user login and account management. They are classified as strictly necessary because a Website will not work properly without them and turning them off would impede a person’s ability to access information and other services on the Website. These cookies are used to allow the Network to operate its Website in a functional manner.
PERFORMANCE COOKIES
: Performance cookies are cookies used specifically to gather data on how people use a Website, which pages are visited most frequently, or if they receive an error message when using the Website. These cookies only monitor the performance of a Website and how its users are interacting with the Website. (e.g., these cookies can count page visits, how much time a user spends on the Website, and loading speeds to improve performance.)
FUNCTIONAL COOKIES
: Functional cookies help to provide services that users request from the Website. These cookies allow the Network to remember the choices you make (such as your username or language preferences) and provide enhanced, personal features. By remembering user preferences, functional cookies help to boost the Website’s performance and make browsing the Website easier and more convenient for users.
TARGETING COOKIES
: Targeting cookies are used by marketing companies to target and retarget you with advertisements that may interest you based on your browsing habits. These cookies are used to deliver advertisements relevant to your interests. Targeting cookies can store which Websites the user visited, the web pages visited, any products purchased, or other advertisements that were clicked on.

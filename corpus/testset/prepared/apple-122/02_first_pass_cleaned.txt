Privacy
at Transit
When you choose Transit, you’re entrusting us with information about where you are, where you’re going, and how you’re getting there. It’s our responsibility to be good stewards of this information, to safeguard your privacy, and to demonstrate that we are worthy of your trust.

Ask only for what’s necessary
We ask for what we need, nothing more. When we need specific information for a specific purpose, we make it clear and ask in advance. And we never collect data in the background when you aren’t using the app.
Disconnect
the dots
Disconnect the dots
Where you are and where you’ve been can be sensitive information. Which is why we keep location coordinates secure, separate and disconnected from other personal data like your email address and user profile.
Data for public transit, not advertisers
Information about where and how the app is being used helps researchers and public agencies better understand the needs of transit riders. Unlike other navigation apps, we never share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

Why we collect information from you
We collect and process personal information only to fulfill two purposes:
Primary Purpose (supporting you through service delivery)
Help our users plan their trips, avoid delays, and make informed decisions about their travel (this involves collection of data about individual users, sometimes including personal information):
Real-time bus and metro schedules
Trip planning and route suggestions
Service alerts and updates
Fare information and payment options
Secondary Purpose (supporting the provision of public transit)
We cooperate with cities and academics, as well as transit agencies, transit authorities and transit operators (“Public Transit Providers”) so they understand traveller patterns and preferences (using pseudonymous data for statistical analysis that does not require divulging the identity of individual users):
To ensure efficient and effective transport services
For long-term planning and infrastructure development
To help transit providers optimize routes, schedules, and frequencies to better match demand
Types of personal information we collect
We collect and process personal information only to fulfill two purposes:
Personal data we collect and store in your account
Name, email address, billing and shipping addresses, payment card number, date of birth, phone number, and saved locations.
Personal information we collect and store separate from your account
If you consent, we collect your location data (longitude and latitude) when you open the app and track your position while you are riding a public transit vehicle. We do not record your location when the app is not active.
In addition, we collect device information, push notification device identifiers, and app usage information.

Direct collection from you such as:

Other reasons to share your personal information
In case you request assistance with the use of our App, we might share your request and personal information, including your contact information, with third parties as necessary to address your request, such as with transit agencies and shared mobility providers directly involved in the issue.
In case a business transaction is completed or contemplated, any information may be transferred to the corporation that owns Transit or the relevant part of it in order to carry its business. A “business transaction” includes, but is not limited to, a reorganisation, merger, purchase, sale or other acquisition or disposition of a corporation or any of its assets. The entity acquiring personal information as part of a business transaction will be bound to process personal information as set forth in this Privacy Policy.
If we receive a request from law enforcement or if we are otherwise required to comply with a legal obligation, we may have to disclose your personal information. We take the time to verify that the request is lawful, and we share no more than we are required to.
When you purchase or access services from a transit agency or a shared mobility provider through our App, such as in cases of suspected credit card fraud, we will share the information required to ascertain your lawful use of those services (including your payment information, relevant personal information, and any relevant location data).
Security of your personal information
We’ve taken steps to protect your privacy by implementing technical and administrative controls to prevent unauthorized access or disclosure of your personal information.
We adhere to best practices and modern privacy system architecture principles, ensuring that personal information is compartmentalized. This approach limits our ability to create a profile of you.
If you ask us to debug a problem we might have to temporarily link your personal account information with non-account personal information. We have strict internal procedures that monitor those activities.
Your personal information from your account is always encrypted. Transit segregates duties to ensure no single Transit employee can ever decrypt your personal information.
We take several measures to protect your privacy when sharing pseudonymized data with third parties. For example, we truncate geographic coordinates to the level of a city block, we exclude all internal identifiers from shared data sets, and, when sharing trip plans, we prioritize using stop positions instead of a user’s location whenever possible.
Retention of personal information
We retain your personal information until you request its deletion. You can delete your account related personal information by using the “delete account” functionality in the App’s settings menu. Once your account information is deleted, the pseudonymised data previously linked to your account becomes anonymous and is not deleted since it can no longer be linked back to you.
Personal information about children
We do not knowingly process personal information from children under the age of 13. If you have reason to believe that a child under the age of 13 has provided information to us, please contact us. If we learn that we have collected information of a child under 13, we will take steps to delete such information within a reasonable time.
Your rights
The law provides you with rights regarding your personal information. These rights may vary depending on the jurisdiction in which you are located, reside, or are a citizen, and they may not apply to all types of personal information. Most individuals using Transit have the right to access their personal information, and the right to request corrections to their personal information under certain circumstances, such as if the personal information is inaccurate or outdated.
We will authenticate you when you want to exercise your rights.
Depending on the circumstances, the following rights may be applicable to you:
Right to be informed
Your right to be informed is primarily met with this Privacy Policy. From time to time we may provide additional information through email communications or our websites.
Right of access
Please contact us through the “Contact us” button from within the app if you wish to receive a copy of your data.
Right to rectification
If you believe that the personal information we hold about you is misleading, inaccurate, or incorrect, please let us know and we will do our best to make the necessary corrections.
Right to erasure
Please use the “delete account” functionality in the App’s settings menu to erase your account related personal information which automatically anonymizes your non-account data.
Right to restrict processing
The app is designed to give you control of which services you use and what information you provide. Please contact us if you have any questions.
Right to data portability
Please contact us if you wish to receive a copy of your data.
Right to object
You may object to our processing of personal information based on legitimate interests.
Right to withdraw consent
Transit adheres to privacy by design principles. All features are initially disabled, and each user must opt-in to enable features that collect personal information. If you change your mind after enabling certain features, you can revert to the original settings or uninstall the app. Withdrawing consent will only affect future collection of personal information. To delete personal information we already hold, use the “delete account” functionality found within the app.
Rights related to automated decision-making including profiling
We do not perform automated decision making.

Identity Verification, Biometric Information, and Images
Identity Verification and Verification Data
To protect the integrity of the Service and the rewards we pay, we may ask you to verify your identity before we release certain payouts or as part of fraud prevention. Verification may collect (i) a photograph of a government-issued identification document, (ii) photographs of your face, including a selfie, and (iii) a short liveness video that includes video and audio. We and service providers acting on our behalf may analyze this material to confirm that the document is valid, you are a live person, your face matches the identification document, you meet the age requirement, and the same person or document is not associated with prohibited duplicate accounts. This analysis may create facial geometry, face-matching results, liveness results, verification status, risk indicators, and similar derived data that may constitute biometric information or a biometric identifier under applicable law (collectively, “Verification Data”).
Verification may also involve technical, device, network, and interaction information used to evaluate identity, eligibility, integrity, fraud risk, and capture quality. We may combine Verification Data with other information associated with your Account or use of the Service for these purposes.
Notice and consent. Identity verification is available only to users who are at least 18 years old. This Policy is not itself a biometric consent or written release. Where applicable law requires one, we will present an additional point-of-collection notice that identifies the verification provider and describes the data, purposes, disclosures, and retention period, and we will request the required affirmative consent or written release before collection. Verification may be required before we release a payout. If you decline or withdraw required consent, we will stop the processing covered by that consent, subject to legal exceptions, but may be unable to complete verification or release a payout that requires it.
Purpose and disclosure limitations. We use identity documents, facial images, liveness recordings, Verification Data, and verification telemetry for identity and age verification, fraud prevention, duplicate-account detection, security, payout administration, dispute investigation, and legal compliance. We do not use facial images, liveness recordings, or biometric Verification Data for advertising, survey matching, or unrelated marketing. We do not sell, lease, trade, or otherwise profit from biometric identifiers or biometric information. We disclose verification information only to providers supporting verification, fraud prevention, hosting, storage, security, and related operations; as legally required; in a permitted business transfer; or with your consent.
Retention and destruction schedule. We permanently and securely delete identification-document images, facial images, and liveness video and audio no later than 30 days after a verification decision is made. We permanently and securely delete derived biometric Verification Data no later than 4 months after collection. We retain non-biometric verification results and technical or fraud telemetry only as long as reasonably necessary for fraud prevention, security, payout disputes, and legal compliance under the retention criteria described below. We destroy biometric identifiers and biometric information earlier when the initial purpose has been satisfied or within a shorter period required by law. For Illinois residents, they will in all cases be destroyed no later than 3 years after your last interaction with the Service.
Safeguards. We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of Verification Data, restrict access to personnel and providers that require it for the stated purposes, and maintain procedures for responding to security incidents and providing legally required notices.

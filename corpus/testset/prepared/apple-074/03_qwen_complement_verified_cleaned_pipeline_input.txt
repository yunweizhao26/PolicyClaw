Cookies and Managing Your Online and Mobile Preferences
This section describes our use of cookies and other automated means of data collection and how to manage your preferences.
What Are Cookies and Other Automated Means of Data Collection?
“Cookies” are small text files that a website’s server or third parties place on your computer. Cookies provide a means for websites that you visit to track browsing activities conducted with your computer. “Pixel tags” (also known as pixels, web beacons or clear GIFs) are typically transparent graphic images placed on a website or in email messages. These pixel tags are used in combination with cookies to track and measure the interactions of users with websites or services. When we refer to “cookies” in this Policy, we also mean other types of technology used to automatically record certain information when you access the Services from your browser, including pixel tags.
You can choose whether to accept the use of cookies and similar technologies in general by changing the settings on your browser, or by changing specific settings for the Services. However, if you disable cookies and similar technologies, your experience may be diminished and some features may not work as intended.
How Do We Use Cookies and Other Automated Means of Data Collection?
We use these technologies to collect your information when you access or use our websites or Services, including our browser extensions and in our email messages. These technologies can be grouped into four categories:
Strictly Necessary
– As their name suggests, Strictly Necessary cookies are essential for our Services to work properly and so that you receive the rewards and savings you expect. These cookies are necessary for the security and stability of our site, ensuring it operates as expected. They do not collect information that is used for marketing.
Analytics
– We use these cookies to understand how you interact with our Services and engage with supported websites. This information is used to provide and improve your shopping experience, including price drop alerts, coupons, and rewards offers.
Functionality
– These cookies enhance our Services by remembering your choices (for instance, not showing the same notification multiple times in one session) and ensuring smooth site operation. They are also used to test new features.
Advertising
– These cookies help us and our partners display advertising and content that is more relevant to you. They track the types of ads shown and how you interact with them. We sometimes partner with third parties to deliver more relevant advertisements and to compile web statistics.
We use Google Analytics to collect and process certain analytics data and analyze your use of our Services. We also use Google reCAPTCHA to protect pages on capitaloneshopping.com. Both of these services are subject to the

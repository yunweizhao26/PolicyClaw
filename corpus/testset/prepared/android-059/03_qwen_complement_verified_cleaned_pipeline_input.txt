e – Offline Mode
When the game is used in Offline Mode, all processing occurs locally on the user’s device. Gameloft does not collect, transmit, access, or otherwise process any personal information or device information while Offline Mode is active. No tracking technologies, analytics tools, or network-based services operate during offline use.
Offline Mode permits access only to game content and features that have been previously installed or stored locally on the device. Features requiring server communication are unavailable, including but not limited to user accounts or login, game save, cloud synchronization, chats, forums, comments or user content, online multiplayer services, online leaderboards, events or limited-time content, downloading updates or additional content not already stored locally, and customer care support.
Upon reconnecting to the Internet and exiting Offline Mode, features that require online access may resume limited data processing in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

Riot Games Candidate & B2B Privacy Notice
Last Modified: September 29, 2025
Riot Games, Inc. is a global gaming company headquartered in Los Angeles with offices and operations around the world. Riot Games, Inc. and its subsidiaries take privacy as seriously as we do play. This Candidate & B2B Privacy Notice (“Notice”) explains what Personal Info we collect during our application and recruitment process, our business to business (B2B) relationships, info about your rights, as well as ways we may use or disclose Personal Info.
By “Personal Info,” we mean any information from or about an identified or identifiable person, including information that Riot Games can associate with an individual person. When we say “Riot Games” (or similar things like “we” or “us”), we’re referring to the Riot Games entity or, where applicable, entities that control and are responsible for your Personal Info.
We have developed this Notice to comply with applicable data protection laws globally. To the extent this Notice conflicts with local laws in your jurisdiction, local laws apply. To learn more about our privacy practices on other Riot Games services, like our games and the collection and disclosure of Personal Info on our websites, please be sure to check out the
Riot Games Privacy Notice
.

1. How We Collect Your Personal Info
This Candidate & B2B Privacy Notice applies to info:
You disclose to us directly (like by submitting an application or talking to our recruiters and interviewers or, where you work for one of our suppliers, customers, partners, agents or investors, when you give it to one of your Riot Games contacts); and
We obtain from others or public sources in connection with:
Our recruitment efforts, such as from background checking agencies (where used as permitted by local laws), external talent database holders (who may collect your info from you, third parties they work with, or from public sources), executive search and recruitment agencies, and from publicly available sources, including any social media platforms you use or other info available online like LinkedIn;
B2B relationships, such as where the company you work for gives it to us (like where you are a contact for services being provided to us).
2. What Personal Info We Collect
The particular kinds of Personal Info we gather often depend on how you interact with us and, for candidates, the role for which you’re being considered. These include:
Contact Info.
We collect names, email addresses, telephone numbers, and similar business or personal contact info to help us communicate with you.
Experience Info.
We collect résumés, CVs, transcripts, cover letters, work samples, and other details about your prior or relevant experience, skills, education, training, or similar background info to support your application and/or our recruitment efforts.
Reference & Background Info.
We collect info from and about any references you provide (it is your responsibility to obtain any necessary consent from your references prior to sharing their Personal Info with us). Where applicable, we may collect info from background checks subject to relevant legal requirements.
Interview Info.
We collect info from any interviews and screenings you may have, including summaries and transcripts using artificial intelligence tools with your consent, as well as details about the type of opening you are or may be pursuing (like current or desired salary, benefits, and similar preferences).
Previous applications/roles.
We ask you to provide and collect info relating to previous applications you have made to Riot Games and/or any previous employment history with Riot Games.
Immigration/visa status.
We collect details of your immigration or visa status.
B2B Info.
We collect tax ID numbers, Social Security numbers, and bank account info to provide payments. We use legal names to run risk assessments.
Demographic & Sensitive Personal Info.
We try to avoid collecting or processing info that reveals your racial or ethnic origin, religious, political or philosophical beliefs, genetic data, biometric data, trade union membership, or info about your health/sex life/sexual orientation (“Sensitive Personal Info”). However, in some circumstances, and where local laws allow, we may collect or request some Sensitive Personal Info for legitimate employment-related purposes where necessary. For example, Personal Info about your racial/ethnic origin, gender, date of birth, and disabilities, as well as photographs, for the purposes of equal opportunities monitoring and diversity and inclusion efforts.
3. How We Use Personal Info
We collect Personal Info to support our application and recruitment process and our B2B relationships, including to:
Process your application (such as adding your Personal Info to our systems);
Communicate with you (like to schedule interview times and dates or in connection with services provided);
Assess your eligibility for our available opportunities or to enter into a contract (including other current or potential future openings at Riot Games you may not have considered);
Measure and improve our recruitment process (like by analyzing hiring trends and enhancing our diversity efforts usually by aggregating your data with others before analyzing it);
Comply with our legal and regulatory obligations (such as submitting required reports, and conducting risk management assessments for B2B relationships);
Verify the Personal Info you submit (including, where applicable, by conducting employment history, background, and reference checks);
In certain cases, help you coordinate immigration, work permit, relocation, travel, or other similar issues;
Perform our business contract with you or where applicable, our business customer, supplier, partner, agent or investor whom you act on behalf of or represent; and
Conduct and develop our business (such as managing our business relationships including account management, business development and marketing in relation to our existing and prospective business suppliers, customers, partners, agents or investors).
We’ll also use Personal Info as we believe is necessary or appropriate under applicable law to protect the rights and property of Riot Games, players, other candidates, our employees, or the public.
If you are accepted for a role the Personal Info collected during the recruitment process will form part of your ongoing staff member record and will be processed in accordance with our GDPR Rioter and Contractor Privacy Notice or Privacy Notice for California Rioters and Contractors, as applicable.
If you are not successful, we may still keep your application to allow us to consider you for other suitable openings within Riot Games in the future and will delete your CV, résumé, and cover letter in line with local retention requirements. Please inform us if you do not want us to do so.
If you work for one of our suppliers, customers, partners, agents or investors, the processing is necessary for our legitimate interests, namely conducting and developing our business.
4. How We Disclose Personal Info and International Transfers
Whenever we permit a third party to access Personal Info, we will implement appropriate measures designed to ensure the Personal Info is used in a manner consistent with this Notice and that the security and confidentiality of the Personal Info is maintained.
As part of the recruitment process, we disclose your Personal Info to hiring managers, interviewers and those directly involved in the recruitment process. As part of the B2B relationship, we disclose your Personal Info with security operations, the legal team, purchasing operations, finance, and those directly involved in the ongoing B2B services. In each case, these individuals may be spread across various offices and locations worldwide. We disclose the Personal Info we collect with our subsidiaries and affiliates, which means your Personal Info could be transferred to or stored in the US, UK, or other locations outside your country of residence.
We use service providers to support our recruitment efforts and B2B relationships. This includes:
recruitment or executive search agencies involved in your recruitment;
data storage, shared services and recruitment platform providers, IT developers and support providers, and providers of hosting services; and
third parties who provide support and advice including in relation to immigration, pre-engagement screening, legal, financial / audit, management consultancy, insurance, health and safety, security and intel, and whistleblowing / reporting issues.
With your consent, we may also utilize an artificial intelligence tool service provider for real-time transcription and note-taking during interviews, so that our interviewers can give you their full attention. Any such tools would not be used for decision-making.
We require these service providers to protect the Personal Info they receive with appropriate security measures and prohibit them from using the Personal Info for their own purposes or outside what is necessary to provide the service. These service providers may be located outside your country of residence or the country where the position for which you applied is located.
We’ll disclose Personal Info to third parties where we have a legitimate interest in doing so or where otherwise permitted by law. For example, we might disclose Personal Info to external parties if you ask us to do so in connection with a relocation, to coordinate travel, or to support you with immigration or similar issues related to your application. We don’t sell or share your Personal Info for cross-context behavioral advertising.
Additionally, we use and disclose Personal Info to comply with our legal obligations and as we believe is necessary or appropriate under applicable law to protect the rights and property of Riot Games, players, other candidates or B2B contacts, our employees, or the public, or in connection with the sale, assignment, or other transfer of all or part of our business.
In some cases, the disclosure described above may result in your Personal Info being transferred internationally, including from the European Economic Area to a country outside it. These countries may have data protection laws that are different to the laws of your country and, in some cases, may not be as protective. However, we have taken appropriate safeguards to require that your Personal Info will remain protected in accordance with this Notice. These include implementing the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses for transfers to countries for which the European Commission has not issued an adequacy decision. The Standard Contractual Clauses can be provided to you upon request to
dpo@riotgames.com
.
5. Categories of Personal Info Collected & Disclosed
In summary, depending on how you interact with us and the role for which you are being considered, the following categories of Personal Info may have been collected and disclosed for a business purpose in the preceding 12 months:
Identifiers
, including name, email address, and telephone number
Characteristics of protected classifications under state or federal law
, including date of birth, age, gender, racial/ethnic origin, disabilities, and medical condition
Audio, electronic, visual, thermal, olfactory, or similar info
, including photographs
Professional or employment-related info
, including résumés, CVs, transcripts, cover letters, work samples, references, background checks, info from interviews and screeners, info relating to previous Riot Games applications, and immigration or visa status
We do not infer characteristics using Sensitive Personal Info, and do not use Sensitive Personal Info beyond the limited business purposes permitted by local laws, including the California Consumer Privacy Act.
6. Security
We use appropriate physical, technical, and organizational security safeguards to help protect your Personal Info from unauthorized access, destruction, use, modification, or disclosure both during transmission and in storage. For example, we use computer systems with limited access in controlled facilities to store Personal Info. We also use technologies like encryption and hashing to protect some of the Personal Info we collect.
However, please note that no server, communications network, or data transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. Our services are no exception and we can’t guarantee the security of any Personal Info transmitted to us.
7. How Long We Keep Personal Info
We’ll generally retain your Personal Info as long as is necessary or appropriate for our recruiting efforts and operating our business (like complying with our legal obligations, managing internal books and records, preventing fraud, resolving disputes, and enforcing our contracts and terms, and for other current and future employment or similar opportunities with Riot Games), unless a longer retention period is permitted or required by law.
If you're successful in your application, we retain the Personal Info you provide during the application process, and Personal Info about your application process, as part of your employee records.
8. Your Rights, Choices & Controls
We’ll collect and use your Personal Info in accordance with your legal rights, such as described in this Notice. Based on your region, you have the rights listed below:
Access:
The right to obtain confirmation as to whether your Personal Info is being processed, to know what Personal Info we have about you, including the categories of Personal Info, the categories of sources from which the Personal Info is collected, the business or commercial purpose for collecting the Personal Info, the categories of persons to whom we disclose the Personal Info, and the specific pieces of Personal Info we have collected about you.
Correction:
The right to have rectified any inaccurate Personal Info concerning you.
Deletion:
The right to delete Personal Info that we have collected from you, subject to certain exceptions.
Restriction:
The right to restrict the processing of your Personal Info in certain cases.
Data portability:
The right, in certain cases, to receive the Personal Info concerning you in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format and/or transmit that Personal Info to another data controller.
Objection:
In some cases required by law, the right to ask us to stop processing your Personal Info.
Right to withdraw consent:
Where your consent is required, you may at any time withdraw such consent by contacting us (though this will not affect the lawfulness of any processing of your Personal Info prior to that).
Website visitors who are using a browser that sends a Do Not Track signal or a browser that is Global Privacy Control compliant will have their signals automatically applied to their consent settings on Riot Games websites.
We encourage you to always make sure that the Personal Info you disclose to us is kept up to date. You may exercise the rights available to you under applicable data protection laws by contacting us at
dpo@riotgames.com
or using the contact details provided in Contact Us below. We may require specific Personal Info from you to help us verify your identity and process your request. If we are unable to verify your identity, we may deny your request.
You may also make a rights request using an authorized agent. If you submit a rights request from an authorized agent who does not provide a valid power of attorney, we may ask the authorized agent to provide proof that you gave the agent signed permission to submit the request to exercise rights on your behalf. In the absence of a valid power of attorney, we may require you to verify your own identity directly with us or confirm to us that you otherwise provided the authorized agent permission to submit the request.
You have the right not to be discriminated or retaliated against for exercising any of these rights. We might not always be able to accommodate your requests in line with our legitimate interests, but if not, we’ll let you know why. You can submit an unresolved complaint to a supervisory authority and we will work with them to address your concern. However, we strongly recommend that you engage with us first and we will endeavor to address any concerns.
9. Legal Basis
Under European data protection law, our legal basis for processing your Personal Info as part of the recruitment process is:
Our legitimate interests (as summarized above in How We Use Personal Info);
To comply with applicable immigration and/or employment laws and regulations;
To take steps prior to entering into an employment or vendor contract with you, where you are considered for employment or a B2B relationship;
In circumstances where you have made the data public;
Where we have your consent to do so. Where we have requested your consent to process your personal data, you have the right to withdraw your consent at any time; and
To protect the rights and interests of Riot Games, our employees, applicants and others, as required and permitted by applicable law.
10. Policy Changes
We’ll update this Notice to reflect changes in technology, law, our business operations, or any other reason we determine is necessary or appropriate. When we make changes, we’ll update the “Last Modified” date above and post it to our careers website. If we make material changes to the ways we process Personal Info, we’ll provide you additional notice as well if we still retain your Personal Info.
We encourage you to check back periodically to review this Notice for any changes since your last visit. This will help ensure you better understand your relationship with Riot Games, including the ways we process your Personal Info.

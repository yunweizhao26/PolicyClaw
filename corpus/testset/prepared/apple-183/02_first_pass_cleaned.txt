PRIVACY POLICY

DinoGame

DinoGame takes the protection of your personal data seriously. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your information in accordance with applicable data protection laws and regulations.

Personal data is collected only where necessary to operate, maintain, secure, analyze, and improve our games, applications, and related services ("Services").

We do not sell your personal information for monetary consideration.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use our Services, how we use that information, how it may be shared, and the choices available to you.

AUTOMATICALLY COLLECTED INFORMATION

When you use our Services, certain information may be collected automatically, including, but not limited to:

- Device type and model
- Device identifiers
- Advertising identifiers, where permitted
- IP address
- Operating system and version
- Application version
- Language and regional settings
- Mobile network information
- Application usage information
- Gameplay activity and progress
- Crash reports and diagnostic information
- Performance and technical information
- Advertising interaction information

We use this information to operate and improve our Services, understand how users interact with our games, fix technical issues, prevent fraud and abuse, provide customer support, and improve the overall user experience.

USER-PROVIDED INFORMATION

Depending on the features available in a particular game or Service, you may voluntarily provide certain information to us.

This may include information such as:

- Player name or nickname
- Account information
- Customer support messages
- Information you provide when reporting a problem
- Information associated with your gameplay or account
- Other information you voluntarily provide to us

We only use this information for purposes related to providing, supporting, maintaining, and improving our Services.

DEVICE IDENTIFIERS

Our Services and third-party service providers may use device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or similar technologies where permitted by applicable law and your device settings.

These identifiers may be used for purposes such as:

- Providing and maintaining the Service
- Analytics and performance measurement
- Advertising and advertising measurement
- Fraud prevention
- Security
- Attribution
- Understanding how users interact with our Services

You may be able to limit or reset advertising identifiers through your device's privacy settings.

Where applicable, we will request any permissions or consent required by the operating system or applicable law before accessing or using certain identifiers.

ANALYTICS

We may use analytics services to help us understand how users interact with our Services.

Analytics providers may collect information such as device information, application usage, gameplay activity, session information, crash information, IP address, and other technical information.

We use this information to:

- Understand how our games are used
- Measure game performance
- Identify crashes and technical problems
- Improve gameplay and user experience
- Evaluate and improve features
- Understand user engagement and retention

Information collected by third-party analytics providers is subject to their respective privacy policies.

ADVERTISING

Some of our Services may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising networks and technology providers.

These third parties may collect or receive certain information from your device, including device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, general device information, advertisement interactions, and application usage information.

This information may be used to:

- Display advertisements
- Measure advertisement performance
- Limit how often advertisements are shown
- Detect advertising fraud
- Measure installs and attribution
- Provide personalized advertising where permitted by applicable law and your privacy choices

Where required, we will request your consent before using information for personalized advertising.

On Apple platforms, where information is used for tracking as defined by Apple, DinoGame will request permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework before such tracking occurs. If permission is not granted, we will not use information for tracking purposes that require such permission.

You may be able to control personalized advertising through your device settings, privacy settings, or consent options provided within our Services.

THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

Our Services may integrate or interact with third-party platforms, software development kits ("SDKs"), analytics providers, advertising providers, cloud services, payment providers, authentication services, and other technology providers.

Depending on the game and platform, these services may include services provided by companies such as Google, Apple, Unity, Meta, advertising networks, analytics providers, attribution providers, and cloud service providers.

These third-party services may collect information directly from your device in accordance with their own privacy policies.

DinoGame does not control the privacy practices of independent third-party service providers. We encourage you to review their privacy policies to understand how they collect and process information.

IN-APP PURCHASES AND PAYMENTS

Our Services may allow you to make in-app purchases.

Payments may be processed by third-party platforms or payment providers, such as the Apple App Store, Google Play, or other authorized payment services.

DinoGame generally does not directly receive or store your complete payment card information when payments are processed by these third-party providers.

We may receive transaction-related information necessary to verify purchases, provide purchased content, prevent fraud, process refunds, and provide customer support.

Such information may include transaction identifiers, purchased items, purchase status, currency, price, and related transaction information.

DOES THE APPLICATION COLLECT PRECISE REAL-TIME LOCATION INFORMATION?

Our Services do not collect precise real-time GPS location information unless a particular feature clearly requires it and appropriate notice and permission are provided.

We may receive approximate location information derived from information such as your IP address. This may be used for purposes including localization, analytics, security, fraud prevention, and compliance with regional requirements.

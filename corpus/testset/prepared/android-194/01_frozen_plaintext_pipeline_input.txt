Once your Data is determined to get erased, your Data will be securely purged from our electronic, manual and other filing systems based on the above criteria and our internal procedures. Our internal policies are committed to be in compliance with the applicable Data Protection Laws.

b. To request correction to your Data that we hold;
c. To request us to stop using your Data for direct marketing; and
d. To make a complaint to a data protection authority about how we are using your Data.
If you wish to access or amend any of your Data we hold, or request that we delete (only applicable where the rights to erasure is required by law) any of your information that is no longer necessary for the provision of our Services, you may contact us in the manner as set forth under the “How to contact us” section.
If you are a user of My HKT, you may exercise your right of access and correction by logging on to your My HKT account where you will be able to view and correct the Data held by us and/or any relevant/applicable members of the HKT Group about you. If you are not a user of My HKT, you may write to our Group Data Protection Officer at the details stated in the “How to contact us” section below.
You may decline to share Data with us and/or withdraw any consents which you may have provided, in which case, we may not be able to provide you with some of our Services.

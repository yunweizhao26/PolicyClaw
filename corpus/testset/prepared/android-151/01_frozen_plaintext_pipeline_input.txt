Privacy Policy
Last updated: September 10, 2026
BABYBUS CO., LTD. and its affiliates (collectively, "BabyBus" or "we") highly respect the privacy of our users, including parents and children (collectively, "you" or "users"). Through this Privacy Policy, we hope to provide you with a clear and comprehensive explanation of what personal information (data) we may collect from you, as well as how we collect, use, disclose, and transfer such information.
This Privacy Policy complies with the requirements of the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act ("COPPA"), the European Union General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR"), and other applicable laws. We may amend this Privacy Policy at any time in accordance with applicable laws; therefore, please read this Privacy Policy periodically to understand how we use your personal information. If we make material changes to this Privacy Policy, we will provide you with appropriate notice as required by law, such as via email.
Definition of "Children":
In this Privacy Policy, pursuant to COPPA, "children" refers to individuals under the age of 13. Pursuant to the GDPR and the laws of EU Member States, "children" refers to individuals under the age of 16 (although some EU Member States may set a lower age threshold, it may not be below 13 years of age). If your jurisdiction defines "children" differently, the applicable local laws shall prevail.

III. Children's Privacy
1. Age Confirmation and Parental Consent Mechanism
We take children's privacy seriously. When users access our services, if they indicate during registration or at key points of operation that they are below the applicable legal age threshold for "children", we will:
(1) Restrict data collection: By default, disable behavioral tracking and personalized advertising features;
(2) Obtain parental consent: Require the provision of a parent's or legal guardian's email address and send a parental consent form (Verifiable Parental Consent) to explain the types of data we will collect and the purposes for which such data will be used;
(3) Confirm consent: The parent or legal guardian must confirm consent by replying to the email. Before obtaining such consent, we will not collect any children's personal data beyond what is permitted by applicable laws (except for technical data necessary for internal operations, such as crash logs).
2. Information We Collect from Children
We may automatically collect the following persistent identifier information generated when children use our apps through the operation of our services (solely to support necessary internal operations and never for behavioral advertising, profiling, or contacting children):
IP address

App Set ID/App Group ID
Internal operations specifically refer to: crash troubleshooting, service stability monitoring, basic data statistics, contextual advertising, security protection, and compliance with legal requirements. We will not use the above data for any other purposes.
3. Retention, Access, and Deletion of Children's Data
We retain children's data only for the period reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected (i.e., providing app services and supporting internal operations).
Parents or children have the right to refuse further use or collection of children's information at any time. If you (the parent) wish to access, view, or delete your children's information, you may contact us via email at [privacy@babybus.com].
To protect children's privacy, we may require parental identity verification information (such as records confirming parental consent via email). Upon receipt of a deletion request, we will permanently delete children's data from our active databases within the timeframe required by applicable laws. However, we may retain de-identified records in backups where necessary for legal compliance or security auditing purposes.
IV. How We Disclose the Information We Collect
We may share or disclose your personal information with a limited number of carefully selected third parties, including:
1. Our Affiliates
We may share information with our affiliates (such as BABYBUS (FUJIAN) NETWORK TECHNOLOGY CO., LTD. and JAPAN BABYBUS CO., LTD.). These companies will use your personal information in the same manner as described in this Privacy Policy. For users subject to the GDPR, the legal basis for data processing by our affiliates is our legitimate interests or your consent, as applicable.
2. Advertising Partners
If you are a parent or an adult (i.e., not a child), we may disclose your personal information to third-party advertising partners based on an appropriate legal basis (if applicable), to assist with measuring the effectiveness of our marketing campaigns.
Please note that we will never disclose information collected from children to advertising partners, nor will we transfer children's data to advertising partners in connection with child-directed services.
3. Disclosure to Protect Us or Others
If we believe in good faith that accessing, retaining, or disclosing any information related to you to external parties is necessary or appropriate, we may do so in order to: comply with law enforcement or national security requests and legal proceedings (such as court orders or subpoenas); protect the rights, property, or safety of you, us, or others; enforce our policies or contracts; collect outstanding debts; or assist in the investigation or prosecution of suspected or actual illegal activities to the extent permitted by law.
4. Disclosure in Connection with Business Transfers
If we are involved in an actual or proposed merger, acquisition, financing due diligence process, reorganization, bankruptcy, receivership, asset purchase or sale, or transfer of services to another provider, your information may be transferred as part of such transaction, to the extent permitted by applicable law and/or contractual obligations.

V. How We Protect Your Personal Information
We take appropriate physical, technical, and administrative measures based on currently available technologies to ensure that your personal data is not lost, accessed or used without authorization, altered, or disclosed.
Although we have implemented measures designed to protect data security, no website or internet transmission is completely secure. We cannot guarantee that unauthorized access, hacking, data loss, or other security incidents will never occur. In the event of a security incident, we will notify affected users and regulatory authorities in a timely manner as required by law. To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, we shall not be liable for any unauthorized access, use, disclosure, or loss of personal information.
By using our services or providing us with personal information, and to the extent permitted by law, you consent to our communicating with you electronically regarding security, privacy, and administrative matters related to your use of our services. If we discover that our security systems have been compromised, we may attempt to notify you by posting a notice within the services, sending a notice by mail, or emailing you.

VII Your Rights
This section varies depending on your jurisdiction. Please refer to the applicable provisions based on your actual circumstances.

You may contact us at [privacy@babybus.com] to exercise the following basic rights:

Right to Object to Processing: You have the right to object when we process data based on our legitimate interests.

Right to Data Portability: You have the right to receive an electronic copy of the personal data you have provided (in a structured, commonly used format).

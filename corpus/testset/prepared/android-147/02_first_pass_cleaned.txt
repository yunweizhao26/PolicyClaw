Personal Information
refers to data that relates to you as an identified or identifiable natural person. It may include, for example, your name, your e-mail address or your gender. Anonymous or de-identified information, which we are not able to identify you, does not qualify as Personal Information.
We collect Personal Information that you provide to us. We also collect a variety of information indirectly, through your use of our Games and Websites. This information may include, but is not limited to, anonymised browser and device information (both software and hardware), data collected through automated electronic interactions, application usage data, demographic information, geographic, geo-location information, statistical and aggregated information. Statistical or aggregated information does not directly identify a specific person, but it may be derived from Personal Information.

5. COOKIES AND SIMILAR TRACKING TECHNOLOGIES
Among other standard internet technologies, we use cookies to improve our Websites and Games. A cookie is a small text file placed on your computer or device when you visit our Websites or access the Games, and it helps us enhance functionality and performance by remembering your actions and preferences so that, for example, you do not need to re-enter login details each time you visit.
Types of Cookies:
Session cookies: These are temporary and are deleted when you close your browser or app.
Persistent cookies: These remain on your device for a set period and are used to remember your preferences for future visits.
Third-Party cookies: Set by external domains or services, such as advertisers, that track user behaviour across multiple sites or apps.
When you visit our Websites or access our Games from within the European Economic Area (EEA), a cookie banner will appear asking for your consent. You can manage your cookie preferences by clicking on "Manage Cookie Preferences" in the banner. Once you make your selection, we will store your choices in a cookie on your device to remember your preferences for future visits. To learn more, please check out our
Cookie notice
.

If you do not wish to receive targeted advertising on your mobile device, you can adjust your device settings to limit tracking across applications. On Android devices, this can be done by enabling the “Opt out of Ads Personalisation” setting. On iOS devices, tracking permissions are managed in the Privacy and Security settings, where you may disable the option “Allow Apps to Request to Track” or adjust permissions for individual applications. In addition, some Ad Partners may offer their own opt-out mechanisms, which are described in our
Ad Partner list

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but some parts of the Services may then not function properly. You can also adjust your browser settings to limit certain tracking by means of cookies.

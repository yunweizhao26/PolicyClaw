How We Use the Information
Contact information
We use contact information you provide us to communicate with you, to fulfill requests, and to maintain business relationships. We may use it to deliver promotional and informational email messages to you.
Internet Protocol Address
We automatically receive your Internet Protocol address (IP address) when you visit a website for which we provide advertising services. The website sends us your IP address and asks us to provide an advertisement to display to you. We use that IP address to ask advertisers to bid on the chance to display ads to you, and to help those advertisers deliver ads to you.
We also use your IP address to determine your general geographic location, to detect and fight fraud, and to be sure that we are complying with regional requirements and preferences.
Information about your browser or device
When we provide services to advertisers, we use information about your browser and device to help advertisers and their business partners deliver ads that display correctly to you, to report on our services and ad performance, and to help advertisers analyze the performance of their ads.
When we provide services to publishers, we use this information to assist publishers in requesting advertisements from advertisers. We assist publishers in communicating this information to advertisers and their business partners so that they may receive advertisements to display on their web pages.
Cookie IDs, Mobile Advertising IDs, or other user IDs
We use these to help with ad delivery, including by helping websites and other media properties communicate to advertisers and their partners information about a user’s activity and device, the website or media property, and the advertising opportunity; to ensure ads are not shown too many times to the same device; to detect fraud and protect security; for reporting on and analysis of the performance of our services and the advertisements, and to improve our services.
We also use the information we collect through these technologies to help us detect fraud and for other security purposes.
In some cases, we use these technologies to help us remember and honor your choices.
Information about your choices
We use information about whether you wish to opt out of targeted advertising, or whether you wish to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information, to allow us and the website to honor your choices and to ask our partners to do so. We do not use this information for any other purpose. We use information about your exercise of rights and choices we receive from companies we work with to help them honor those rights and choices.
Information about use of our technology
When we license technology to websites and provide technology related services, such as maintenance and assistance with integration, we may have access to user device information in the course of providing such services for websites. We use this information to provide services to our customers, the websites, and to maintain and improve our technology and our services.
Information about Email Receipt
We use information about whether our emails are opened to determine which messages are most effective and understand how our customers respond.
Website Activity
We collect information about how you use our web pages and the pages of publisher websites where we provide Services, when you interact with us there. We use this information to ensure you see relevant ads and do not see any particular ad too often. We also use this for analysis to provide business insights, fraud detection, and security, and to improve our products and services.

Legal Basis for our Processing of Your Personal Information
. Below are the types of lawful basis that we will rely on to process your Personal Information:
Consent.
Consent means your freely given, specific, informed agreement to processing, shown by an affirmative action you have taken to communicate that you agree to your personal information being processed. We rely on Consent as the legal process for placing Tools on your browser or device, and for using them to store information on your device. We also rely on Consent as the legal basis for providing personalized advertising. When we provide Services that facilitate the delivery of personalized advertising, including the use of a your behavior across web sites and over time, or the development of a profile, we also rely on Consent. If we provide Services that make it possible to deliver personalized content to you, we will rely on Consent as the legal basis for the data processing required. For Website analytics we sometimes rely on Consent, and sometimes legitimate interest, depending on the data collected, and legal basis used by Sellers and Buyers.
Legitimate Interest
means the interest of our business in conducting and managing our business to enable us to give you the best service/product and the best and most secure experience. We make sure we consider and balance any potential impact on you (both positive and negative) and your rights before we process your Personal Information for our legitimate interests. We do not use your Personal Information for activities where our interests are overridden by the impact on you (unless we have your consent or are otherwise required or permitted to by law). We also do not use your Personal Information where such interests are overridden by your data-protection interests or fundamental rights and freedoms.
We sometimes rely on our legitimate interest to use limited information (such as the content you are currently viewing, or your general geographic area) to provide basic ads to you. We do not believe that the use of this general information, which is not used to create a profile of you, presents substantial risks to you as a data subject. Any risks are outweighed by the benefits all parties derive from using basic advertising to support the delivery of Content.
When a Seller relies on its legitimate interest to process limited personal information for the delivery of basic ads, instead of obtaining your consent to do so, we too rely on our legitimate interest to perform the services that make it possible to provide basic ads. Similarly when a Seller uses limited personal information to select content, we too rely on our legitimate interest to do so. In both of these cases there is little risk to your rights and freedoms based on the limited personal information used.
We rely on legitimate interest to determine how many times an advertisement has been presented to you so that you do not see it too often, and to detect fraud so that Sellers only pay for legitimate advertising views. In both of these cases, the risk to the data subject based on the limited data used is smaller than the benefit that we, the Sellers, Buyers, and Partners, and you the data subject, derive from maintaining the usefulness and integrity of the advertising services we provide. Similarly, we rely on our legitimate interest to measure the performance of advertising. This too helps to ensure the quality and integrity of the advertising Services without adding any substantial risk to your rights and freedoms.
We rely on our legitimate interest to use the data we are processing when we perform Services to support, develop, and improve our Services. This does not present any additional risk to You and it helps us provide high quality Services that benefit you and the Sellers, Buyers, and Partners who rely on us.

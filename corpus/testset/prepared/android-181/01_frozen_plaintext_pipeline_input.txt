II. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?
Personal information or personal data means any information about an individual from which that person can be identified, either directly or indirectly. It does not include information where the identity has been removed (“Anonymous Data”). We and our third-party service providers (such as Google Play, and potentially other third-party service providers) may collect, use, store, and transfer different kinds of Personal Information about you, which we have grouped together as follows:
Identity Information
includes real name, nickname, username, email address, or similar identifier (including, if you have shared them with us, your user display name and/or user image thumbnail from online services).
Contact Information
includes billing address, postal code, delivery address, email address, and telephone numbers (and job title and company name, if applicable).
Financial Information
includes bank account and payment card details.
Transaction Information
includes details about payments to and from you and other details of products or services you have purchased from us.
Technical Information
includes internet protocol (IP) address; operating system/version/language and platform; device type; device manufacturer; device model; screen size/density; RAM size, storage size, free/unused storage; connection type (e.g., WiFi, 4G, etc.); carrier identifiers (SIM code, mobile country code, mobile network code); installed applications/apks and their versions (including local file path), permissions, publishers, and installation sources; browser package name; and visited websites/URLs (if you have enabled anti-phishing).
Online Identifiers
include an online identifier or other persistent identifier that can be used to recognize a person, family, or device, over time and across different services, including, but not limited to, a device identifier (such as IMEI); cookies, beacons, pixel tags, mobile ad identifiers (including Google Advertising ID) or similar technology; customer number/internal IDs, unique pseudonym or user “alias”; telephone numbers, or other forms of persistent or probabilistic identifiers (i.e., the identification of a person or device to a more-likely-than-not degree of certainty) that may be used to identify a particular person or device.
Internet or Network Information
includes browsing history (URLs), search history, and information regarding a person’s interaction with an internet website, application, or advertisement; also details of the connected WiFi network (if WiFi security has been enabled in some of our applications).
Profile Information
includes your email address and password, purchases or orders made by you, your interests, preferences, product feedback, and survey responses.
Usage Data
includes information about how you use our Services, products, and services, including all areas of our Services that you visit or use and the time of day you visited the Services, among other information.
Marketing and Communications Information
includes your preferences in receiving marketing from us and our third parties and your communication preferences.
Location Information
includes information collected or inferred about your location using a variety of technologies, such as IP address and connected or nearby Wi-Fi networks, or precise location derived from GPS coordinates or telemetry data (including latitude/longitude).
We also collect, use, and share Aggregated Information, such as statistical and demographic information, for the purposes of profiling our users and improving our tools, or offering tools that better serve our users. Aggregated Information may be derived from your Personal Information but is not considered Personal Information in law as this information does not directly or indirectly reveal your identity. For example, we may aggregate your Usage Data to calculate the percentage of users accessing a specific website feature. However, if we combine or connect Aggregated Information with your Personal Information so that it can directly or indirectly identify you, we treat the combined information as Personal Information which will be used in accordance with this Privacy Policy. We do not collect any Special Categories of Personal Data about you (this includes details about your race or ethnicity, religious or philosophical beliefs, sex life, sexual orientation, political opinions, trade union membership, information about your health, and genetic and biometric data). Nor do we collect any information about criminal convictions and offenses.

XII. EUROPEAN RESIDENTS YOUR EUROPEAN PRIVACY RIGHTS
PSafe is committed to complying with the rights of personal data subjects provided for in Articles 13 to 22 of the GDPR[cite: 440, 441].
Your Rights
Explanation
Right to be Informed (Art. 13 & 14)
You have the right to receive clear and transparent information about the processing of your personal data, including the purposes, data categories, retention period, and other relevant information.
Right of Access (Art. 15)
You can request a copy of the Personal Information PSafe has collected about you. This includes details about the processing purposes, data categories, recipients, storage period, and more.
Right to Rectification (Art. 16)
You can ask PSafe to rectify your personal data if it is inaccurate.
Right to Erasure (Art. 17)
You can request the erasure of your personal data when it is no longer necessary, you withdraw consent, you object to the processing and there are no overriding legitimate grounds, the data has been unlawfully processed, or erasure is required for legal compliance.
Right to Restriction of Processing (Art. 18)
You may request the restriction of processing in specific situations, such as when you contest the accuracy of the data, the processing is unlawful, or you need the data for legal claims.
Right to Data Portability (Art. 20)
You may request to receive your data in a structured format and, where technically feasible, have your data transmitted directly from one controller to another.
Right to Object (Art. 21)
You may object at any time to the processing of your personal data on grounds relating to your particular situation[cite: 447, 451].
Rights related to Automated Decision-making and Profiling (Art. 22)
You have the right not to be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing, including profiling, that produces legal effects concerning you or similarly significantly affects you.

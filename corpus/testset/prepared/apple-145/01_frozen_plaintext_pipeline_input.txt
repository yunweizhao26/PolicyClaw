Sale/Sharing/Targeted Advertising
Under California and other state privacy laws, "
sale
" and "
sharing
" have very specific legal meanings that cover more than a cash-for-data swap.
"Sale"
means handing over your personal information to another company in exchange for
anything of value
- not just money. That can include getting access to better advertising tools.
"Sharing"
(a California-specific concept) means sending your info to ad platforms so they can show you Booksy ads as you browse the rest of the internet - what the law calls
cross-context behavioral advertising
.
"Targeted advertising"
is the same idea under other state laws.
Some of what we described in this “Cookies (Not The Kind You Eat) and Other Similar Tools” section fits these definitions. You can opt out anytime by using the
“Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information”
link or by adjusting your cookie settings (see “Your Privacy Choices and Rights”).

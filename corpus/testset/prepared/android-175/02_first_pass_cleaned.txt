Part 2: Premium, Ad-Free Games
Now, we’ll discuss our data privacy policies for our premium games available through app stores such as Apple Arcade, Amazon Kids+, and educational versions (the “Premium Games”).
What Personal Information do we collect?
First, we’ll discuss what we
don’t
collect. We don’t collect personal data in these Premium Games without your explicit consent.
Players can enjoy our Premium Games safely without worrying about their privacy. We’ve designed our Premium Games to avoid any automatic or involuntary collection of any personal data. Any game progress and other game-related information is stored on your device and not collected by us.
We don’t collect names, addresses, or any contact information, unless you contact us first for customer or technical support (the legal basis for collecting this information is your consent, under GDPR Art. 6 (1) (a).) Otherwise, we don’t collect email addresses, phone numbers, photos, videos, or audio recordings. We don’t track your location, and we don’t use persistent identifiers that would allow us to track players across apps or websites.
Now, we’ll discuss what we
do
collect. For our Premium Games, any data collection and sharing must be initiated by you. For example, if you contact us directly, including for customer or technical support, we will only collect and use your personal information for that purpose. You may also choose to publicly share game screenshots, progress, and other gameplay information through your device’s sharing functionality.
How we share Information
We share data only in very limited circumstances, when required by law or when necessary to comply with subpoenas, court orders, or other legal processes.
For Apple Arcade Users
In addition to the sharing described elsewhere in this Privacy Policy, we also share gameplay data from Premium Games with Apple, as part of their platform operations. If you choose to opt in to certain data sharing through your Apple device, you may share basic usage and crash data with us, share certain data with Apple Game Center for achievements, and with Apple iCloud to sync progress across devices.
We may also share data with Apple-approved service providers who process data on our behalf (such as for cloud storage or technical support), but only under strict contractual restrictions. These service providers are prohibited from using the data for their own purposes and can only use it according to our written instructions. They cannot share the data further.
If required by law, or if necessary to comply with subpoenas, court orders or other legal processes, we may share data to the extent required.
Additionally, any data retention by us will comply with Apple’s requirements.
Your choices about data collection
You can decline to provide certain data through your device settings, but this may limit some Premium Game features.
For example:
If you decline to allow the game to store cloud sync data, you may lose game progress when you switch to another device
If you decline certain permissions, some gameplay features may not be available
The core single-player experience remains available even if you decline optional data collection
You can also withdraw permission after initially providing it, through your device settings. We respect your choices about data collection.
No third-party services
These Premium Games are intentionally isolated from third-party services. We don’t include advertising networks, analytics tools from other companies, or any third-party tracking technologies.
The platform providers (such as Apple, Amazon, and others we offer the Premium Games through) handle all payments and subscriptions according to their own privacy policies, which means we never see or collect payment information.
How we keep players safe
In order to keep players of all ages safe while using our Premium Games, we’ve built in several protections:
No in-app purchases within the Premium Game itself
No chat features or ways to communicate with other players
No collection of photos, videos, or location data
The platforms themselves provide additional layers of protection, such as parental controls that you can use to further manage the experience. All payments and subscriptions go through the platforms’ secure systems, never through us directly.
Premium Game data retention
We retain personal data we collect through the Premium Games only as long as necessary to provide the services and comply with legal obligations.

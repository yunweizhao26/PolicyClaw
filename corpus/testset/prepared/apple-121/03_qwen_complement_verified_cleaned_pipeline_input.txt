2. Our Games
2.1 Why do we collect or use your Data
When you install and play MadBox’s Games, we can collect and process some of your Data based on your express consent or for different purposes.
In cases in which MadBox is a data “controller” (meaning, when MadBox is the main decision-maker, exercising overall control over the purposes and means of the processing of your Data), MadBox only uses your Data with a legal basis for doing so, such as:
- for the performance of our contract with you;
- where you have given your consent for a specific purpose;
- to comply with our own legal and regulatory obligations;
- for our legitimate interests or those of a third party.
A legitimate interest happens when we have a business or commercial reason to use your personal information and includes our own interests or the interests of third parties, and can cover commercial interests (e.g. sustain our free-to-play model), so long as this is not overridden by your own rights and interests.
In cases we are required under law or regulation to obtain your consent for the processing of your personal information, we will ask for your consent.
Here's why MadBox may collect your personal information and the legal basis we rely on in each case :
To provide and operate our Games: Contractual Necessity
To show personalized ads, based on your preferences and interests : Consent
To gather and provide third-party analytics, i.e. data collected through our applications may be shared with third-party partners that provide MadBox’s with analytics services : Consent
To gather first party analytics, i.e. Data collected through our applications may be used for the purposes of MadBox’s internal analytics tool ›› to understand how users interact with our applications to continuously improve them. This Data is not shared with third party partners : Legitimate interest
Attribution, i.e. match an advertisement of one of our Games you may have seen in another application with the install of such Game : Legitimate interest
To ensure the functionalities of our applications (for instance, debug, maintain the apps) : Legitimate interest
For all data processing activities that rely on users’ consent, you can always change your preferences and withdraw your consent in the privacy settings of any of our applications.
Note that, if you opt-out, you will still be able to see ads in our applications and some of our partners may still serve ads to you.

4. Your choices and rights
Under data protection law, you have certain rights in connection with your personal information and how we handle it. You can exercise these rights at any time by contacting us at
privacy@madboxgames.io
:
-
Right of access
. You have the right to know what information we hold about you and in some cases to have the information communicated to you. If you wish to exercise this right please contact us letting us know that you wish to exercise your right of access and what information in particular you would like to receive. We reserve the right to ask for reasonable evidence to verify your identity before we provide you with any information.
-
Right to rectification.
You have the right to ask us to rectify personal information you think is inaccurate. You also have the right to ask us to complete information you think is incomplete.
-
Right to erasure.
You have the right to ask us to erase your personal information in certain circumstances.
You can contact us, as indicated in the “Contact” section below, in order to have the data previously collected by MadBox on you and/or your device permanently erased.
In addition, in order for your data not to be collected anymore (for the future), you can, in the following order:
Activate “Limit Ad Tracking” (LAT) in your device settings to opt-out of having an ID for Advertisers and don’t see specific ads targeted at you; and
Desinstall our Games; and
Reinstall our Games.
Where we delete personal information about you, we may still retain some or all of that information for other purposes such as maintaining financial records, protecting or enforcing legal rights. We may also retain your information in an anonymised form.
-
Right to restrict processing.
In some cases, you may have the right to request a restriction of the processing of your personal data, such as when you are disputing the accuracy of your data held by us.
-
Right to data portability.
When the processing of your personal data is based on consent or contract, you may have the right to request that personal data that you have provided to us is provided to another data controller of your choice in a portable format.
-
Right to object to processing
. You have the right to object to the processing of personal data about you which is processed on the grounds of legitimate interests.
You are not required to pay any charge for exercising your rights. If you make a request, we have one month to respond to you.
Please contact us at
privacy@madboxgames.io

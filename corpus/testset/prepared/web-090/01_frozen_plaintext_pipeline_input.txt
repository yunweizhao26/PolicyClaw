1. What Information do we collect?
We collect Information you provide us, from your use of the Services, and from other sources. Here are the types of Information we collect about you.
1.1. Information you directly provide us
When you use our Services – such as creating a DeviantArt account, registering for special services (e.g. Premium Content), making purchases, subscribing to newsletters, or communicating with us – we collect such information you provided directly. This may include:
Basic Account Information:
Name, email address, password, and date of birth (required at signup).
Account Settings and Onboarding (as applicable):
Pronouns, country, optional information you included under the “About” section in your profile, and preferences about the content and how you use the platform.
Transaction Information for Buyers (including Core Members and Sellers):
Full name, credit card number, country, and, if you are buying physical products, your full address, postal code (if applicable), and telephone number.
Content and Activity:
Information about the content you upload, submit, or interact with – including art, images, GIFs, videos, journal entries, literature, posts, comments, favorites, “Watch” activity, and other interactions with users.
In-platform Messaging:
Content of messages or other direct communications within our platform.
We collect only the information necessary to provide our Services securely and in compliance with applicable laws.
1.2. Information collected through your use of the Site or Services
When you visit or use our Site or Services – including browsing pages, viewing accounts, editing your settings, using features, or interacting with content – we automatically collect a range of data about your activity and your device. This includes:
Usage Data:
Actions you take on our platform, such as clicks, page views, searches, purchases, navigation paths, interactions with features, downloads, and other usages.
Device and Technical Information:
Information such as your IP address, browser type, operating system, device type, device identifiers, and basic geolocation (determined via your IP address).
Session and Real-time Interactions:
We use technologies to monitor user sessions and track interactions to help us understand browsing patterns, diagnose problems, and optimize our Services.
Cookies and Tracking Technologies:
For more about our use of cookies and similar technologies, see Section 5 ("Cookies and Tracking Technologies") of this policy.
We use these types of data to:
Keep our Services secure and fully functional
Analyze site and user behavior for improvement
Customize your experience and show relevant recommendations
Detect, prevent, and investigate fraud or misuse
1.3. Information we collect from other sources
We may receive certain information about you from third parties where required to operate, secure, and provide our Services. This includes:
Third-party Login Providers:
When you sign up or log in through services like Google, Apple, or Facebook, we collect your name, email address, and any other information you authorize via your privacy settings. We never receive or store your passwords for such third-party providers.
Fraud Detection and Security Providers:
We may receive fraud risk information, geolocation data, and abuse signals from our partners to help us keep our platform safe.
Payment Processors:
For transactions handled by PayPal, Stripe, or other payment providers, we get transaction IDs, payment status, payer names, and relevant details necessary for processing and verifying payments. We do not have access to your full payment credentials.
Discord:
If you choose to link a Discord account for tier access, we may receive your Discord username, user ID, related role, and tier-related information.
Other Providers:
Occasionally, we may obtain information from other security or service providers where needed to detect illegal activities or prevent abuse.
We use this information only as required to operate our platform, provide our Services, ensure transaction security, or protect against fraud or abuse.
2. Why do we use your Information?
2.1. We use your Information for the following purposes
To provide and operate our Services:
We use your account details, preferences, content activity, and payment information to create your account, enable platform features, process transactions, and deliver content.
To ensure safety, security, and fraud prevention:
We use information such as device and technical details, account identifiers, purchase records, and data from trusted security providers to detect and prevent fraud, verify your identity, secure your account, and protect our platform from abuse and illegal activities.
To personalize and improve our Services:
We analyze your activity on our platform, including the way you interact with our features and content, to tailor recommendations and results, remember your preferences, and continuously enhance your experience.
To communicate with you:
We use your contact details to send service updates, provide information about your account, deliver security notifications, and respond to your support requests and inquiries.
To deliver marketing and promotional content (where allowed by law or with your consent):
We may use your Information to send you special offers, product updates, or relevant marketing content. You can opt out of receiving promotional communications at any time, as further detailed in Section 6.1 below.
To enable social, community, and interactive features:
We may use information you choose to provide when interacting with content in the Site, such as posts, messages, comments, and social connections, as part of platform interactions and community engagement.
To run contests, events, or promotions:
If you participate in a contest or promotional event, we use your entry details and contact information to manage participation, communicate with winners, and provide prizes or rewards.
To create aggregated and statistical insights:
We analyze user trends and behaviors by using aggregated or de-identified information so we can improve our Services, understand user needs, and advance our platform’s features. This aggregated data does not identify you individually.
To comply with legal and regulatory requirements:
We use your Information to meet our obligations under laws, regulations, and legal processes, and to respond to official requests from authorities.

We only use your Information when permitted by law, including where:
It is necessary to perform our contract with you (such as providing the Services or supporting your account).

It is necessary for our legitimate business interests – like improving and protecting our services, provided that these interests do not override your fundamental rights or freedoms.
We have obtained your consent, where required (for example, for sending marketing communications). When we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

The Personal Data That We Collect or Process

Personal data

We collect and process the following types of personal data:
‍
- Account and contact details, including your name, address, email, phone number, username, and any other contact information you provide.
- Payment information, such as credit or debit card details and information about payment methods used with our Services.
- Account history, including transactions, purchases, order history, and discounts.
- Demographic information, such as age, gender, income, education, or family/marital status, only if you consent to providing it.
- Location data, including general geographic information (country, state, city).
- Device information, such as IP address, device identifiers, operating system and version, preferred language, hardware and browser details.
- Content and information you choose to share through our Services or communications with us, such as emails or chats.
- Images, voice recordings, and videos collected or stored in connection with the Services, if you consent.
When you use our APPs, we may also collect:
‍
- Optional health data, such as mood, feelings, and mental state.
- Log and usage data, including diagnostic and performance information automatically recorded when you use our apps, such as IP address, device details, browser settings, activity timestamps, pages viewed, searches, features used, device events, error reports, and hardware configuration.

We only process personal data necessary for the purposes described and as required by the services or applications you use.
Some information may be considered sensitive under certain laws. We collect and process sensitive data only with your consent, when required. If you choose not to provide certain information, we may be unable to deliver requested features or services.

Statistical or aggregated information.
This data does not directly identify individuals, but may be derived from personal data, such as calculating the percentage of users who access a specific feature.

Technical information.

If non-personal statistical or technical data is combined with personal data so that it identifies an individual, we treat it as personal information.

Automatically Through Our Services

As you navigate through and interact with our Services, we may use automatic data collection technologies to collect information that may include personal data. The technologies we use for this automatic data collection may include:
-
Cookies.
A cookie is a small file placed on your device when you interact with the Services. You may refuse to accept or disable cookies by activating the appropriate setting on your browser or device. However, if you select this setting, you may be unable to access certain features of the Services. For more information about the cookies we use on our websites and the choices available to you, see our Cookie Policy at https://home.monkeytaps.app/cookie-policy.

Web Beacons.
Some parts of the Services and our emails may contain small electronic files known as web beacons (also referred to as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs) that permit the Company, for example, to count users who have visited those parts or opened an email and for other related statistics (for example, recording the popularity of certain content and verifying system and server integrity).
To the extent any of these automated technologies are considered a personal data sale, targeted advertising, or profiling, under applicable laws, depending on where you live, you may opt out from use of these automated technologies for such uses by adjusting your device’s privacy settings (such as iOS App Tracking Transparency or Android advertising settings), responding to the consent options presented in our APPS where applicable, or, for Our Website, by using a browser-based opt-out preference signal such as the Global Privacy Control or emailing us at hello@monkeytaps.app with the subject line “Consumer Privacy Rights Request”.

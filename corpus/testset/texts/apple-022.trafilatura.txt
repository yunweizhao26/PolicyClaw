Depending on your place of residence, under applicable law, you can make certain choices and exercise certain rights with respect to our use of your Personal Information. Please note that rights and requests may be subject to applicable exceptions. We will not discriminate against you if you decide to exercise these rights.
- Your Marketing Choices
We may send you periodic marketing and promotional emails, push notifications and/or text messages to your mobile device and, where required by law, we will obtain your consent to do so. If you no longer wish to receive our marketing and promotional communications, you may opt out of receiving them by following the opt-out instructions included in any communication that you receive from us or by visiting your Account Settings. Please note that even if you opt out of receiving marketing and promotional communications from us, we may still send you administrative and transactional messages from which you cannot opt out.
- Correct or Update your Account Information and Settings
If you have created an account through the Services, you may review, correct, or update your information (including your name, telephone number, delivery address, and payment information) at any time by logging in to your account and visiting the “Account Settings” page. Please note that changes to your settings may require some time to take effect. If you do not have or no longer have access to your Instacart account, please email us at customerprivacy@instacart.com to submit your requests.
- Other Privacy Rights
Depending on where you reside, you may have certain additional privacy rights with respect to your Personal Information, under applicable law. The exact scope of these rights may vary by state and include certain exceptions:
- Right to Knowwhat categories of data we have collected about you, the categories of sources of this data, and the categories of third parties to whom we disclose your information (as described above in this Privacy Policy).
- Residents of certain states may have additional information rights, such as the right to request categories or specific third parties to which we disclose or sell Personal Information or share it for targeted advertising. If you reside in a state that allows for these additional disclosures, we will provide such information as part of your Right to Access request.
- Right to Access and obtain a copy of your Personal Information in a portable format.
- Right to Correct inaccurate Personal Information we hold about you.
- Right to Delete the Personal Information we hold about you.
- Right to Opt Out of sale and sharing of your Personal Information or disclosure of your Personal Information for targeted advertising.
- How to Exercise These Privacy Rights
To exercise these Privacy Rights, please follow the instructions below:
- Right to Opt Out - you may exercise your right to opt out, where available, by visiting this link. You may also exercise your opt out rights by enabling a browser-based opt-out preference signal that complies with applicable laws, such as the Global Privacy Control (“GPC”). We listen for and honor GPC signals as required under applicable laws. To learn more about GPC, please click here.
- All other Privacy Rights -
- For Account Holders. If you have an active Instacart account, click here to submit a request. You will be prompted to re-log in to your Instacart account.
- For Non-Account Holders. If you do not have an Instacart account, no longer have access to your Instacart account, or you used Instacart through one of our embedded partner experiences (such as Groceries Powered by Instacart), please email us at customerprivacy@instacart.com to submit your request.
- Authorized Agents. Depending on where you reside, you may authorize another person or entity to exercise certain rights on your behalf (an “Authorized Agent”). In such states, Authorized Agents can submit all eligible privacy requests on behalf of consumers by emailing us at customerprivacy@instacart.com. Authorized Agents may also click here for instructions on opting out.
To protect the confidentiality of your Personal Information and that of others, we will only process your request for access, deletion and correction when your identity has been verified (or where otherwise not permitted). If your request was submitted by an Authorized Agent, where permitted or necessary to maintain security, we may require written proof of the Authorized Agent’s permission to submit the request on your behalf and may confirm the request directly with you (other than for opt-outs).
- Right to appeal
If you submit a request to exercise any of the rights described above and we do not take action on the request, you may appeal our decision if you are in an eligible jurisdiction. To appeal a refusal to take action on your request, please email us at PrivacyAppeals@instacart.com with the subject line “Rights Request Appeal.” If you are unsatisfied with the way that we have handled your appeal, you may have the right to complain to your state’s attorney general.
- Do Not Track
“Do Not Track” (“DNT”) is a developing standard for web browsers that aims to inform the websites you visit that you do not want information about your online activity collected over time and across third-party websites or online services. Although we do our best to honor the privacy preferences of our users, we do not interpret or respond to DNT or other similar signals from your browser at this time. For more information, visit http://www.allaboutdnt.com.

1. PERSONAL INFORMATION COLLECTED
1.1 USER PROFILE
When You create a user profile in the Services and confirm being the holder of a certain number, Truecaller will collect the information provided by You. In order to create a user profile, You must register Your first name, last name and phone number. Truecaller verifies Your phone number by sending or receiving you a verification code via SMS or by performing an automated "missed call". Additional information that may be provided at Your option include, but is not limited to, photo, gender, birth date, street address and zip code, country of residence, email address, additional information about You including name of Your company, job title, website, Your social media profile or other web URL, and such similar information. Truecaller may supplement the information provided by You with information from third parties and add it to the information provided by You e.g. demographic information and additional contact information that is publicly available.

version of the Services You use; Your service request to verified business made within the app including post-call delivery action data (such as Your selection to reschedule, cancel, or share delivery instructions when You interact with a verified business within the app), Your real-time location coordinates if You choose to share it to assist a verified business with a delivery, or Your other service request to them; depending on Your app settings and features used, limited real time activity data (such as driving and walking while using Family Protection) and other information based on Your interaction with our Services such as how the Services are being accessed (via another service, web site or a search engine); Your paired devices (such as smart watch); the pages You visit and features You use on the Services; the services and websites You engage with from the Services; content viewed by You, content You have commented on or sent to us and information about the ads You see and/or engage with; 2. USE OF PERSONAL INFORMATION
2.1 PROVIDE, IMPROVE AND PERSONALIZE OUR SERVICES
Truecaller may use the personal information collected to provide, maintain, improve, analyze and personalize the Services including to/for its Users, verified business customers, and trusted third-party service providers. More specifically, Truecaller may use such information to:
provide smart caller ID, dialer and messaging functionality that, among other features, allow for population of unidentified numbers and other data in call or message logs, display the name associated with a certain number for incoming or outgoing calls and messages or following a manual number search against the Truecaller database;
display the number associated with a certain name following a manual name search against the Truecaller database, subject to that name searches will only produce a number linked to the name if the number is available for search in a public database or relates to a User who has made his or her name available for search, provide Your information to the person You reach out to when requesting a contact via the Truecaller name search service, in which case Your request will be forwarded to that person via an SMS and he/she may choose whether or not to share the phone number with You at his/her own discretion;
display information based on connections a User may have in common, directly or indirectly, with other Users via a “social graph” algorithm, display the “who viewed my profile”, "who searched for me" and “availability” functionalities of the Services;
enable You to use and share Your information in connection with Your registration, login or other use of or interaction with third party services e.g. payment service providers, online services, social networking sites and other third-party API’s;
provision services to verified businesses in the interest of prevention, detection, of any offence or contravention of any law for the time being in force in India, to enable verification, fraud detection and prevention based on trust and reputational signals associated with a phone number within our Services;
send in application push notifications and reminders and deliver messages via the Services, in which case Your messages will be kept for a limited period of time in order to deliver the messages but Truecaller will not, unless specifically stated, monitor the content of Your messages;
maintain User spam lists and build a community based spam blocking directory;
detect, flag, and filter messages and calls;
to display and provide AI summaries of feedback received from Users;
assess suspicious or unknown content, identifiers, communications, links, senders, other signals or information You input while using our trust and safety feature and provide You with the relevant trust, safety, and risk indicators and awareness information;
based on Your choice, share log of verified business call You receive from a verified business with it for its internal analytics purposes;
process and transmit Your specific post-call delivery action selections (including Your location data, if You choose to share it) or other service request within the Services, in real-time to the respective verified business to facilitate Your delivery requests or other service request and update their operational workflows; and
otherwise improve our Services, business and operations.

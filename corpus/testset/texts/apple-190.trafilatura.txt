Anonymized & Aggregated Data
Categories we may collect (with representative examples)
-
App usage & interaction statistics (aggregated)
Examples: feature usage counts, button click counts, crash event counts (statistics only)
-
Device & system information (de-identified / aggregated)
Examples: device model, operating system version, language settings, app version
-
Connectivity & performance metrics (aggregated)
Examples: connection success rate, latency/jitter distributions, packet loss rate, throughput ranges, error codes and failure reason statistics
We do not include: your source or destination IP addresses, DNS queries, websites visited, downloaded content, connection timestamps, or per-session identifiers that enable tracking a specific user/session.
-
Bandwidth consumption & capacity statistics (aggregated)
Examples: total or time-bucketed bandwidth statistics, server resource utilization (for capacity planning only)
We do not record: browsing content or website/domain information.
-
Diagnostics (minimized and de-identified before retention)
Examples: crash stacks and performance diagnostics (for troubleshooting)
-
Install attribution data (Android only; de-identified / aggregated)
Examples: referrer/campaign identifiers (as provided by Google Play), installation timestamp, Google Play-defined referrer parameters.
Used for: install attribution, data analysis, and effectiveness measurement (e.g., understanding which channels/campaigns led to an install).
We do not: use it to identify any specific individual, to link it with any personally identifiable information (PII), for profiling or personalized advertising.

We do not use such tools to collect VPN traffic, websites visited, or DNS queries.
-
Install Attribution (Android, where applicable)
We may use the Google Play Install Referrer API (provided by Google Play) to retrieve install attribution information at first install for Android, solely for installation attribution and analytics/effectiveness evaluation.
-
Eligibility Verification (where applicable)
We may work with independent third-party platforms that verify eligibility for certain discounts or promotional benefits, such as student discounts, and facilitate access to applicable discount codes. These third parties collect and process personal data in accordance with their own privacy policies and act as independent data controllers for such processing.
We only receive the result of the eligibility verification and do not receive any identifiable personal data from these platforms.

Country-specific provisions
Users in European Union
- The right to be informed; This means anyone processing your personal data must make clear what they are processing, why, and who else the data may be passed to.
- The right of access; this is your right to see what data is held about you by a Data Controller.
- The right to rectification; the right to have your data corrected or amended if what is held is incorrect in some way.
- The right to erasure; under certain circumstances you can ask for your personal data to be deleted. This is also called 'the Right to be Forgotten'. This would apply if the personal data is no longer required for the purposes it was collected for, or your consent for the processing of that data has been withdrawn, or the personal data has been unlawfully processed.
- The right to restrict processing; this gives the Data Subject the right to ask for a temporary halt to processing of personal data, such as in the case where a dispute or legal case has to be concluded, or the data is being corrected.
- The right to data portability; a Data Subject has the right to ask for any data supplied directly to the Data Controller by him or her, to be provided in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format.
- The right to object; the Data Subject has the right to object to further processing of their data which is inconsistent with the primary purpose for which it was collected, including profiling, automation, and direct marketing.
- Rights in relation to automated decision making and profiling; Data Subjects have the right not to be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing.

1-2. TAPBLAZE ID
Some games allow players to restore their progress by using their email to register for a TapBlaze ID. TapBlaze only permits the creation of a TapBlaze ID in games with age gates when the user has identified as 16 years of age or older. TapBlaze may use tools or third-party analytical software to automatically collect and use certain non-personal data that does not directly enable TapBlaze to identify you. The types of non-personal data TapBlaze may collect and use include, but are not limited to:

3. Policy Regarding Children
TapBlaze applies special protections for the personal data of:
- players identified as under the age of 16 through an age gate (in those games that have one), and
- all players in games without an age gate.
TapBlaze does not collect, use, or disclose such players’ personal data for profiling, personalized advertising, or geolocation tracking. We use only limited types of personal data, such as the persistent identifiers listed in the chart below (sometimes in combination with the data described in Section 2), for the following purposes:
- To deliver the game and access network features
- To provide you with the products and services you request
- To serve contextual advertising based on the content of the game you are playing (including frequency capping, fraud prevention, and attribution)
- To maintain and analyze game functionality
- To detect, investigate and prevent actions to ensure legal and regulatory compliance
- To protect the security and integrity of our online services and the safety of our users
The following table sets forth, for each category of personal information we collect from children, the purpose / business need and the retention period.
| Personal data | Purpose/Business need | Maximum retention period |
|---|---|---|
| Device and other persistent identifiers (device ID, IP address, advertising ID) | Internal Operations: delivering the game, network communications, contextual ad frequency capping, fraud prevention, security, legal compliance | Retained for the duration of an active game session or, where necessary for Internal Operations continuity (e.g., fraud detection), no longer than 18 months from last active use of the applicable game, after which identifiers are deleted or de-identified |
| Age indicator (under/over 16) and child-directed flag | To determine appropriate experience and advertising type (contextual vs. behavioral) | Retained for the life of the active game on the child’s device plus two (2) years following the last active session, or until a parent requests deletion, whichever comes first |
| Approximate location (country/state, from IP) | To deliver contextual advertising appropriate to language and region; legal compliance | Not stored by TapBlaze beyond the game session; shared in real-time with contextual ad networks as defined in this section subject to their data retention policies |
| Customer support communications from parents | To resolve support inquiries and maintain records of parental requests | Deleted promptly after fulfillment of request, unless it is reasonably necessary to retain the record to resolve the support inquiry fully. In such circumstances, TapBlaze will retain such records until the purpose for retention no longer exists or for a maximum time period of up to one (1) year from the date of the communication. |
We use technical and contractual measures to limit our service providers and third parties with whom we share personal data from using such information for any other purpose. We work only with selected ad networks (defined in Ad Serving Technology section) that respect the tag-for-child-directed (TFCD) parameter—aka the ‘COPPA flag’—on a per-app or per-request basis for contextual advertising, so that interest-based advertising is switched off, and personal data collection is restricted. In addition, these ad networks are able to categorize and tag their ad creatives by age, so that the ads are appropriate content-wise.
Because we don’t collect or use the personal data of players under the age of 16 (except as set forth above), they are not able to make their personal data publicly available through our games.
If TapBlaze discovers that we have inadvertently gathered personal information from a child under 16 years of age, we will take reasonable measures to promptly delete such personal information from our records.
Please note that our customer support features are meant for players 16 years of age or older. If we receive a customer support inquiry from a player that is under the age of 16, we will delete all personal information (e.g., name, email, etc.) immediately after fulfilling the request.
We do not retain children’s personal data indefinitely.
If you are a parent or legal guardian of a child under the age of 16 who uses TapBlaze's services and have any questions or comments, please contact us at privacy@tapblaze.com. If you are a parent or guardian, you may review the information we have collected from your child or ask us to make no further use of, or delete, such information. If you believe that we might have any personal data from or about a child under the age of 16, please contact us here. We may need to verify your identity before implementing your request. If we need to verify your identity, we will only ask for the minimum information necessary to do so.
Please note that in-game advertising for users under the age of sixteen (16) are only CONTEXTUAL.
The third-party ad networks which may display contextual advertisements to users under the age sixteen (16) are listed in Appendix D.

Privacy Policy of Parallel Space
Parallel Space respects and protects the privacy of individuals interacting with all official websites and user communities related to the Parallel Space series of products. This Privacy Policy (hereinafter referred to as the "Privacy Policy") outlines our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information. The Privacy Policy applies to the entire series of Parallel Space products, including but not limited to: Parallel Space, Parallel Space Lite, Parallel Space Pro, as well as the 32-bit Support expansion and 64-bit Support expansion for the respective versions. We do not intentionally access your information or disclose it to any third party without your prior consent, unless otherwise stipulated herein.
1. Information We Access and Collect
1.1 We Do Not Collect Personally Identifiable Information. Personally Identifiable Information includes your name, address, email, telephone number, fax number, information you stored on your device, and information that can be used to identify you or other individuals when you use third-party applications, services or websites.1.2 Non-Personally Identifiable Information. When you interact with our application, we may collect non-personally identifiable information, such as your device model and the Android version you use. A detailed list of the information we access and collect is as follows:
(1) Session and Available Data. "Session and Available Data" refers to the data and information relevant to connections and services that you provide to us when you use third-party applications, services or websites through our applications and services. Session and Available Data may include information related to connection request, server communication, and data sharing (such as network testing, quality of service, date, time, and place/location). Please note that Session and Available Data does not include any Personally Identifiable Information, nor does it include any content (photo, contact, calendar, etc.) that you send or share through third-party applications, services, or websites.1.3 Special Note about Sensitive Information. In order for you to use third-party applications, services, and websites through our applications and services normally, we must access your microphone, camera, or location, with your permission, as described below:
(2) Login Data. When you interact with us, or use our applications, services or website, our system may automatically collect your unique device serial number, IP address, the browser or operating system you use, and the date and time of your visit (i.e. "Login Data").
(3) Summary Information. "Summary Information" refers to the data that we collected from a group or service types or users, from which all personally identifiable information has been removed. This means the information about how you use third-party applications, services and websites through our applications may be collected and analyzed together with the usage information of other individuals, but personally identifiable information will not be included in the end Summary Information. Summary Information helps us understand user demand trends, develop better new features and provide tailor-made applications, services, and websites.
(4) Registration Information. You may use Parallel Space without providing any personal information, but if you choose to purchase any VIP subscriptions or one-time purchase items, an association will be made with your Google account to retain and transfer your access to the purchased content. All associated account information is processed by the authenticator (such as Google Play) and is not stored or used by us.
(1) Microphone. We access the microphone on your device (with your permission) to ensure normal usage of third-party applications, services, and websites that may require microphone permission to function normally.
(2) Camera. We access the camera on your device (with your permission) to ensure normal usage of third-party applications, services, and websites that may require camera permission to function normally.
(3) Location. We access and collect your location information (with your permission) to ensure normal usage of third-party applications, services, and websites that may require location information to function normally, and for R&D purposes as described in this Privacy Policy.
(4) Installed Applications. We access and collect your installed applications to improve and enhance our services, and in the following ways: We analyze the data to optimize the performance of cloned apps, ensuring smooth operation, identify and resolve compatibility issues with your device's operating system, and enable certain cloned apps to function correctly, including account linking, notification management, and data synchronization.

4. How to Manage Your Information
If you no longer wish to use Parallel Space and would like us to stop using your information, please send an email to help@lbesec.com. In the subject line, state your intention for account deletion, and include your account information and purchase details for any active VIP upgrades in the body of the email.IMPORTANT NOTE: If you proceed with this action, you will lose access to your account or any associated VIP upgrades. Additionally, you must cancel auto-renewal in your Google Play account settings to stop further billing for any active auto-renewable subscriptions. Please allow up to 7 working days for us to process your account deletion request. Once deleted, accounts cannot be restored.

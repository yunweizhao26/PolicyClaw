3. AI-Powered Features and Data Handling
Some features in the app are powered by third-party artificial intelligence and cloud services. These features may include, but are not limited to: text summarization, translation, writing enhancement, content generation, OCR (extracting text from images or scanned documents), AI photo filters, and full-document rewriting or comparison.
How AI Processes Your Information
When you use these features, the content you choose to process, including selected text, full documents, scanned pages, and the images or photos you upload for OCR or AI filters, is securely transmitted to third-party AI or cloud providers so they can generate the result you requested.
For image-based features (OCR, AI photo filters, document scanning), this means the actual image or document file leaves your device and is processed on the provider's servers. We transmit only the content needed to perform the feature you triggered, and it is used solely to produce that output.
Neither we nor our providers use your inputs or outputs to train or improve AI models without your explicit consent. Our providers are contractually obligated to process this data securely and only for the purpose of delivering the requested feature.
Retention of AI Inputs
We keep the information involved in AI features only for as long as needed to provide those features and for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.
- The text and requests you submit are processed to generate the result you requested.
- Features that require a full file upload may hold the uploaded file on our provider's servers to complete processing. These files are protected by access control and encryption while stored, and are never used to train models or shared with third parties beyond the provider performing the processing.
We retain uploaded files and AI-generated results for as long as necessary to deliver the feature you requested, operate and secure the service, and comply with our legal obligations, after which we delete or anonymize them. You can request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at the email in the "Contact Details" section, subject to any retention we are required by law to maintain.
A local interaction history may be retained on your device. You can manage it from the conversation history page, reachable via the upper-right corner of the AI interface, where you can select and delete multiple records manually.
Disclaimer on AI-Generated Content
AI-generated outputs are produced by probabilistic models and may not always be accurate, complete, or contextually appropriate. Results should be reviewed carefully and should not be relied upon for legal, medical, financial, or other professional advice. We disclaim any liability for decisions or consequences resulting from the use of AI-generated content.

6. Data Security and Storage
We are committed to protecting your data in accordance with internationally recognized standards, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).
We do not require account registration. The app follows the principles of data minimization and privacy by design, collecting only the information necessary to operate specific features.
Certain technical and usage information (for example, crash diagnostics, performance monitoring, and analytics) is collected automatically through third-party tools, as described in Data Collection and Usage. This data is transmitted to and processed by those providers strictly for operational and maintenance purposes. It is retained in accordance with each provider's own policy, and we do not use it for behavioral profiling, cross-app tracking, or targeted advertising.
The documents and files you manage in the app are stored in different places, and how you remove them depends on where they live:
- In-app data. Content stored inside the app itself can be permanently deleted by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. If you do not grant the app storage or file-access permission, the file data shown in the app is stored as part of the app's in-app data, clearing app data or uninstalling the app will remove it.
- Original or exported files in public storage. Files the app saves to shared or public folders on your device are not removed when you clear app data or uninstall the app, you may need to delete these yourself using a file manager.
- Cloud-processed data. For AI features that require a full file upload (e.g., OCR, AI photo filters, rewriting, summarization, or file comparison), temporary uploads to our service provider's servers may occur to facilitate processing. These files are processed using strict access control and encryption protocols, are retained only for as long as necessary to complete processing and meet our legal obligations, and are never used to train models or shared with third parties beyond the provider performing the processing.
We implement a combination of organizational and technical measures to prevent unauthorized access, including secure transmission protocols, access controls, data isolation, and periodic internal audits. While no system can guarantee absolute security, we continuously monitor and enhance our safeguards to maintain a high level of data integrity, confidentiality, and availability.

Privacy Rights & Security
What personal information does Bajaj Finance collect?
Bajaj Finance collects personal details such as your name, age, address, mobile number, email, PAN, KYC documents, bank account details, and credit scores, along with certain non-personal information like browser and device data collected through your visits and app usage.
Why does Bajaj Finance collect my personal information?
Your information is used to provide and improve financial services, evaluate and develop products, comply with legal and regulatory requirements including credit reporting, and, where applicable, share necessary details with group companies or third parties involved in delivering these services.
Does Bajaj Finance share my personal information with third parties?
Bajaj Finance does not share your information with external organisations except where necessary to provide services, meet legal or regulatory requirements, or complete a transaction, and only with service providers bound to use it strictly for the specified purpose.
Can I request deletion or correction of my personal information?
Yes, you can request correction of your personal data through Menu > Help & Support > New Request > Profile Details, or request erasure through Service > Your Account > Manage Account and Data > Right to Erase, subject to regulatory retention requirements.
Does Bajaj Finance use cookies on its website?
Yes, Bajaj Finance uses cookies to temporarily store session details like your mobile number and to remember preferences that improve your browsing experience. Cookies do not store personal information such as your name or account number directly.
What should I do if I suspect my personal information has been compromised?
Contact Bajaj Finance immediately through its official customer care number listed on the website to report the concern. Avoid sharing further personal details until you have verified the situation, and always access Bajaj Finance only through official, verified channels.

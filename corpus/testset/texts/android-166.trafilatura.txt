2. How do we use cookies and similar technologies
Like many online services, we use cookies and similar technologies to verify log-in information, operate and provide our Services, analyze, and improve user experience. Cookies are usually small text files that are stored on your device's browser directory or program data subfolders for different periods of time. Some cookies are “session cookies”, meaning that they exist only while your browser or the application is open, and will be deleted automatically once you close your browse or application; other coolies are “permanent cookies”, which survive after your browser or application is closed, and can be used by the Service to recognize your device when you open and use the Service again. Cookies allow FreeReels to access or store information from your application about you, your settings or your device.
As a rule, cookies do not contain any information that could identify you directly. They do, however, make it possible to offer you a more personalized user experience. You have the right to choose whether or not to accept cookie., Most devices and browsers are initially set up to accept cookies but offer settings to modify cookie preferences. If you do not accept these cookies, you may not be able to use the full functions of FreeReels. For example, we use first-party cookies to save your login information for future logins to our service. Such cookies are strictly necessary for the normal operation of FreeReels. You can find out more about cookies on allaboutcookies.org .

2. How do we use cookies and similar technologies
Like many online services, we use cookies and similar technologies to verify log-in information, operate and provide our Services, analyze, and improve user experience. Cookies are usually small text files that are stored on your device's browser directory or program data subfolders for different periods of time. Some cookies are “session cookies”, meaning that they exist only while your browser or the application is open, and will be deleted automatically once you close your browse or application; other coolies are “permanent cookies”, which survive after your browser or application is closed, and can be used by the Service to recognize your device when you open and use the Service again. Cookies allow FreeReels to access or store information from your application about you, your settings or your device.
As a rule, cookies do not contain any information that could identify you directly. They do, however, make it possible to offer you a more personalized user experience. You have the right to choose whether or not to accept cookie., Most devices and browsers are initially set up to accept cookies but offer settings to modify cookie preferences. If you do not accept these cookies, you may not be able to use the full functions of FreeReels. For example, we use first-party cookies to save your login information for future logins to our service. Such cookies are strictly necessary for the normal operation of FreeReels. You can find out more about cookies on allaboutcookies.org .

Data transfers
Your personal information may be stored and processed outside of the country where it is collected, including outside of the United Kingdom (“UK”) and European Economic Area (“EEA”). When transferring information, we ensure that appropriate and suitable safeguards and technical measures are in place to protect your personal information. To do this, we will only transfer data to countries that have been identified as providing adequate protection or where we have approved transfer mechanisms in place to protect your personal data.
For transfers of personal information from the UK/EEA, we make use of standard contractual clauses that have been approved by the UK Information Commissioner’s Office and European Commission. Where appropriate, we may rely on Binding Corporate Rules put in place by our suppliers, or we may implement other similar measures required by laws around the world.
Retention of your personal information
We will only keep records of your personal information for as long as is reasonably necessary for the purposes for which we have collected it, and to comply with any statutory or regulatory obligations. We respect requests to stop processing your personal data for marketing purposes but will retain a record of your request indefinitely. This is retained only for the purpose of suppression to ensure we respect your right to opt-out of receiving marketing from us.
Your rights
In some circumstances, you may have the following rights in relation to your personal information:
- To request access to your personal information;
- To request rectification of your personal data;
- To request erasure of your personal information;
- To object to processing of your personal information, including for direct marketing;
- To request human intervention where there is automated decision-making.
If you wish to exercise any of the rights described, you can complete our Personal Data Request Form or contact us at [email protected]. We may ask you for further information to help us locate your data and speed up the request process. Where you request a copy of your personal data, we will ask you to provide proof of identity to ensure we are sharing the personal data with the correct person.
In the case of some of our educational subscription products which you access via your school’s subscription, we maybe coordinate with your school to process your request.
Complaints
Under the recent Data Use and Access Act (DUAA) legislation, you also have a statutory right to complain directly to OUP about how we are processing your personal data. You can do this using our Personal Data Request Form as above.
If you are unhappy with OUP’s response to your complaint, you also have the right to lodge a complaint regarding our processing of your personal information with a data protection supervisory authority in a country where you live, work, or where you believe a breach may have occurred.
For individuals in the United Kingdom, you can contact the Information Commissioner’s Office via their website www.ico.org.uk
For individuals in the EU, you can find your supervisory authority by visiting the following website: Our Members | European Data Protection Board (europa.eu)

A. Cookies
What Are Cookies?
Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They contain alphanumeric identifiers and are used to recognize your browser, remember your preferences, and collect information about your interactions with our Services.
Types of Cookies We Use
-
By duration:
-
Session cookies: These cookies are temporary and are deleted when you close your browser. They help us:
-
Maintain your session as you navigate through the site;
-
Process transactions and requests; and
-
Verify your identity during login and gameplay
-
Persistent cookies: These cookies remain on your device after your session ends. They are used to, for example,:
-
Remember your preferences and settings across visits;
-
Track aggregate and statistical information about user activity; and
-
Improve site performance and personalization
-
By source:
-
First-party cookies: These cookies are set directly by PLAYSTUDIOS.
-
Third-party cookies: These cookies are set by external partners (e.g., analytics and advertising providers).
The website https://playstudios.helpshift.com/hc/en/ uses the cookies _faq_view_uuid and _faq_view_uuid_legacy to manage user sessions and track interactions with the FAQ pages. These cookies are necessary for the core functionality of the site, such as maintaining continuity during support inquiries, and therefore do not require visitor consent.

At this time, this Site does not use clear GIFs, web beacons, or pixel tags for analytics or advertising purposes. However, such technologies may be used in the future.

If you submit a request to have your data deleted, we will delete, put beyond use or anonymize your personal data such that you cannot be identified from the data that we retain, unless we have a basis to maintain it (see “How Long Will We Store Your Personal Data?”).

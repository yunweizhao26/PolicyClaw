Answers:

- prohibited: the policy states that the flow does not happen in this context.
- conditional: the policy decides the flow only once a fact that the scenario leaves out is known. Name that fact.
- conflict: the policy both permits and prohibits the flow in this context.
- not_addressed: the policy does not describe this flow.

# Policy text preparation

`python -m policyclaw.prep TSV OUT` reads rows of `id<TAB>plaintext_path` and writes one folder `OUT/<id>/` per policy. The first file, `01_frozen_plaintext_pipeline_input.txt`, is the plaintext read as text, so Windows line ends become newlines. The model is `LLM_MODEL_NAME` through `policyclaw.llm` (for example `LLM_BACKEND=gemini LLM_MODEL_NAME=gemini-3.8-flash GEMINI_THINKING_LEVEL=LOW`), and its answers are cached in `LLM_CACHE` (default `OUT/llm_cache`). `THREADS` policies run at a time, and an existing policy folder is never overwritten. With `--no-model`, a third column names an existing cleaned file, and only the segmentation runs.

The cleanup (`clean.py`) keeps the source lines that belong to the privacy policy and never rewrites them. A pass cuts the page into chunks of about 20,000 characters with up to five lines of overlap, numbers the lines, and asks for the ranges of policy lines with `prompts/clean_select.txt`. An answer with malformed ranges goes back to the model with its error, up to three answers in all. An audit with `prompts/clean_verify.txt` then shows every line tagged KEEP or REST and asks for missed and wrongly kept lines. The audit ends when two consecutive answers leave the selection unchanged, or with the union of all selections when a selection repeats. It runs on each chunk and once more on the whole page. The first pass gives `02_first_pass_cleaned.txt`, and a second pass over it gives `03_qwen_complement_verified_cleaned_pipeline_input.txt` unless it would empty a non-empty first pass. `cleanup_audit.json` records every answer and the token usage.

The segmentation (`segment.py`) uses no model. It cuts the cleaned text into sections at numbered, all-capital, and Markdown heading lines, and each section into paragraphs at blank lines. Short fragments and bullet items join a neighboring paragraph, the paragraphs after one that ends with a colon join it while they continue its list, and a paragraph longer than 4,000 characters is cut at sentence ends. Each segment is then located in the cleaned text, so that its text is the exact slice given by its `source_char_range` in `05_segments_v15_exact.json`. The file names and the segment format are those of the earlier Qwen runs.
